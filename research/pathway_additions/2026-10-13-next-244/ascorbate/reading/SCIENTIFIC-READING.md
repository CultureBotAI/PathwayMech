## Complete selected primary papers

**PMID11741871 / PMC134747 (Yew and Gerlt, 2002)**: complete material article text, Tables 1–2 and all footnotes, Figures 1–4 including image pixels read. Recombinant UlaD/E/F were amplified from MG1655 genomic DNA, expressed from His10 constructs in BL21(DE3), and affinity tags were removed by thrombin before the reported purified-enzyme assays. UlaD decarboxylates 3-keto-L-gulonate6P, UlaE converts L-xylulose5P to L-ribulose5P, UlaF converts L-ribulose5P to D-xylulose5P. SgbE/SgbH display corresponding activities in vitro but SgbU failed the 3-epimerase assay. All coupled assays contained MgCl2; its presence is not proof that every catalyst requires Mg. The yjf-sga six-gene deletion abolished ascorbate fermentation while the yia-sgb deletion did not. MG1655/BW25113 anaerobic growth occurred at low ascorbate concentrations; high concentrations inhibited growth. The proposed hydrolysis-before-PTS sequence in Figure 3C was explicitly hypothetical: hydrolase and tautomerase were not assigned. Figure 4 preparative YiaK/LyxK chemistry is not part of the physiological Ula route. Native27.0 and later transporter/UlaG evidence resolve the ordering without retroactively attributing the modern full route to this paper.

**PMID12644495 / PMC151508 (Zhang et al., 2003)**: complete material article text, Table 1 strain/plasmid table, Figures 1–8 and pixels read. BW25113 individual in-frame UlaA/B/C deletions lose transport, phosphorylation and anaerobic growth; complementation controls support all three components. The general PTS test compares JM101 with PB11 ΔptsHIcrr, and exogenous cAMP did not rescue. Thus the deletion supports a general EI/HPr requirement; it does not experimentally separate each protein with a clean single deletion. Crude extracts contain the required soluble components as well as enriched membranes; PEP-dependent phosphate ester production was phosphatase-sensitive. UlaG deletion loses growth but accumulates transport label, consistent with intracellular catabolism; UlaG catalytic specificity remained proposed here. Apparent wild-type transport Km18µM includes uptake/metabolism/excretion; the more direct Km9µM is specific to ΔulaG and must not be generalized. ΔulaR derepresses uptake, shortens lag and enables microaerophilic growth. The detailed serial PTS phosphotransfer scheme is a model rather than direct measurement of each transfer. The cited UCSD supplementary index failed certificate validation; TablesS1–S3/FiguresS1–S9 were not acquired or claimed as read. Main-text figures and tables are sufficient for the retained claims.

**PMID14996803 / PMC355983 (Campos et al., 2004)**: complete material article through Discussion/acknowledgements, Tables 1–2 and Figure 7 condition matrix, Figures 1–7 and all pixels read. JA134 is an ECL1 Lyx+ derivative, not wild-type MG1655; JA211 is JA134 yiaR::cat ΔulaR and JA216 adds crp::cat. Growth/reporters used 20mM ascorbate with 20mM nitrate anaerobically and CAA supplements. Purified NusA/His6 UlaR had its tag removed with thrombin before DNA-binding studies. UlaR represses the divergent ulaG and ulaABCDEF promoters; cooperative four-operator binding and cross-promoter dependence support a DNA-loop model, but looping is not directly visualized and 5/10bp spacing insertions did not derepress. cAMP-CRP binds the ulaA promoter and activates ulaABCDEF, whereas ulaG does not depend on CRP in these reporter tests. IHF contributes to low basal expression, especially at ulaA; it is not a direct protein inhibitor of Ula enzymes. Free L-ascorbate does not displace UlaR from DNA. Glucose represses both promoters through more than cAMP depletion; the authors propose an additional inducer-exclusion mechanism. Reporter expression is not whole-pathway flux. FNR-containing deletion background did not alter reporter induction, so the 2003 growth phenotype is not a direct FNR-control edge. Ascorbate6P was a hypothesized effector in 2004, subsequently tested in 2008.

## Restricted full-text sources: explicit limits

**PMID20359483 / DOI10.1016/j.jmb.2010.03.041 (Garces et al., 2010)**: whole EuropePMC primary abstract/metadata retrieved and read. The abstract reports Mn2+/Co2+-loaded UlaG lactonase activity on ascorbate6P, apo/Mn structures at2.6Å, and a hexamer organized as a trimer of dimers. It reports Zn replacement of bound Mn but that does not establish catalytic Zn support; no Zn cofactor edge is made. OpenAlex reports closed access, Elsevier public XML supplies metadata only, and CSIC handle10261/53290 links a restricted-access placeholder. Full methods, figures, kinetics and experimental construct were not obtained and are not claimed read. Catalysis/assembly are independently supported by the full native27.0 protein and reaction originals. No experimental-construct details are invented.

**PMID18844374 / DOI10.1021/bi800748x (2008 UlaR)**: whole EuropePMC primary abstract/metadata retrieved and read. It explicitly identifies ascorbate6P as direct effector and proposes DNA-bound tetramer versus effector-induced DNA-free dimer, using mutagenesis/gel retardation/ITC/ultracentrifugation. Its full body was not obtained; CSIC/Unpaywall points to a restricted placeholder. This cannot supply undisclosed assay conditions or construct identity. A retained effector edge, if integrated, must state abstract-only access and restrict itself to the reported result.

**PMID21943130 / PMC3219644 (2011 family analysis)**: entire material text (background, results, discussion, methods) inspected, including local rereads repairing truncated initial tool output. Figure captions read; figure pixels and14 supplements not inspected. This source is therefore contextual only and is not used to establish new candidate graph assertions. It corroborates2010 UlaG chemistry and assembly; notes Zn as a potential inhibitor rather than cofactor and no observed Fe3 binding for E.coli. Cross-species structures are mostly predictions; no broad taxon pathway claims follow. Its isolated wording 'L-gulonate6P' in the opening overview conflicts with the chemically specific3-dehydro product and is not adopted.
