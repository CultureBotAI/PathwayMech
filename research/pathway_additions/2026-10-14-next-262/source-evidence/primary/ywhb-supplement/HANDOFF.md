# YwhB primary reading supplement

Author supplement for PMID:17902707 (DOI:10.1021/bi701231a). Complete official PMC author-manuscript HTML was independently read, including all experimental procedures, results, discussion, four tables and references. Twenty original NCBI graphics were retrieved and visually inspected: Figures 1–2, Schemes 1–6, two mutant-table reaction drawings and ten inline table reaction drawings. The parent package and frozen Pra/predecessor packages were not modified.

`material-claim-ledger.json` provides 22 scoped claims and limitations. `kinetics-and-stereochemistry.json` contains ten kinetic rows with exact table assignments, units, errors and four new isotope outcomes. `reading-coverage.json` records source hashes, reading coverage, original graphic URLs and access limitations. Source artifacts and complete derived text are external reading materials, not compact maintained projections.

YwhB catalyzes both adjacent 1,3 ketonization of the six-carbon diacid enol (paper 2→1) and pathway-like 1,5 ketonization (2→3). Their kcat/Km values are 1.3×10^5 and 2.8×10^4 M−1 s−1, respectively, while their kcat values are 10 and 26 s−1: an efficiency preference must not be described as higher turnover. Some substrates did not reach saturation, so the authors identify some fitted kcat values as estimates without marking individual rows.

The direct stereochemical outcomes concern deuterated C-3 products: HPD ketonization favors 3R by ~4.4:1; phenylenolpyruvate favors 3R by ~3:1; the six-carbon diacid favors 3S by ~8:1. These are partial selectivities of isotope-labeled products, not R/S assignments of their ordinary C-3-methylene metabolites or independent alkene E/Z determinations. The 4-OT diacid comparator gives a racemate; the authors retain alternative interpretations and do not conclusively exclude 4-OT acceleration of the 1,3 reaction.

Two details need preservation during curation:

- Table 2 gives P1A HPD Km 378±100 µM versus WT 243±42 µM, an increase. Section S15 incorrectly describes a 1.6-fold decrease. Retain the table numbers and flag the internal conflict.
- YwhB diacid reactions were trapped after 1 min; 4-OT was added with NaBH4 and incubated overnight. Do not label those preparations and trapping protocols identical.

P1A/R11A were expressed in BL21(DE3)pLysS and reported as >95% purified with sequence and mass checks. The full supporting methods remain unread: the official PMC supplement URL returned download challenge HTML, and Europe PMC returned an XML notice that the article is outside its OA set. Neither payload is accepted as PDF evidence. Prior protocols and prior hexamer-assembly evidence are citations rather than independently read originals here. Exact mutant construct termini/tag state and current accession identity remain unresolved.

The paper leaves YwhB’s biological role unknown. These biochemical results support component-level alternate ketonization, not a complete native B. subtilis catechol pathway. No new direct quotations were added.
