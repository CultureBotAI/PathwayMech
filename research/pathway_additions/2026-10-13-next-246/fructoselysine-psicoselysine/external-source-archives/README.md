# External primary retention

`retention.json` identifies two deterministic archives outside Git and records every member size/hash after reading the archives back. They retain bounded primary acquisition and reading folders, including rejected response bodies, tool transcripts, local PDF renders and projections. Retention is not a claim that every archived body was appraised; use the reading ledgers.

The descriptor is explicit about the missing 2002 original PDF. A web-reader transcript digest cannot fill that gap. The 2020/2021 reference digest covers the complete LMU thesis, with the reproduced article located on PDF pages 85–100. The 2004 paper has its own complete institutional PDF binding.

Temporary paths are not public or durable hosting promises. Public acquisition URL/time/status/hash bindings are in `../primary-provenance/acquisition-manifest.json`. Raw HTTP bodies, headers, cookies, full article text and images are not maintained in this dossier. The preflight archive is a bounded supporting collection, not a copy of the authority cache.
