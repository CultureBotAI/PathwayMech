The complete 16-page early-view version of PMID32979851 was read as reproduced on one-based PDF pages 85–100 of Franziska Koller's 2021 LMU thesis. Main figures 1–9 were visually inspected in local renders. The retained original digest binds the whole 125-page thesis; it does not identify a standalone journal PDF. Other thesis chapters were not fully appraised.

Supporting figures S1–S5 and tables S1–S4 were not obtained. Publisher DOCX requests failed, and the thesis reproduction ends at the article's supporting-information notice. Accordingly, exact supplementary insert/primer sequences remain unverified.

`claims.json` distinguishes BW25113/JW reporter experiments, purified tag-cleaved FrlR/operator binding and indirect CRP attribution. The shared promoter was assayed; each endogenous frlABCD transcript was not measured separately. The article does not establish MG1655 origin of all reporter DNA. Frl6P is an inferred effector, not a directly assayed FrlR ligand. FrlA transport, Crr interaction, host adaptation and FrlR autoregulation are not established by these experiments.
