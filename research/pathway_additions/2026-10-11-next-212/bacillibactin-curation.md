It is not an independent scientific approval or a completed repository integration. Original articles and database response bodies remain outside the prospective maintained dossier.

The older B. subtilis ATCC21332 experiments, strain 168 sfp0 defect, native MetaCyc assemblies and MIBiG cluster annotations are context, not exact KH3-4 catalyst or assembly authorities. ## Chemical identity and carrier states

All original native reaction sides, coefficients and directions are retained in the projection. The reversible chorismate reaction is displayed in its biosynthetic direction while retaining reversible direction metadata. The native compound structures and charge states are used directly. ATP, AMP and NADH use independently checked exact ChEBI ionic structures because canonical MetaCyc HTML titles equal their frame IDs and are not accepted as resolved authority labels. The native terminal carrier `DHB-glycyl-arginyl-DhbF` is explicitly an arginyl class with no CML or InChI. It cannot be renamed to threonyl. Its use in RXN-19461/19462 conflicts with the preceding threonyl carrier and primary Figure 1b. Both affected graph edges explicitly document the source correction and separately attach native, MetaNetX and primary evidence. Native transformation identities are retained; exact native-equation equivalence is not claimed.

MNXref production documentation reports release 4.5 and imported MetaCyc 27.1 / ModelSEED 2.6.1. No versioned MNX bulk dump was downloaded. Imported alternateName/sameAs values do not prove equivalence to old MetaCyc 22.5. All carrier nodes are chemical reactive-state classes. The two protein-continuation wildcards are not an exact protein sequence. Coefficients therefore express source-defined reactive-state bookkeeping, not whole DhbF molecule counts or assembly stoichiometry. The independent structure replay cleaves only the terminal MNX thioester to derive a common holo reactive fragment. Its formula difference is one third of bacillibactin. That calculation supports conditional reactive-part balance; it is not an independently issued free carrier formula or whole-polypeptide balance.

## ATP, maturation and omitted claims

Three different adenylations occur once per DHB-Gly-Thr repeat. The nine explicit NRPS reactions, multiplied appropriately for three repeats, sum to three DHB, three glycine, three L-threonine and nine ATP producing bacillibactin, nine AMP, nine diphosphate and six protons in the selected ionic forms. All intermediate adenylates and formal carrier states cancel. The net is atom- and charge-balanced. **Nine ATP is mechanistic stoichiometric derivation, not a measured ATP/product ratio.** The source's older RXN-9374 six-ATP composite is retained as a conflict and excluded from the graph; it is neither substituted for nor added to the twelve steps. The scout's current Rhea33812 six-ATP observation remains contextual and is not a separate reaction evidence claim in this draft.

PPTase-dependent DhbB activation is supported by an approximately 340 Da mass shift and no-PPTase control. DhbF maturation is supported by coexpression and productive reconstitution; its large mass prevented direct MALDI occupancy analysis. The two qualified activation edges distinguish these evidence strengths. MbtH was included in the reconstitution but no isolated omission test or stable complex stoichiometry was established, so it remains context rather than a required catalyst/assembly edge. MgCl2, CoA and DTT assay conditions are reported in the primary index; no free Mg2+ cofactor dependency is inferred from a recipe. Physiological localization, regulation, uptake, iron loading, mutasynthetic analogues and downstream hydrolysis remain outside the named complete biosynthetic boundary.
