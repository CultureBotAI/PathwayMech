  functions of the bifunctional phosphatase are retained. Aldolase isoforms and the additional FBPase
  are alternatives, not serial requirements. Native parent-strain assemblies are connected to Kazusa accessions
  only through explicit database component mappings, not assumed culture identity. The erroneous native
  TPI link to mouse Q95554 is rejected; Q59994 has independent inferred activity evidence. Native TKTASYN

  surrogate-donor assay is not evidence for either canonical transketolase substrate pair. Sequence-matched
  synthetic CP12 rescue supports conditional system-level regulation in GT PCC6803, not a purified inhibition
  assay or counted ternary complex. Metal annotations and assay metals remain distinct; AMP-dependent
  inactive phosphatase assemblies are not treated as invariant active complexes. Carbon uptake, light
  reactions, photorespiration and biomass synthesis lie outside the native 13-member boundary.
