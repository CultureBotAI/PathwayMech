# Aerobactin biosynthesis: external author handoff

This is a complete four-transformation research draft for **GO:0019270, aerobactin biosynthetic process**, implemented by the native gene products of **Klebsiella pneumoniae hvKP1 (NCBITaxon:1267897)**. It is not integrated into the current repository and has not received independent scientific approval. The exact native definition is MetaCyc AEROBACTINSYN-PWY, not a shortened assembly module. The current task followed the prior next-201 discovery lead, whose primary-source and ATP-product gaps are now explicitly adjudicated.

## Organism, proteins and assemblies

The choice of hvKP1 follows the strongest inspected full-pathway evidence, rather than substituting E. coli because its reviewed protein accessions are convenient. PMID:29618511 Experimental procedures/sec12 identifies hvKP1 as the source of all four genes; Results/sec4 and Figure 3 establish their combined pathway roles. E. coli is the heterologous expression host, not the target organism. The original NCBI contig AOIZ01000119.1 identifies the four source loci; their translated CDS sequences independently match EMB09144.1–EMB09147.1 exactly. All four original GenPept source features specify strain hvKP1 and taxon 1267897. The NCBI taxonomy response independently agrees.

PMID:27253399 Experimental Methods/sec2.1 explicitly names the selected 574-residue IucA accession EMB09144.1. Its historical issuing-authority label remains “hypothetical protein G057_19877 [Klebsiella pneumoniae hvKP1]”; the primary source establishes IucA function without inventing a label alias. The B/C/D source products are explicitly named in the original genome and protein records. The record uses those native gene-product identities for functional `enables` relations. The full sequences of all tagged, fusion or surface-mutant experimental constructs were not independently recovered, and exact equality between native and engineered sequences is not claimed. The main articles are available; the attempted 2018 supplement payloads were errors or HTML rather than PDF, and are recorded as unavailable.

Primary assembly findings are retained without transferring E. coli complexes or inventing a new complex identifier:

- **IucA:** native gel, size exclusion and SAXS in PMID:27253399 sec3.3/Figures 4–6 support a tetramer, despite a computational monomer prediction. The `enables` relation denotes gene-product function, not monomer sufficiency.
- **IucC:** PMID:29618511 sec9/Figure 7 reports tetramer and dimer populations in solution, with ATP-associated conditions favoring dimer. The authors favor the dimer as physiological and discuss the tetramer as a possible preparation artifact. The crystallographic surface mutant and the wild-type solution measurements are not conflated.
- **IucD/IucB:** the complete route was reconstituted, but IucD-only free hydroxylysine was not detected and the IucB preparation was a cleavable fusion. Their findings do not establish a fixed native hvKP1 assembly stoichiometry. E. coli CPLX-6915 and CPLX-6916 are comparison evidence only and are not imported as hvKP1 complexes.

FAD on IucD is supported by the original 2018 Figure 1 and its reconstitution context. IucA Mg2+ is supported by actual ATP-bound structural coordination in PMID:27253399 Results/sec3.2/p1 and Figure 2A, not merely an assay MgATP condition. No IucC Mg-cofactor edge is added from modeled metal placement. No exclusive cellular localization, all-enzyme complex, native regulation or inhibition edge is invented.
