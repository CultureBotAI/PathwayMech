## Citrate lyase activation

The native pathway explicitly contains reactions 6060–6064 and three catalysts: CitG/P77231, CitX/P0A6G5 and CitC/P77390. These are separate enzymes, so this maturation pathway meets the multi-enzyme boundary. It differs from citrate cleavage or the glyoxylate cycle. [PMID10924139](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10924139/) supports CitG/CitX dependence of holo-ACP formation and identifies AMP-ACP as a side product rather than a required intermediate. [PMID11042274](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11042274/) verifies the precursor and its transfer. Both papers were inspected as complete indexed abstracts, not full articles.

Current reviewed UniProt connects CitG to RHEA15117 and CitX to RHEA16333 with experimental citations. CitC connects to RHEA23788, but its displayed catalytic annotation has no inline experimental evidence code. Preserve that distinction. The whole apo, holo and acetylated carrier objects are RHEA-COMP10157, 10158 and 13710 respectively, as independently named in the UniProt reaction cross-references; their exact Rhea object properties and reaction sides still need inspection. Do not replace whole protein states with free reactive fragments.

[MetaCyc P2-PWY](https://pathway.yeastgenome.org/getxml?META:P2-PWY), retrieved version 22.5, includes branches reported in other organisms. It assigns the alternative acetyl donor reaction to Klebsiella aerogenes and deacetylation to Rubrivivax gelatinosus. Native reaction6062 reverses the expected deacetylation chemistry without ATP and has no catalyst; reaction6064 also has no E. coli catalyst. Neither is an established E. coli step simply because it appears in the native map. The candidate's next task is the organism-supported CitG→CitX→CitC sequence, not every cross-species activation/deactivation branch. Inspect exact reaction objects, the primary CitC evidence and original complex structures before deciding final inclusions.

## Anaerobic allantoin degradation through oxamate

The current yeast records `gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY-5694` and `gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY-5697` use the allantoicase route that releases urea and ends at glyoxylate or ureidoglycolate. The E. coli candidate instead uses AllB/P77671, AllC/P77425 and AllE/P75713 to form ureidoglycolate while releasing ammonium, then AllD/P77555, AllFGH and AllK/P37306 produce oxamate and recover ATP. Shared allantoin or carbamate chemistry does not make these complete routes identical.

[PMID38888336](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38888336/) full text identifies AllF/Q47208, AllG/P77129 and AllH/P0AAS5 as OXTCase components. Deletion/complementation supports oxamate formation; purified proteins were assayed in the reverse direction because oxalurate was unavailable. Mg supplementation supports activity. A 1:1:1 complex mass was assumed for kinetic calculations, while actual quaternary stoichiometry remained unresolved. Do not label the assembly an experimentally established heterotrimer or assign whole-complex catalysis separately to each subunit. The native map omits the catalyst entirely; resolve an external complex identity before curation. An invented local complex ID or arbitrary reuse of a subunit is inappropriate.

Current UniProt has RHEA22984 and physiological reverse reaction22986 for OXTCase. The older MetaCyc22.5 [PWY0-41 definition](https://pathway.yeastgenome.org/getxml?META:PWY0-41) still marks OXAMATE-CARBAMOYLTRANSFERASE-RXN hypothetical. Preserve the dated source disagreement: the 2024 experiment resolves the older gap. Current AllK has net RHEA10152 plus component reactions30755 and57716; avoid double counting the net and explicit carbamate-decay alternatives. The [AllK primary abstract, PMID36384810](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36384810/), independently supports ATP formation with purified YbcF/AllK.

Native reactions5987 and5989 assert NAD and NADP alternatives for AllD. [PMID23284870](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23284870/) full text instead finds NAD preference and explains the NADP-binding conflict; inspect its Figure S2 data before retaining an NADP branch. NADPH in the upstream coupled assay is not evidence that AllD uses NADP. UniProt currently assigns AllD RHEA15329 with NAD. Native AllB/AllC/AllE complexes and metal alternatives still require independent review.

[PMID41016821](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41016821/) full text provides later anaerobic regulation and MC4100 physiology. Its DNA-binding assays use regulator-containing crude lysates after purified-protein attempts failed. Do not turn predicted promoter motifs or whole-regulon implications into direct causal edges. Retain strain distinctions: native map E. coli562, reviewed proteins K-12/83333, and experimental strains including MC4100 and recombinant expression hosts.

## Succinate-to-propanoate cycle

Native PW002058 has three reactions:6006 mutase,6008 decarboxylase and6009 CoA transferase, with Sbm/ScpA/P27253, YgfG/ScpB/P52045 and YgfH/ScpC/P52043. Current UniProt assigns RHEA22888,27666 and28010, with experimental citations; ScpA requires adenosylcobalamin. The native scope is narrower than the overlapping PW002057/SMP0002069 “Propanoyl-CoA Degradation” map, which adds uncatalyzed carboxylation/epimerization branches. Prefer the exact three-enzyme cycle over importing that collage unchanged.

[PMID10769117](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10769117/) establishes the three activities but explicitly leaves their metabolic context unresolved. [PMID18950999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18950999/) establishes Sbm/YgfD expression, GTPase activity and interaction, which does not itself establish physiological succinate-to-propionate flux. These were inspected as complete indexed abstracts. The attempted Europe PMC full-text retrieval of the latter failed and is recorded as a failure.

[MetaCyc PWY0-43](https://pathway.yeastgenome.org/getxml?META:PWY0-43), version22.5, independently preserves the proposed-route limitation. A future record may represent experimentally supported enzyme chemistry with that explicit limit if the skill boundary is satisfied; otherwise inspect later native-strain physiology before promotion. Engineered production studies are not automatic evidence of unmodified-strain flux. Preserve R stereochemistry, distinguish mutase/decarboxylase assemblies, and do not import YgfD as a fourth catalyst merely because its gene shares the operon.
