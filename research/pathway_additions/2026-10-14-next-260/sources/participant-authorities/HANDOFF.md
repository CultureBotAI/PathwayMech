## Primary reagent scope

The final preparation claim ledger is bound at SHA-256 **fd6da28356747837c9dbe2b0098385bf4f4b0a91c2e19f1c14377dc4193de971**. Its preparation, assay and source-form limits remain controlling; chemical identity alone proves no biological effect.

- **Cadmium is now supported by the primary ProA paper.** CdCl2 is CHEBI:35456 and cadmium(2+) CHEBI:48775. The five supporting salts and eight non-supporting/inhibitory salts remain specific to the purified native preparation, dose panel and assay. Quantitative inhibition is not invented for the latter group.
- **EDTA and reduced glutathione need explicit named-parent qualifiers.** CHEBI:4735 and CHEBI:16856 verify the parent structures, not delivered salts, aqueous protonation, metal complexes or a universally active species. K82 EDTA stabilization/no activity effect, 1983 chelation/rescue, GSH protection during DTNB co-incubation, LigJ enhancement, ProA no effect and stored ProH rescue are different observations.
- **Generic cysteine and DTT retain unspecified stereo where the paper does.** CHEBI:15356 is a generic cysteine candidate; explicit L-cysteine or L-cysteine hydrochloride can instead use their verified specific entries. Native L-CYS is not overwritten. DTT's unassigned issuing parent does not select one of the verified D/L comparators or assert every stereoisomer was assayed.
- **Phosphate was a KH2PO4/K2HPO4 mixture.** Broad phosphate-ion class CHEBI:35780 can identify the effector while CHEBI:63036 and CHEBI:131527 retain delivery composition. It was not an isolated PO4(3−) experiment. Arsenate class CHEBI:22629 similarly avoids inventing sodium stoichiometry, hydration or protonation from “Na arsenate.”
- **The paper's phosphorous-acid name must not select a tautomer by label alone.** Current CHEBI:36361 depicts P(OH)3; CHEBI:44976 depicts the P–H parent HP(O)(OH)2. On pp338–339 the authors explicitly propose X–PO3(2−), X=H. This supports intended P–H connectivity as a bounded interpretation, not a measured microspecies. The proposal is separately recorded as prep-119.
- **NaHCO3 was directly added; carbonate was proposed.** CHEBI:32139 and component CHEBI:17544 preserve delivery. A likely carbonate activator in discussion must not replace the measured reagent. Lithium/potassium acetyl phosphate, PubChem:6098323, independently reconstructs from acetyl phosphate(2−), CHEBI:22191, plus Li+ and K+.
- **ATP has two condition-specific signs.** Mg-competitive inhibition in the companion ProA experiment and activation in the phosphate-depleted assay both remain. ATP, ADP, pyrophosphate and thiamine diphosphate have verified parent/protonation candidates, with explicit unresolved salt/speciation qualifications. Their candidate structures are not separate tested conditions.
- **Preserve the printed CuSO2 row.** The 1983 Table II apparent formula error is unresolved; the exact native CuSO4 closure cannot silently repair it. The table used combined, partially purified enzyme-I/enzyme-II fractions and paired coupled readouts, not pure hydratase.
- **Avoid synonym-only salt substitutions.** CHEBI:755954's “sodium borate” structure is a particular hydrated tetraborate, unsupported by the paper's unspecific name. CHEBI:41796 carries a sodium-diethyldithiocarbamate synonym but is the neutral acid; the exact sodium salt is CHEBI:82587. Those differences remain visible.
- **Negative controls stay negative/contextual.** Lack of stimulation or cleavage is not inhibition. The pyrone positional isomers, chelators, seven ProA reagent non-effects, phosphate-panel nonactivators, ethanol/acetone controls, buffer comparisons and nonenzymatic controls have explicit scope. The inhibitory impurity in practical-grade coumalic acid is not assigned to purified coumalic acid.
- **Optical l/d is not absolute R/S or biochemical L/D.** Generic HMG/HG identities and glyoxylate support alternate in vitro cleavage bookkeeping. Native R-HMG and issuing L-HG remain separate comparators. Three alternate reaction balances and 20 formula-level salt checks pass, but none adds a mandatory gallate-II step.
