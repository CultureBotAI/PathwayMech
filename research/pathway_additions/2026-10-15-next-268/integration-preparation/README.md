The [inventory](inventory.json) binds the complete author primary-fragment packet,
the original authority implementation packet, and 41 explicitly cited supporting
artifact paths. The latter include authentic source originals and derived identity
projections; the inventory preserves their exact original paths and distinguishes
this finite selection from exhaustive retention of all research.

Every archive member was fully decoded, hashed and compared with its source.
Standard tar readers can inspect the archives in a fresh scratch directory;
archived scripts need not be executed. File bytes and relative paths are exact,
while filesystem metadata is not part of the evidence identity.

The authority packet is the immutable predecessor of the correction for issue
615. The primary packet is an author draft, not its own independent acceptance.
Final scientific and implementation reviews, corrections and repository checks
are separate successor records. Earlier primary corrections and core reviews are
retained under `../accepted-preparation/`.
