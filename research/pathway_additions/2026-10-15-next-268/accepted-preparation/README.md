# Accepted preparation records

These eight archives retain selected immutable source-coverage and review packets for
phenylacetate degradation I (aerobic). The [inventory](inventory.json) identifies
each author or independent-review role and every original relative path, size and
SHA-256. It includes hidden and ignored regular files. Every archive was fully
decoded and checked against the original files after creation.

Use a standard tar reader to inspect an archive or extract it into a new scratch
directory. Archived helper scripts are historical evidence; extraction does not
require executing them. Filesystem timestamps, permissions and inode identities
are not retained as evidence claims. The original packet manifests and freeze
records remain unchanged inside the archives.

The original design review retains its three findings. Its accepted corrections
are represented by the addendum and successor core implementation review. The
primary correction author packet and its independent acceptance are separate.
Acceptance applies only to each review's stated scope. The native draft is an
intermediate, and the core review excludes its author's renderer.

This selection does not claim retention or fresh review of every discovery
archive, primary source or external ontology database. Final pathway evidence,
scientific review and repository validation remain separate integration steps.
