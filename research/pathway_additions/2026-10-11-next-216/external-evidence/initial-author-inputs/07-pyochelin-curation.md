# Pyochelin biosynthesis: complete external author draft

This draft represents all 11 native reaction objects of MetaCyc PWY-6408, under the active exact process GO:0042864. Its boundary is assembly from **supplied salicylate**, not the larger chorismate-to-salicylate supply pathway. PchA/PchB, PchC editing, transport, iron complexation and regulation are retained as context rather than added as unsupported objects. The record has not received independent scientific approval or maintained-corpus integration.

## Native coverage and carrier accounting

The complete native list is RXN-11113, RXN-11115, RXN-11117, RXN-11118, RXN-11120, RXN-11121, RXN-11122, RXN-11123, RXN-11125, RXN-19466 and RXN-19469. `pyochelin-source-projection.json` accounts for each object and every graph edge in both directions. RXN-11114 is an alternative overall equation, not a missing twelfth member; adding it would doublecount assembly.

RXN-11115 is a shared cysteine-adenylation object used once by each module. The formal sum therefore turns it twice and each other object once. It consumes one salicylate, two cysteines, three ATP, one NADPH and one SAM, and produces one native pyochelin I monoanion, three AMP, three diphosphate, NADP, SAH, water and three protons. The ATP count is a stoichiometric derivation, not a measured ATP/product ratio.

Native PchE and PchF carrier frames are chemical reactive-state classes. PchE has distinct ArCP and PCP reaction sites on the same polypeptide. Their source coefficients must not be interpreted as counts of independent proteins or used to derive an assembly stoichiometry. The original 2022 supplement Figure 1c, the full PchE architecture and the PchF primary scheme support the domain chemistry. Explicit derived reactive-group SMILES with PchE/PchF domain anchors check all 11 equations independently; these SMILES are an analytical atom/charge model, not newly issued structures, exact stereochemical assignments or full-polypeptide formulas. Both domain anchors cancel in the formal net.

Small-molecule mappings require original native InChI equality with ChEBI 255 plus charged-atom checks. Native cysteine, SAM and SAH use zwitterionic forms. InChI by itself can normalize their protomers, so original CML nitrogen and carboxyl oxygen charges are retained in the bridge ledger. All 13 selected small-molecule bridges preserve native formulas and total charges.

## Endpoint and stereochemical decision

Native CPD-9992 issues a monoanion with `/p-1/t9-,10+,13-/m1/s1`. Comparison with the original neutral CHEBI:67183 structure assigns it to pyochelin I after explicit protonation normalization; the native structure alone does not establish physiology. It is not an exact match to neutral CHEBI:67183, neutral pyochelin II CHEBI:67185, or the neutral I/II class CHEBI:29669. The 2007 original indexed abstract explicitly identifies P. aeruginosa I and II as interconvertible diastereoisomers; the enantiomers in P. fluorescens CHA0 must not be transferred to PAO1.

The draft uses the authentic native I monoanion as a **source-defined projection of one experimentally observed product**, rather than claiming I-only physiology or I-selective enzymatic release. That limit is prominent in the description and the release-product evidence. The bounded independent endpoint assessment supports this choice with the stated limits; independent whole-record review is still required. Before freezing, its source-locality recommendation was applied: native structure, the separate ChEBI I comparison, and primary I/II physiology have distinct evidence blocks. The assessment and exact pre-freeze delta are bound in `pyochelin-endpoint-assessment-context.json`. An alternative is the neutral I/II class, but that would require explicit acid-base normalization of the terminal equation and must not be advertised as native exact equivalence. Neither an invented isomerization identifier nor omission of terminal release is acceptable.

Carrier names leave some stereocenters unspecified. The 2003 abstract establishes that PchE epimerization follows amide formation, not simple cysteinyl-S-PchE loading, and reports D/L mixtures after release in the experiments. The native first-cyclization object compresses the coupled ring-forming/epimerization sequence. No extra unsupported epimerization reaction or exclusive intermediate stereochemical purity is asserted.

## Protein and primary evidence scope

The current graph uses PAO1 Q9HWG3 (PchD), G3XCV2 (PchE), Q9HWG4 (PchF) and Q9HWG5 (PchG). PchG is an unreviewed current entry; its role is independently supported by PAO1 genetics and primary reconstitution. The current PchD and PchE/F annotations include similarity or curator inference. Functional `enables` relations preserve those gene-product roles without asserting purified monomer sufficiency.

The 2022 complete original article and 27-page supplement directly describe PAO1 PchE. Its deposited 7EMY core is exactly current G3XCV2 plus a 17-residue tag. PchE is observed as a homodimer. No separate verified assembly identifier was found, so this observation remains explicit evidence on the protein's functional role; the native DEFG class CPLX-7976 is not substituted for a PchE dimer. The same structures directly place magnesium with cysteinyl-AMP, supporting a bounded metal-binding relation. Magnesium is not transferred to PchD/F merely because an assay used MgATP. Current UniProt annotations identify the covalent phosphopantetheine-arm cofactor; it is not a free stoichiometric substrate.

The older AF184620/AF184621/AF184622 sequences are PA14. Their original CDS translations exactly match Q9RFM9/Q9RFM8/Q9RFM7 but differ from current PAO1 by 3/14/19 residues. The PAO1 AF074705.2 submission independently supplies PchE and PchG sequences identical to the selected current entries; its PchF differs at six residues. The later PchF paper names O85740, now a secondary accession of Q9HWG4, but accession redirection does not prove that every historical engineered construct had the current sequence. The primary PchF work tests substrate analogues and does not establish complete thiotemplate steady-state kinetics. The 2016 PchG paper likewise warns that a free oxidized analogue is a limited model; its Yersinia Irp3 crystal dimers are not PchG assembly evidence.

Complete original bodies were inspected for PMID:35105906, PMID:30525512 and PMID:27601130. The older 1999/2001/2003/2007 sources are retained as original indexed abstracts unless the access ledger explicitly says otherwise. No unavailable full article is represented by a fabricated digest. Full-title extraction uses only the original article TITLE passage or article-meta title, not abbreviation headings or bibliography titles.
