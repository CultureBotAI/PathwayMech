# Independent maintained-record review preparations

This package preserves the complete independent technical preparation (92
files) and scientific preparation (131 files), including their explicit
actual-retention supplements. The [source manifest](source-file-manifest.json)
records every file, including hidden and ignored entries, original paths,
hashes and sizes. The [archive](files.tar.gz) contains each as an exact regular
member under `technical/` or `scientific/`, plus the retention helper. All
members were read back and compared byte for byte with the source.

The two reviewers found no new actionable defects in their declared scopes.
Their reports preserve source-reading and check attribution, limitations,
historical failures and pending final-gate/canonical-review status. The technical
review covers the actual record, authority implementation, preserved baseline,
retained evidence, generated graph and exports. The scientific review covers
the whole seven-step record and exact edge, identity, source and construct
qualifications. Neither report substitutes preparation for a completed saved
review or establishes PR publication, merge or issue closure.

The later canonical YAML/Markdown observations remain separate immutable
reviews under `reviews/structured/`. Source evidence and the terminal full gate
logs are owned by their separate evidence-retention and verification packages.
