## Primary evidence and scope

**1982, PMID6180735 / DOI10.1042/bj2040191.** All six original NCBI scanned pages191–196 were visually read. The organism is axenic Entamoeba histolytica **200:NIH**, not an inferred HM-1 strain. Partially purified native transketolase, PFK and aldolase preparations supported an in vitro pentose-to-hexose system; minus-enzyme and minus-PPi controls were inactive. Labelled glucose experiments retained C1 and C6 in RNA ribose, supporting a nonoxidative route. Reverse operation in vivo is a reasoned interpretation, not a separately measured complete flux map.

Transketolase required thiamine pyrophosphate and Mg2+ in the apoenzyme add-back experiment (p193, Fig1), with order-of-addition lag. Both canonical substrate pairs were assessed; Table2 reports apparent, cosubstrate-dependent Km values. PFK S7P Km64µM and PPi Km approximately10µM are reaction parameters, not binding Kd. Native aldolase preparation had slight PFK contamination; SBP Km13µM versus FBP3µM, relative SBP Vmax half FBP, and adding TPI did not change SBP cleavage rate (p194). No exact modern sequence/accession is inferred for any 200:NIH preparation. R5P isomerization and Ru5P epimerization are native pathway-definition events, not separately claimed purified-enzyme characterizations in this six-page study. Coupled assay enzymes remain reagents.

**2020, PMID31871051 / DOI10.1074/jbc.RA119.011239.** The complete retained main XML, including Methods, was read. C. thermosuccinogenes xylose/glucose metabolomics showed increased SBP and S7P pools; this does not directly measure all six enzyme activities or flux. E. coli BW25113 ΔtalAB was used to establish an SBP reference by retention/mass/fragmentation. It is not evidence for a native E. coli PPi branch. The thermocellum protein was amplified from DSM1313; thermosuccinogenes locus tags are joined to DSM5809 through the issuing genome proteins, but the unspecified main-text metabolomics culture is not silently relabelled to that strain.

Recombinant CDQ83_11320 and Clo1313_1876, expressed with engineered affinity tags in E. coli, catalyzed S7P phosphorylation with PPi. Minus-PPi and minus-enzyme controls support the assignment. These biochemical edges require evidence-backed experimental_construct scope, including the His-tag/heterologous preparation. The accompanying rabbit aldolase creates DHAP/E4P; it does **not** characterize a specific native clostridial aldolase. The paper's proposed trace TPI contamination explanation for low DHAP remains a hypothesis. PPi is a consumed phosphoryl donor, not merely an activating cofactor. MgCl2 in the assay is not on its own an independent universal metal-requirement experiment.

Table1 distinguishes PPi enzymes (S7P Km≈0.1mM), E. coli ATP-PfkA (much larger S7P Km), and thermosuccinogenes ATP/GTP-PFK CDQ83_07225 (no S7P activity through4mM). F6P cooperativity and ammonium dependence concern the ATP/GTP enzyme, not PPi-PFK. The proposed glycolytic relief valve, evolutionary distribution and PPi energy-economy explanations remain hypotheses. A third annotated PFK had no demonstrated activity; homology is not an activity assignment.
