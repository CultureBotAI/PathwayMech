# Complete E. coli thiamine diphosphate biosynthesis I

The target is **MetaCyc:THISYN-PWY**, “superpathway of thiamine diphosphate biosynthesis I,” represented for the E. coli K-12 protein core. The full exact native route comprises PWY-6892 (seven thiazole-branch reactions), PWY-6890 (two AIR-to-HMP-PP reactions), and PWY-6894 (coupling and terminal phosphorylation). It is a connected, multibranch cofactor pathway, not a general thiamine-metabolism umbrella. The native route begins at AIR, cysteine, tyrosine, pyruvate and D-glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate, with the declared carrier states and cosubstrates. Precursor biosynthesis is not recursively added.

The record has ten current directed Rhea reactions because one net sulfur-transfer reaction replaces two native relay reactions. All 59 exact Rhea side occurrences, coefficients and five whole protein carrier states are retained. All ten reactions balance in the Rhea representation; carrier backbones cancel and reactive-part formulas are used only for checking, never as replacement graph nodes. Generic AH2/A does not specify the physiological electron donor. This is a complete reaction-level representation of the chosen pathway, with explicit limits on elementary intermediates and organism-specific mechanistic proof.

## Experimental scope and conflicts

* **Sulfur transfer:** PMID:10781607, DOI:10.1074/jbc.M002680200, experimentally reconstituted E. coli IscS-mediated ThiS thiocarboxylation and reported sevenfold stimulation by ThiI. Its indexed abstract was inspected; attempted publisher full-text endpoints did not yield article text. The guessed JBC path was unverified and is not cited as an authoritative article location. PMID:9632726, DOI:10.1074/jbc.273.26.16555, identified ThiS thiocarboxylate and ThiF adenylation; the inspected abstract distinguishes thiI+ and thiI− expression strains. Current UniProt P77718 assigns net RHEA:43340 and attaches PMID:10753862. That paper's inspected abstract concerns tRNA 4-thiouridine, so its citation is not promoted to direct ThiS reaction evidence.
* **ThiI is not an unconditional sole catalyst:** full primary PMID:11438688 / PMC37467 describes IscS/ThiF in vitro thiocarboxylation without essential ThiI. It identifies an acyldisulfide and a C184S complementation defect; it does not establish that all proposed intervening states or a specific ferredoxin are obligatory. Full PMID:21724998 / PMC3165671 is a Salmonella study: its rhodanese-domain sufficiency and bypass phenotypes show that tRNA and thiazole sulfur mobilization must not be equated. These Salmonella experiments are scope constraints, not E. coli catalyst edges.
* **ThiF/ThiS assembly:** full PMID:16388576 / PMC2566941, DOI:10.1021/bi051502y, shows a crystal heterotetramer, two ThiF plus two ThiS, and structural zinc. ThiF is annotated as a homodimer. No fabricated whole-assembly CURIE is introduced; the functional ThiF edge states the assembly requirement and its nearest evidence. ThiS has its own gene-product role plus distinct whole-carrier states. The old abstract's acyldisulfide mechanism is not copied into additional net edges.
* **ThiGH assembly and reaction:** PMID:12650933, DOI:10.1016/S0014-5793(03)00204-7, isolated a large complex anaerobically; PMID:17403671, DOI:10.1074/jbc.M700782200, reconstituted ThiGH/ThiFS-dependent thiazole formation with the ThiH Fe-S cluster. Both indexed abstracts were inspected. Full PMID:19923213 / PMC2820752, DOI:10.1074/jbc.M109.056606, explicitly reports a complex with at least six heterodimers (~440 kDa) and monomeric ThiH fractions. Thus native CPLX0-3949's six-plus-six composition is a qualified experimental assembly, not refuted merely by the term heterodimer. Its two subunit links are retained. The study used NADPH, flavodoxin 1 and flavoprotein:NADPH oxidoreductase; Rhea's net NADPH side is not a claim ThiH directly oxidizes NADPH without electron-transfer partners. The assumed natural reductant system was not established as uniquely physiological.
* **Thiazole tautomer:** full PMID:19216519 / PMC2765510 and PMID:21534620 / PMC3116082 establish the unexpected product and TenI chemistry using B. subtilis proteins. Current MetaCyc, Rhea and E. coli UniProt assign the (R,Z) tautomer to E. coli ThiG/ThiE. These are preserved as curated assignments, not relabeled as direct E. coli stereochemical measurements. UniProt ThiE's Ref.5 is the 1995 Backstrom et al. JACS study, not the later tautomer paper. No TenI, ThiO or aerobic glycine-oxidase branch is added to THISYN-PWY.
* **ThiC:** PMID:15326535, DOI:10.1039/B405429F, supports E. coli cell-free activity with an unidentified additional protein and assay stimulation by SAM/nicotinamide cofactors. Full PMC2587053 (PMID:18953358, DOI:10.1038/nchembio.121) used Caulobacter ThiC for structural/biochemical work. Full PMC3147014 (PMID:20886485, DOI:10.1002/anie.201003419) establishes formate/CO formation and radical chemistry, with precise species/method qualifications retained. E. coli's exact net reaction, homodimer and 4Fe-4S cluster are current HAMAP ECO:0000255 assignments. No guessed additional catalyst or NADPH cofactor edge is added to ThiC.
* **ThiD:** current P76422 gives monomeric ThiD and direct catalytic evidence from PMID:10075431; its abstract was inspected. Native HMP-P-KIN-CPLX has coefficient four from the 1998 Reddick et al. Tetrahedron study. The graph uses functional `enables`, discloses both annotations and does not invent a universal assembly or ATP-associated Mg requirement. Its HMP salvage activity is outside this route.
* **ThiL:** current P0AGG0 directly annotates RHEA:15913 using PMID:4567662, whose retrieved indexed entry has no abstract; the 1972 full text was not inspected. PMID:6284709 / PMC220312 supports K-12 JC1552-derived mutant rescue/activity mapping; the available article HTML contains the abstract and scanned-page access, not machine-readable complete article text. No false full-text experimental claim is made.
* **Dxs and metal scope:** fresh P77488 and native CPLX0-743 preserve its homodimer, TPP and Mg assignments. The substrate and catalytic annotations cite PMIDs:9371765 and 9482846; these papers are database citations here, not newly inspected primary articles. Mg binding cites PMID:17135236. ThiE Mg is ECO:0000250 by similarity. No absence of a cofactor comment establishes metal independence for ThiD or ThiL. Bound structural Zn in ThiF is not described as a catalytic metal.

The core taxon is NCBITaxon:83333. No compartment node is inferred merely because soluble proteins were purified.
