# Complete glyoxylate assimilation: PWY-5744

This author dossier covers the entire native 13-object glyoxylate-assimilation cycle, including acetyl-CoA-to-propionyl-CoA regeneration, both protein-bound biotin half-reactions, and native carbon dioxide/bicarbonate equilibration. It does not substitute the five-reaction terminal module for the complete pathway. Those five dedicated reactions were reconstituted with four recombinant enzymes in the 2009 primary study. The complete selected cycle has net glyoxylate plus one inorganic-carbon unit to pyruvate, with three NADPH and two ATP consumed. The separate first 3-hydroxypropionate cycle generates glyoxylate; its overlapping regeneration chemistry does not make the two complete pathways duplicates.

The maintained target is `data/pathways/glyoxylate-assimilation.yaml`. Its exact frozen hash and counts are recorded by `glyoxylate-validation.json`. Whole-corpus integration, generated pages, history, and full gates are parent-owned and are not claimed by the author replay.

Twenty-three of25 native small-molecule InChIs exactly match the selected current ChEBI forms. Native CPD-9409 is a material exception: its label says (2R,3S)-beta-methylmalyl-CoA while its actual encoded acyl stereocenters are hydroxy-S/methyl-R. Current ChEBI75634 is hydroxy-R/methyl-S. RDKit CIP assignment on the exact original InChIs, with atom correspondence rather than reading raw InChI signs as R/S labels, verifies the difference. The original 2009 Figure1, actually downloaded and visually inspected, explicitly identifies the pathway intermediate as (2R,3S) using free-carboxyl-end numbering. The 2008 hydratase discussion identifies the hydroxyl position as3R under thioester-end numbering. Both support the current hydroxyl-R structure. The encoded native structure is rejected as an exact mapping, with its original bytes and conflicting label retained. CPD-627 is a different, lesser specificity issue: it specifies the correct S-citramalyl center but leaves five CoA stereocenters undefined; the current physiological CoA form is narrower, not an exact native structure match. No exactMatch or sameAs is asserted for either case.

Its CT alpha/beta locus and chain labels are reversed in Methods, so individual A9WBQ8/A9WBQ9 catalytic roles retain their actual current HAMAP inference tiers; no exact whole-ACC stoichiometry is fabricated.
