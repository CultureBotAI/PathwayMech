## DesD identity and energetic conflict

Original MetaCyc RXN-11001 and official KEGG R13603 each encode one ATP, one AMP, one PPi and a water product. The organic transformation is the correct complete B assembly. The record explicitly corrects the co-substrate coefficients while retaining the source-issued reaction identity and the original conflicting equation. It never claims exact equation equivalence.

PMID:37207292, original NSF-hosted author manuscript Figure 3 (PDF p.11), shows B formation from HSC–HSC plus HAC by all four tested DesD orthologs. HSC plus HSC–HAC did not give detectable B under those assay conditions. Figure 4 (p.12) shows two successive ATP-to-AMP/PPi adenylation cycles. These figures were rendered and inspected. The mechanistic net in the selected ionic forms is:

`2 HSC + HAC + 2 ATP4− → desferrioxamine B+ + 2 AMP2− + 2 PPi3− + 2 H+`.

There is no net water in that corrected equation. Both the native one-ATP equation and the corrected two-ATP equation balance atoms and charge. Balance therefore cannot demonstrate energetic coupling. Two ATP is derived from the primary-supported two-cycle mechanism, not a measured ATP/product ratio. PMID:42630954 (2026), original PMC13495013 Introduction §1 paragraph 3 and Discussion §3 paragraph 5, supports the iterative model but retains uncertain binding order and cellular product-pool control. Its structural experiments principally concern *S. griseoflavus*, not an unqualified coelicolor mechanism. The 2016 culture-labeling inference of a different condensation order is retained in the scout assessment; the purified 2023 substrate-feeding result is the basis for the selected model, not proof of universal in vivo flux.
