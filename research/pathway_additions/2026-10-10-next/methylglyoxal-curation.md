## Inspected evidence and decisions

- **Reaction 1:** [PMID16077126 full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1196063/) identifies acetol from purified YghZ with NADPH (Results; Figure 3B). NADH activity is below 3% of NADPH activity and reverse acetol-to-MG activity was not detected under its assay conditions. Native PathBank says reversible; MetaCyc selects right-to-left relative to its oxidation equation. The record selects the experimentally supported reductive `RHEA:27988`, right-to-left relative to master 27986. It does not convert a negative assay into universal thermodynamic irreversibility. Current reviewed Q46851 is Gpr/L-glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate reductase, not an uncharacterized protein.
- **Reaction 2:** MetaCyc RXN-8632 and current reviewed P0A9S5 explicitly assign GldA to acetol + NADH + proton → (S)-1,2-propanediol + NAD. Native `GLYCDEH-CPLX` points to this reaction; its component `GLYCDEH-MONOMER` independently maps to P0A9S5. `RHEA:28668` is the selected left-to-right member of master 28667. The full primary paper PMID18179582 was blocked; its complete indexed abstract and publisher abstract were inspected, but they are not used as direct evidence of an acetol-specific assay. The enzyme assertion is correctly attributed to the inspected reviewed database.
- **Stereochemistry:** 2005 NMR detected 1,2-propanediol without resolving enantiomers. [PMID10049880 full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC91161/) used chiral GC to find R-diol in engineered AG1 strains; its Discussion assigns that to a proposed R-lactaldehyde route, and the purified Table 5 enzyme is from K. pneumoniae. It neither directly proves nor directly refutes isolated E. coli GldA production of S-diol from acetol. The S assignment remains explicitly a current Rhea/UniProt/native annotation, not an enantiomer-resolved result of either inspected study. No R/S interchange is silently introduced.
- **Gpr assembly:** Current Q46851 and native G7558-MONOMER describe tetrameric versus octameric studies. The record's functional `enables` edge does not assert a monomeric physical catalyst or invent a complex identity. Gpr has a more efficient L-GAP activity; no exclusive MG substrate/physiological-role claim is made.
- **GldA assembly:** Native GLYCDEH-CPLX lists eight monomers but explicitly also an active dimer. [PMID30839292 full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6404857/), section 3.1/Figure 1b, reports a tetramer in 5% glycerol and contrasts dimer/octamer mixtures without glycerol. The graph records membership, not an invariant stoichiometry.
- **Zinc and nucleotide conditions:** The 2019 GldA crystal contains zinc at the glycerol-bound active site; its NAD+ was modeled from another structure. UniProt zinc cofactor evidence is ECO:0000305 while the one-zinc-per-subunit note is ECO:0000269. These limits are preserved. The PMID6365902 complete abstract describes both NADH and NADPH supporting hydroxyacetone reduction; retaining the named pathway's NADH branch does not assert NADPH exclusion. No new NADPH second-step reaction or catalytic-site claim is invented from this abstract.
- **Physiology:** MG1655 provides the native annotation and source genes. The 2005 intact-cell acetol/diol time course used engineered MG-accumulating CK281 derivatives; crude extracts and purified proteins support different levels of inference. Double mutants on a glyoxalase-deficient background support YghZ detoxification contribution, not universal exclusive pathway flux. Later PMID34699695 was inspected selectively for pH-dependent glycerol-fermentation context in BW25113; no exact YghZ→GldA flux claim is transferred from that broader study.
