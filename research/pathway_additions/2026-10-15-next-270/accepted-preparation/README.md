# Native methanogenesis preparation evidence

These ten standard tar.gz archives retain the complete frozen source, issuer, graph-design and independent-review packets listed in [inventory.json](inventory.json). Every physical regular file is retained, including hidden/ignored files, original manifests, freeze proofs, failed attempts and copies already inside an owner. Repeated occurrences are preserved rather than deduplicated. Exact file bytes and relative paths define identity; filesystem metadata does not.

The source chain is [discovery](discovery.tar.gz), [independent preflight review](independent-preflight-review.tar.gz), [source preparation](methanogenesis-source-preparation.tar.gz) and [independent source closure review](independent-source-closure-review.tar.gz). The original S15 locator finding remains in that review. The [locator correction](source-locator-correction.tar.gz) and [independent acceptance](source-locator-independent-acceptance.tar.gz) are separate successors. The corrected twenty-claim ledger is `after/claim-dispositions.json` inside the correction archive (SHA256 `08730dcd80abda474f8fa5dc3bdd3ad2d9edb21e79f94a7b278001ef183fac2b`). Issue 626 integration and merge are later work, not asserted by this retention group.

The [issuer preparation](methanogenesis-issuer-preparation.tar.gz) and [independent issuer review](independent-issuer-review.tar.gz) preserve finite molecular, reaction, protein/gene and taxon dispositions. Native generic carriers, stereo/ionic/compartment distinctions, historical strain/sequence and GeneID/paralog conflicts, cofactor scope and activity-direction limits remain explicit. Issuer annotations are not automatically primary assay evidence. Full external ontology-cache hashes retain their original scope; selected issuing statements do not imply that the full database is included here.

The [native graph design](native-graph-design.tar.gz) and [independent graph review](native-graph-independent-review.tar.gz) cover the bounded six-member, 101-node, 125-edge native representation, including thirty material occurrences, forty-three component occurrences, fifteen activity dispositions, seven regulations and twenty corrected claims. The endpoint remains methyl-CoM; METHFORM is boundary context. Exact activity identities, Mtr sodium/carrier locations, generic classes, source-only assertions and both Mch direction statements remain distinct. The biochemical probe is not the complete taxon/primary-evidence maintained record. Later independent acceptance documents supersede earlier pending-review status only within their own scope; frozen author wording is preserved.

Every outer archive member was decoded and checked against a fresh physical-file inventory, all source owners were rehashed unchanged, and deterministic recompression reproduced each archive byte-for-byte. Nested archives and binary containers—including compressed scientific data and spreadsheet containers—are retained as opaque hashed bytes. Their contents were not recursively decoded or newly scientifically reviewed by this packaging task. The inventory identifies such occurrences without claiming exhaustive nested-content interpretation.

Use a standard tar reader to inspect an archive or extract it into a fresh scratch directory. Archived helper scripts are historical artifacts and should not be run inside immutable owners. These archives do not include the prospective authority implementation/review, mutable record candidate or later publication artifacts. Mechanical retention is not new scientific, implementation, maintained-record or publication acceptance. Public-release licensing review remains deferred as instructed.
