## Pathway scope

to the Z-enol hydroxypentadienoate in Paenibacillus sp. JJ-1b. GO:0019619 is broader;
GO:0019617 and GO:0019618 denote different cleavage routes. The native pathway,

## Mechanistic summary

The five selected reactions are MetaCyc:RXN-10882, MetaCyc:RXN-10883, RHEA:34220,
RHEA:33432 and MetaCyc:4.1.1.77-RXN. Complete sides and coefficients balance atoms
and charge. Exact native CML connectivity, full InChIs, pinned chemical identity
and current issuing structures were compared. Generic native SMILES, omitted
stereo in some pinned SMILES and InChI proton normalization are documented; no
single shorthand or database cross-reference establishes molecular equivalence.

The actual first intermediate is native CPD-14966, the Z,Z compound. A narrative
hyperlink instead names the distinct E,Z CPD-11842. The graph follows the complete
reaction/layout objects and source figure geometry, retaining the disagreement.
The native tautomerase Rhea cross-reference 32214 has different stereoisomers;
33432 is the independently exact equation. Native decarboxylation produces the
enol CHEBI:67152; Rhea 24261 instead produces its keto tautomer. The native
reaction is therefore retained. PWY-5162 consumes the same enol through its
source-selected reverse hydratase orientation.

PraA, PraH, PraB, PraC and PraD are supported by individual recombinant crude-extract
assays. All five UniProt sequences exactly match deposited AB505864.1 translations.
They are unreviewed entries; automatic annotations are not substituted for those
experiments. Seven affected protein endpoints preserve construct scope and the
unresolved terminal/tag details. PraH's measured MYDVH start matches the deposited
sequence; no native truncation is asserted. The 1993 native preparation's mass and
assembly are not treated as a sequence measurement of the 2009 recombinant enzyme.

The native complex model, iron cofactor and four in-vitro inhibitor assertions
retain their actual sources and historical scope. EDTA and nitrilotriacetate are
supported by native structured source annotations. The PMID:8392511 abstract
reports hydrogen peroxide and potassium ferricyanide inactivation and subsequent
ascorbate recovery. Two recovery edges qualify the native-complex object as a
distinct previously inactivated state, one for each oxidant. These assertions do
not establish physiological regulation, baseline activation, damaged residues,
metal-state occupancy or treatment-specific doses and recovery protocols. The
historical preparation remains distinct from the complete 2009 sequence.

The oxidants are CHEBI:16240 and the exact potassium salt CHEBI:30060. The
unspecified ascorbate stereochemistry is preserved with the chemical-entity class
CHEBI:22651. Issuing and pinned chemical identities, counterions, composition and
charge were inspected; no single exact structure is invented for that class.
The successor retains 22 quoted words from the primary abstract, including all
repeated qualifier evidence. These four edges and two molecular-state qualifiers
address issue #531 without rewriting the original author evidence or independent
finding. PraB accepts NAD and lower-activity NADP in vitro;

curation normalization, not assay protonation measurements. Magnesium added to
