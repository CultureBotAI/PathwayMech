# Pathways reviewed against the 184-record baseline

The publication adds two complete pathways: **MetaCyc:BIOTIN-BIOSYNTHESIS-PWY** (biotin biosynthesis I) and **MetaCyc:PWY0-1471** (uracil degradation III). Together they add 179 mechanistic edges and 310 evidence entries. The resulting maintained corpus has 186 records. The original 184 records, history and saved reviews remain unchanged.

Biotin retains all fourteen native steps, including the early BioC/BioH and fatty-acid elongation branch. Its first condensation catalyst and the formal BioB donor convention remain explicitly qualified. Uracil degradation retains the complete modern Rut route, distinguishes demonstrated YdfG activity from the predicted RutE alternative, and preserves the revised RutC/RutD evidence and experimental conditions. See `biotin-curation.md`, `rut-curation.md`, their source manifests, independent audits and immutable reviews for the evidence.

**CMP-Kdo is withheld from the maintained corpus.** Its independent review identified a material alpha-versus-beta substrate conflict in the exported biological assertions. [Issue346](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/PathwayMech/issues/346) remains open. The exact reviewed candidate is retained at `deferred/cmp-kdo-biosynthesis.yaml`; it is research evidence, not a published pathway record. `kdo-withheld.json` maps the former working-tree target and unpublished history scaffold to their byte-identical archives. `kdo-publication-exclusion.json` verifies exclusion from current records, pages and KGX. The original `kdo-*` author dossiers and replay describe the historical candidate, including their former target path; they do not supersede the adverse review. Future replay should use an isolated research checkout rather than restore the rejected candidate into a publication tree.

The shared authority snapshots retain independently verified identifier/name facts for both published pathways and the withheld candidate. Those names do not assert the disputed KdsB catalysis. All baseline authority entries and source-manifest objects are preserved. The first complete test run used the 187-record draft including CMP-Kdo; after withdrawal, rendering, corpus validation, identifiers, lint and KGX were rerun for the final 186-record publication. The renderer, exporter, tests, biotin and Rut scientific bytes were unchanged by the withdrawal. Exact commands, times and hashes are in `integration-validation.json`.

`identifiers-added.json` enumerates every identifier in the two published records and identifies additions relative to the full baseline. `generated-graph-kgx-audit.json` checks generated SVG and KGX against the final YAML. It is structural verification; the CUA inventory exposed no browser surface for visual or interaction inspection. The shared iModulonDB queries were replayed from retained response bytes and remain computational expression context only.

Discovery continues. `pending-leads.json` retains dTDP-L-rhamnose, the Gram-positive heme route, a guanine-salvage lead and the blocked CMP-Kdo candidate. The frozen PathBank screening and refreshed exact-title hints do not establish semantic coverage or source exhaustion. Duplicate searches included hidden and ignored files in both the current worktree and the now-readable, older original checkout.
