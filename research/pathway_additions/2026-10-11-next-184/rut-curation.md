# Uracil degradation III: complete corrected Rut route

The selected identity is **MetaCyc:PWY0-1471**, “uracil degradation III.” GO:0006212 denotes uracil breakdown generally and does not distinguish this route from other uracil catabolic mechanisms. The original MetaCyc22.5 definition and PathBank:SMP0002040/PW002026.owl establish the named boundary: uracil to two nitrogen equivalents, carbon dioxide and 3-hydroxypropionate. The new record preserves that entire boundary while replacing obsolete elementary assignments with the later experiments and current reviewed reaction annotations. It adds the explicitly required RutF FMN-recycling reaction, not a neighboring metabolic pathway.

## Evidence and modern correction

The complete 2010 primary papers were inspected as retained PMC HTML. Mukherjee et al. (PMID20369853, paragraphs P1–P8, Figure1 and Scheme1) directly characterized RutA ring opening with partially purified RutF or substituted Fre, FMN and NADH. Kim et al. (PMID20400551, Methods sec13, Results sec17–20 and24, Discussion sec26, Figure5, Table4) measured ureidoacrylate hydrolysis and NADPH-dependent terminal reduction by YdfG. Their peracid, RutC-reductase and RutD-deaminase assignments were mechanistic proposals, not demonstrated catalyst assignments.

Adak and Begley (PMID28661684; complete PMC6040642 HTML, Figure4 and its preceding experiment) established FMN-N5-oxide by comparison with a synthetic standard, mass spectrometry and oxygen-isotope labeling. The selected current Rhea31588 net includes the subsequent spontaneous reduction of that flavin state with NADH. It does not assign every elementary step to RutA or introduce an extra free peracid pathway branch. The 2020 publisher abstract (PMID32066967; DOI10.1038/s41589-020-0476-2) reports the further N5-peroxide mechanistic refinement. Only its abstract and public figure captions/data-availability material were accessible in the retained publisher HTML; its complete Results and supplement were not inspected, and no detailed claim is attributed to them.

The complete 2021 primary JATS XML (PMID33839153, PMC8113886) was inspected. The Results, Figure2/Table1 and coupled-assay Methods directly establish RutC 3-aminoacrylate deamination against spontaneous background and an inactive R104A control. Figure6 and the polarity experiments establish that the earlier RutC/RutD deletion growth defects do not prove essentiality: inactive RutC plus truncated RutD in the supplied operon restores growth under the tested room-temperature conditions. RutD-mediated carbamate hydrolysis remains a plausible model. The graph retains spontaneous carbamate decay without a RutD catalyst edge.

The biochemical reconstruction therefore uses six reactions: RutF21622, net RutA31588, explicit RutB31604, spontaneous carbamate15650, RutC34948 and terminal reduction26440. Net RutB42624 is excluded to avoid counting carbamate decomposition twice. Thymine reactions and the alternative 2-aminoacrylate activity are outside this exact uracil route.
