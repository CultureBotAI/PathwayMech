## Exact pathway boundary

The exact native ID is **MetaCyc:GALLATE-DEGRADATION-II-PWY**, issuing display name **gallate degradation I**. Do not correct the intentional accession/name numbering difference. Its four formal members are:

1. `GALLATE-DIOXYGENASE-RXN` / directional `RHEA:28928`: gallate + oxygen to keto 4-oxalomesaconate + two protons.
2. `RXN-9983` / `RHEA:28932`: keto 4-oxalomesaconate to the formal E,E enol. The native reaction is reversible and explicitly spontaneous; catalytic GalD and historical alternatives also exist.
3. `RXN-2463` / `RHEA:17403`: select the native **right-to-left** direction, hydrating the formal enol to generic 4-carboxy-4-hydroxy-2-oxoadipate (CHA).
4. `RXN-2464` / `RHEA:28936`: CHA cleavage to pyruvate + oxaloacetate.

All four complete selected equations balance and match the independently extracted directional Rhea equations. The net equation is gallate + oxygen + water → pyruvate + oxaloacetate + two protons. The three downstream native reactions are reversible; a pathway arrow is the selected route direction, not a claim that their reverse reactions are chemically impossible.

`GALLATE-DEGRADATION-I-PWY` displays **gallate degradation II** and is the distinct five-member PDC route. `GO:0019396` is broader, not an exact pathway identity. Existing `P184-PWY` shares the three downstream reactions and GalD/GalB/GalC; it is not this complete route. TCA/oxaloacetate and upstream syringate `PWY-6339` are interfaces, not additional formal reaction members.
