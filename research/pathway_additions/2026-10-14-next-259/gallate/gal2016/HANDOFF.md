Completed bounded author research for PMID:26867578 / PMC4817193 / DOI:10.1074/jbc.M115.682054. This is a claim-proposal package, not an independent review or an authority approval. No maintained files were changed.

The full retained article, all five tables, nine figure images and six equation images were inspected. `claim-ledger.json` contains 34 bounded claims, including 15 edge-proposal entries. Every source assertion is at most 400 characters and has a native HTML/figure/table locator; no verbatim primary quotations were added. `source-coverage-ledger.json` accounts for main text, figures, tables, equations, adjacent-pathway evidence and conditional negatives. `native-table-rows.json` preserves all 80 parsed table rows, including headers and section rows.

The strongest additions for a gallate record are reversible CHM hydration by GalB, the experimentally observed negative optical CHA product, and in vitro zinc/iron(II)/cobalt(II) cofactor support. GalA/GalD preparation, the four-enzyme coupled pathway, and omission/substitution controls are also useful, but individual GalA/GalD/GalC identity and chemistry should join the parent's 2005/2011 evidence rather than derive solely from a coupled assay.

Critical limits:

- GalD is explicitly recombinant and retains its His6 tag because of an internal thrombin site. The tag terminus is not specified here. GalB's four-residue N-terminal truncation is the crystallographic construct; preserve it for crystal zinc and structure claims.
- KT2440 is the GalB gene source; KT2442 is the explicit native-host overexpression source for metal analysis. E. coli BL21(lambdaDE3) is the recombinant host. General kinetic methods do not identify every GalB assay batch's host, and GalB's tag status is not explicit. Do not assert a native unmodified molecule or assign every kinetic batch to one expression host.
- The paper supplies no GalB protein accession and no separate GalC preparation description for the coupled assay. Join independently inspected construct and authority evidence before binding CURIEs. LigJ comparison material is Comamonas E6 PmdE (BAI50711.1), while the comparative LigJ crystal is from R. palustris; neither is automatically the SYK-6 protein.
- Polarimetry identifies (−)-CHA; Figure 9 explicitly leaves its absolute configuration unknown. The depicted S product is model-compatible, not an experimentally established absolute product. The (2E,4E)-CHM identity relies on prior characterization; the fresh 2016 result establishes use of the same GalD-produced substrate by GalB and E6 LigJ.
- Zinc predominated after aerobic native-host purification, with only about 23% occupancy. The authors explicitly leave the in vivo metal unresolved because isolation could lose iron. Fe2+ and Co2+ support higher assay turnover than Zn2+, but efficiencies differ by less than threefold.
- Long EDTA treatment is reported below assay error, while Table 2 gives apoGalB 4.0% and EDTA 0.030% residual activity under the panel conditions. Retain these conditions; neither universal zero activity nor metal-independent catalysis follows. The low Cu2+ panel result is not a cellular inhibition/regulation claim.
- CHPD/HMG reversible activity is real in vitro but lies outside this narrow gallate route; the DDVA connection is proposed. Figure 8 is a small computational cluster sample, not experimental confirmation of complete pathways in all displayed taxa.
- Figure 6's caption swaps B/C relative to the actual axes; small Table 5 versus narrative numerical differences and Figure 2 interface-panel inconsistencies are recorded without silently resolving them.
