# DesB 2014 author evidence handoff

Fresh bounded reading of PMID:24657997 / PMC3962378 / DOI:10.1371/journal.pone.0092249 is complete. Publisher HTML and XML were snapshotted before parsing. All main text, five main figures, two main tables, and all ten linked supplementary PDFs were inspected; each supplement is one page and was rendered with MuPDF and visually read. The 2009 expression/purification reference explicitly cited by the paper was also retrieved and read to clarify construct provenance. This package is author research, not an independent review or authority approval. No maintained files changed.

`claim-ledger.json` contains 27 bounded claims and four schema-compatible edge proposals: DesB catalysis, gallate input, oxygen input, and ferrous cofactor. Direct gallate/PCA binding observations are retained separately because the schema does not have a binding predicate. `source-coverage-ledger.json` accounts for every main section and all 17 visual/table objects. `main-table-rows.json` retains all 65 main-table rows. The raw supplement tables and their legible rendered copies are retained externally.

The source supports recombinant SYK-6 DesB as a swapped homodimer, with a non-heme Fe(II) site in each intersubunit active site, distinct from KT2440 GalA. Its reactivated wild-type material turns over gallate; H124F and H192F are specifically qualified inactive variants under the reported assay. Gallate induces a substrate-coordinating iron position in some anaerobic structures, while bound PCA is nonproductive.

Limits to preserve during integration:

- The assay reactivation mixes EDTA-treated apoDesB with FeCl2 and ascorbate under anaerobic conditions. These preparation chemicals do not all become physiological substrates, cofactors, or regulators. Activity was measured at 30 C in air-saturated Tris buffer. The authors explicitly caution that reactivation and assay differences affect comparisons with prior data.
- The 2014 preparation cites PMID:19923743 / DOI:10.1107/S1744309109041086. Its section 2.1 identifies E. coli BL21(DE3), a desB expression plasmid tied to nucleotide accession AB190989, and POROS PI/HQ purification. Neither inspected method description supplies explicit tag state or a protein accession. Native authority binding belongs to the parent/native agent; do not use AB190989 as a protein CURIE.
- Figure 1A draws an unnamed enol ring-opened product, without fresh product stereochemical identification. Reconcile its drawing with native OMA/CHM reaction assertions and the earlier chemistry before assigning an exact tautomer or E/Z identity.
- Table S1 reproduces 2005 Kasai et al. data rather than fresh 2014 measurements. It reports ND for DesB with PCA/3MGA but does not define ND in that supplement. The discussion also mentions trace pyrogallol activity, so the title's strict specificity should not become a universal no-other-substrate claim. Historical desB knockout/growth findings likewise need the original Kasai2005 paper.
- The R/A iron occupancies were set to 0.5 during refinement, not measured as equal populations. Two iron positions do not mean two simultaneous iron cofactors per active site. Some crystals lacked a clearly refinable shifted site. The authors leave the detailed productive coordination geometry and dynamics uncertain.
- Reversibility refers to the proposed internal iron shift, not reverse chemical ring closure. His124/His192 acid/base roles and the oxygen cavity are proposed mechanistic interpretations. The aerobic-crystal experiment permits an alternative in which substrate is consumed by enzyme dissolved into solution.
- Table S2 conflicts internally with the main text for aerobic 3WRA: protomer B substrate occupancy is listed as 1.0, while substrate B-factors are blank and the text says no bound substrate. Preserve the discrepancy; do not promote that cell as a measurement.
- The publisher-linked Figure S6 PDF (`s006.pdf`) prints Figure S7 and labels the ligand PCA, whereas its publisher caption describes gallate. The oxygen site is predicted, and this artifact cannot establish observed oxygen binding.
- Alternative-metal discussion cites other dioxygenases. It does not supply fresh DesB cobalt/zinc/manganese evidence, nor permit transfer from GalB2016.

All 21 direct-download artifact hashes were rechecked in `verification.json`; two additional saved web transcriptions provide publisher/identifier context. The raw PubMed HTML request returned a challenge response and is explicitly excluded as biological evidence; the public PubMed web transcription verifies PMID/PMCID/DOI. No main-paper or supplement access gaps remain. The 2009 follow-up is used only for the cited preparation; its text was read, but its separate figures were not visually reviewed.
