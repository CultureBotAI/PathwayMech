## Material integration decisions

* Keep the full five-step route and its native ordering/module boundaries. PWY-5419/PWY-5420 are distinct oxidative sibling context. The shared terminal module's occurrence in PWY0-1277 does not make the full route redundant.
* PWY-5162 explicitly lists ADHE-CPLX as enzymes-not-used. Its full assembly, alternative activities and regulators are closed for exclusion/adjacent context; do not make AdhE a route catalyst merely because the generic acetaldehyde reaction lists it.
* REG-5876 records NADH inhibition, but the cited 1993 primary directly shows NADH stimulation of aldolase in purified native CF600 DmpFG, with lesser NAD+ stimulation. Delayed-addition controls localize the effect to the aldolase reaction. Reject the native inhibitory sign as accepted biology while preserving it as source metadata. Do not invent direct nucleotide binding or a demonstrated allosteric transmission mechanism.
* REG-5875 and REG-5877 label Zn2+ inhibition and Mn2+ activation ALLOSTERIC. The 1993 paper supports effects, not those specific allosteric mechanisms. Mn2+ at 1 mM stimulates six- to eightfold; Zn2+ inhibits; Mg2+/Ca2+ have no effect in that preparation. EDTA dialysis did not lower activity, and essential tightly bound metal versus activation remained unresolved in that paper. These claims do not transfer to MhpE or other aldolases.
* DmpFG is a biological 2+2 tetramer containing two DmpFG catalytic heterodimers. The 1993 native solution estimate is 148 kDa; the inspected 2003 p6993 section explicitly calls the biological unit a 140-kDa tetramer. Its crystal asymmetric unit contains two tetramers/four heterodimers. Do not turn the heterodimer subassembly or crystal-unit count into the complete physiological stoichiometry.
* Preserve formal 2Z-dienol CPD-14447, historical 2E CPD-6761 and keto OXOPENTENOATE source distinctions. Parent/CPLX-7241 prose and formal native chemistry differ. Root independently found the native hydrolase's Rhea14552/14549 cross-reference uses keto CHEBI11641 rather than native formal2Z product; exact reaction replacement belongs to chemistry adjudication. Historical substrate labels do not establish assay stereochemical purity.
* Bordetella sp.10d hydratase has native Mg2+ cofactor and Hg2+ inhibition, a 470-kDa partner preparation, 29/27-kDa bands and 1:1 molar ratio; stoichiometry was not reported. Protein prose names3E-2-oxopent-3-enoate while the native activity points to formal2Z dienol. The eight decarboxylase inhibitors remain scoped to the adjacent partner activity.
* E. coli MhpE class-I prose conflicts with imported manganese-binding annotations at15/197/199. Current annotations use sequence-model inference, not metal-binding measurements. Keep both and their evidence. MhpE/MhpF solubility/translation coupling and MhpF monomer activity remain their own source assertions, not DmpFG assembly transfers.
* PKO1 TbuJ/K evidence uses unpurified cloned-cell extracts and historical substrate preparations; six-carbon alternatives were not directly assayed. TodH/TodI have expression/sequence evidence; TodG uses mutant/construct evidence. Bilophila retains its tagged recombinant preparation, pH dependence and native partially-purified versus prose-purified discrepancy. The complete 29 catalyst decisions preserve these distinctions.
* Parent-route taxon assignments, child-module assignments, component-source taxa and heterologous hosts remain separate. Saccharolobus98/2 parent assignment lacks a species-specific experimental support item here. Historical mt-2, generic P. putida and modern sequence taxa must not be merged without identity authority. Gene/product relations remain native identity context; no unsupported encodes edge or accession is minted.
* The three compartment objects support only their specific protein locations. The Chlamydomonas ADH1 chloroplast and E. coli component cytosol/membrane locations do not localize the entire catechol route.

The chemical endpoint handoff contains all 22 exact full originals and their role associations. CPD-400 is guaiacol; CPD0-1364 is 3-pyridinecarboxaldehyde adenine dinucleotide; Thiols is a generic class. ZN+2 is Zn2+ (InChI=1S/Zn/q+2). Earlier unverified names, organism wording and one charge typo are explicitly superseded in communication-correction-ledger.json. The old PMC2531070 BioC file is an endpoint error, not a paper; it was never used as primary evidence.

## How to use the package

Start with native-closure-verification.json, formal-activity-closure.json, curated-catalyst-scope-decisions.json, material-conflicts-and-dispositions.json and follow-policy-and-open-gaps.json. Assembly, catalysis, cofactor, regulation, location, ordering, gene and taxon projections preserve exact source locators and hashes. Complete originals and slot XML remain available for any unresolved reading. The 132 mechanism candidates are author extraction aids, not preapproved graph edges; REG-5876 is explicitly rejected.

primary-conflict-evidence-bindings.json binds the personally inspected conflict locations to the primary role's stable artifacts. The 1993 artifact is a complete transformed extraction, not raw PDF bytes. The 2003 artifact is only the successful official p6992–6993 excerpt embedded alongside failed requests, not an entire-paper read. MhpF Ki/mode remains a native assertion pending primary details. Chemistry and identity remain separately owned. Later primary findings should be added as a successor dossier, never by rewriting this frozen package.

compact-retention-manifest.json proposes portable evidence/decision files and points to all external originals through hashes. Full originals, raw XML projections, intermediate receipts and helpers remain external provenance. Helpers are curation-only, not product runtime code. artifact-manifest.json hashes every package file except itself, including ignored/hidden paths if present. Its own digest is supplied in the final handoff message. Any retained subset must preserve the full-object, version and source/preparation distinctions; compact retention is not permission to drop a difficult mechanism.
