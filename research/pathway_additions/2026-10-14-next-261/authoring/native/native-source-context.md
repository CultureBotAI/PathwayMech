### ADHE-MONOMER:comment:1:paragraph2

The unusual quarternaty structure of AdhE was noticed very early; 20-60 subunits are helically arranged to form rod-like ultrastructures that appear to change conformation in the presence of Fe 2+ and NADH [ Kessler92 ]. More recently, cryo-EM structures confirmed the higher-order helical architecture and conformational changes upon cofactor addition [ Kim19 , Pony20 , Kim20 ]. The two active sites are topologically separated; ALDH active sites face the outer surface, while ADH active sites face the inner surface [ Kim19 ]. Helical filament formation is essential for AdhE function in vitro and in vivo ; the extended conformation allows substrate channeling between the two active sites [ Pony20 , Kim20 ]. Crystal structures of the apo- and cofactor-bound alcohol dehydrogenase domain of AdhE have been solved at 1.95 and 1.65 Å resolution, respectively [ Azmi20 ].
