Catechol degradation III is the complete six-step ortho route from catechol to succinate and acetyl-CoA. Oxygen-dependent intradiol cleavage gives cis,cis-muconate, followed by cycloisomerization to (S)-muconolactone, isomerization to the enol lactone, hydrolysis to 3-oxoadipate, succinyl-CoA-dependent CoA transfer and thiolysis. The four-step catechol ortho-cleavage module and the two-step 3-oxoadipate degradation module are both represented inside this parent. The independently reconciled formal net is catechol + O2 + H2O + CoA(4−) → succinate(2−) + acetyl-CoA(4−) + 2 H+; succinyl-CoA is consumed by transferase and regenerated by thiolase.
