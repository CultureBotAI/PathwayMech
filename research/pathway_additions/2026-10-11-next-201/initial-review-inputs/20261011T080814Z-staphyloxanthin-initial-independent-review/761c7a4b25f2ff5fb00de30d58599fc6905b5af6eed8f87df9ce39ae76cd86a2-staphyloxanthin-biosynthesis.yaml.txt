label: staphyloxanthin biosynthesis
description: 'The complete native nine-reaction pathway converts two farnesyl diphosphates through enzyme-retained
  presqualene diphosphate, three successive CrtN desaturations, CrtP oxidation, AldH oxidation, glycosylation
  and terminal acylation to staphyloxanthin. The final pigment has beta-D-glucose and a 12-methyltetradecanoyl
  group; upstream isoprenoid and fatty-acyl donor biosynthesis are outside this boundary. Five reviewed
  protein sequences are from S. aureus Newman; the source-defined AldH is from KCTC1928, with direct loss-of-function
  evidence in RN4220. These strain scopes are not collapsed into an experimentally verified Newman six-protein
  reconstitution. The older five-enzyme model and CrtP acid-forming assignment are superseded by AldH
  evidence. Engineered E. coli makes different C14/C16 fatty-acyl analogues. Native activated glycosyl
  and acyl donor assignments remain qualified: the CrtQ step preserves broad nucleotide-sugar classes,
  and the CrtO acyl-CoA is a formal native proposal rather than a purified donor-specific assay. The carotenoid
  fatty-acyl stereocenter remains unspecified as in the selected authorities. CrtP AH2/A are generic donor/acceptor
  classes; FAD side accounting for CrtN and formal NAD+ use by AldH do not establish complete physiological
  redox recycling. Functional protein edges do not establish monomeric sufficiency or fixed oligomeric
  stoichiometry; CrtN has heterogeneous active aggregates. Later AldH dimer structures used a C244S mutant
  and a diapolycopenal analogue; their assembly is not silently assigned to wild-type KCTC1928 AldH.'
