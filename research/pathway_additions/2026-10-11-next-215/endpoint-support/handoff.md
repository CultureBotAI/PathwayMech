These source conventions can be represented as such; they cannot be attributed to the 2015 cyclic-product characterization or described as proof that free cellular staphyloferrin B is exclusively linear.

The original publisher supplement to PMID 26030732 / DOI 10.1021/jacs.5b04557 provides the missing distinction. **S7** describes synthetic SB as two epimers in a 1:1 mixture arising from equilibrating lactamization; **Tables S3/S4 and Figure S2** show the five-membered hemiaminal and its unassigned additional stereocenter. This is a ring–chain chemical distinction, not a mere drawing convention or protonation difference. The separate SBimide conversion is not the same process. Nor may the supplement's earlier fragment/pH discussion be generalized to the Newman cellular equilibrium. The abstract's biological experiments concern USA300 JE2; exact Newman intracellular form proportions remain unmeasured in the inspected sources.
