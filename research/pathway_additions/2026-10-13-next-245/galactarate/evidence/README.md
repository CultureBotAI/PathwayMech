# External original retention

`external-archive-manifest.json` binds all 35 record references to unchanged original bytes and verified external archive members. It also retains the source originals used by the independent 2020/2025 appraisals, chemical identity audit, preflight, and the twelve final new source-authority owners. This is a retention receipt, not evidence that every retained source was read; use the corresponding reading ledgers for appraisal scope.

Seven archives remain outside the repository: author, preflight, identity, GarD 2020, oxidized-sugar 2025, whole Rhea RDF, and new authority originals. All 159 members were read back and SHA256-verified after archive creation. The authority archive contains the twelve selected staging inputs and all four newly acquired NCBI Gene responses; pre-existing authority owners remain bound through the canonical union's provenance rather than being recaptured wholesale.

Each scientific original has its public acquisition URL, response hash, source version where available, and archive/member binding. Failed requests and challenge/error responses remain distinguishable from usable evidence. Complete raw bodies, PDFs, images, spreadsheets and original acquisition histories are not committed. A temporary filesystem path records where an archive was retained; it is not a claim of public or permanent hosting.

Archive metadata is deterministic: sorted regular members, zero mtime/uid/gid, empty owner names, fixed 0644 permissions, and gzip with zero mtime and no filename. The original member bytes are unchanged. Parent integration owns the record, author matrices, canonical snapshots, native reviews and publication gates.
