Assayed GarD was recombinant and TEV-cleaved; “native” in Methods 4.5 distinguishes unlabeled activity material from SeMet crystals. Assay phosphorylation occupancy is unspecified.

At 30°C, degassed/semi-anaerobic ten-minute assays used 1 mg/ml protein, 30 mM galactarate, 90 mM β-mercaptoethanol, Tris pH7, and ±0.5 mM FeSO4. Six measurements gave approximately 4 μmol/min/mg with iron; activity without iron was undetectable. Product assignment used 250-nm semicarbazone detection and enzyme-free background subtraction, not independent stereochemical determination or substrate screening.

SEC/nativePAGE support approximately 130-kDa dimers. SeMet crystals contain calcium; iron-bound crystals were unsuccessful. Calcium rescue, manganese synergy, iron stoichiometry/sites, Fe3+ substitution, and phosphorylation-dependent regulation are not established. Predicted iron ligands remain hypotheses.
