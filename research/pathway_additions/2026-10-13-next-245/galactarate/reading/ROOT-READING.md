## 2000 regulation, PMID10762278

The MC4100 experiments support coordinated expression during growth on
galactarate, glucarate and glycerate. Table1 gives enzyme activities and the
single-copy lacZ reporter experiments distinguish the garD and garPLRK
transcriptional units. Figure1 supports gene arrangement; Figure2 supports the
reported induction pattern. Downstream garL/R/K expression is an operon-level
inference, not independently mapped promoters. JA175 garP::Tn5 is polar, so it
does not isolate transport function. Alternative uptake is plausible but the
source does not directly demonstrate GudP flux.

SdaR/CdaR loss in JA176 and JA177 and pRM1 complementation support positive
control. Mutants are controls for active regulator inference, not native active
protein endpoints. The historical 391-residue reference differs from current
P37047's 385 residues; the paper's P297S numbering is not remapped. The sdaR
reporter supports positive autoregulation. Acid-dependent induction does not
establish direct CdaR ligand binding. These distinctions appear on regulatory
and induction edges.

The HTML includes two figures and Table1, all read. The source's statements are
bounded to the inspected MC4100 conditions and named derivatives. No claim of
all E. coli strains or bacterial distribution is inferred from this experiment.

## 2015 GarK product selection, PMID25823014

The four-deletion background removes both phosphoglycerate mutases and both
glycerate kinases. Plasmid GarK/GlxK restores the lower-metabolism entry phenotype,
whereas the plant enzyme supports upper-metabolism entry. Table1 and Figure2
therefore support the 2-phosphoglycerate product. Figure1 is experimental design
context, not a new complete galactarate-pathway measurement.

Cloning describes His6-bearing forward primers without reporting tag cleavage.
The Results, Figure2 and Table1 give the explicit four-deletion genotype. The
printed donor spelling K12 Mg1665 is retained as a source discrepancy. Growth
was measured at 37°C, with 0.2% carbon supplements and repeated OD observations;
the authors note clone-yield variation. No native oligomeric state or metal
occupancy is inferred. Both original images are PNG bytes despite their local
.jpg suffix; acquisition records preserve actual content type and byte hashes.

## Structured-source and additional-primary coverage

The complete native four-step reaction list, reaction-ordering, reaction-layout,
species and compound sides were inspected. GarD and GarL complexes are the
native two- and six-subunit assemblies; their membership and catalytic
assignment are separate claims. Direct native UniProt cross-references ground
the GarR/GarK assignments. The broad GO catabolic process includes a second
route and is not used as exact pathway identity.

Reviewed GarL metal annotations identify magnesium and less-efficient cobalt,
iron and manganese alternatives. They are database-supported rather than an
unseen primary-paper appraisal. Historical phosphate-acid proposals are disputed;
inhibition lists lack independently appraised concentrations/conditions here,
so they are left out rather than promoted to causal edges. GarR donor-class
annotations do not erase the native intermediate stereochemistry.

The 2020 paper provides recombinant GarD activity and solution-dimer evidence;
its calcium-containing SeMet crystal is distinct from the unlabeled activity
sample. The exact SNA structural remnant is confined to the identity dossier,
while the graph cites the paper's three-residue structural observation and
leaves activity-sample scar/phosphorylation occupancy unspecified.

The 2025 paper adds GarP/L/R participation through BW25113 deletion and plasmid
rescue, using an acidification endpoint and a BL21 positive comparator. It does
not add a fifth core reaction or prove transport coupling. Its separate 5α
growth experiment and Enterocloster alternative machinery are not merged into
the MG1655 native chemistry. The graph avoids exact uniform replicate-count or
purified-chemistry claims. Full source-data and reading boundaries are retained
in the independent 2025 appraisal.

PMID9772162 remains abstract-only after retained access failures. Its database
citation supports attribution of database annotations, not a claim that the
