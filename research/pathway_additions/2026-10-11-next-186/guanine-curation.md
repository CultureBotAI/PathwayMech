The selected record is `MetaCyc:PWY-6579`, **superpathway of guanine and guanosine salvage**, at `data/pathways/guanine-and-guanosine-salvage.yaml`. The exact complete native superpathway identity is therefore used. The superclass name is not used as a pathway identifier.

Hidden- and ignored-inclusive searches were executed in both the new worktree and the original Documents checkout, both at baseline 407f1e2. Only Git metadata and virtual environments were excluded. ## Protein, strain and assembly evidence

The original reviewed entries identify Gsk P0AEW6/b0477, DeoD P0ABP8/b4384 and Gpt P0A9M5/b0238 as K-12 proteins. Native complexes CPLX0-322, DEOD-CPLX and GPT-CPLX explicitly annotate MG1655 and contain 2, 6 and 4 subunits, respectively. Exact native component identifiers are bridged through inspected UniProt BioCyc cross-references; GSK-MONOMER and GPT-MONOMER native UNIPROT xrefs were also checked. Canonical recommended protein names and original entry/sequence versions are preserved; no global UniProt release is inferred from an entry's annotation date.

The main primary source, PMID32857952 (DOI10.1016/j.molcel.2020.08.005), was published in 2020 and became available in PMC in 2021. Complete original HTML and independently acquired BioC XML were inspected. Results, Fig. 1–3 and methods support Gsk activity, GDP/ppGpp binding and inhibition, ligand-dependent oligomerization and the MG1655-derived mutant scope. Gsk expressed in BL21(DE3) for purified-protein experiments is distinguished from the MG1655-derived physiological strains. Affinity tags were removed before biochemical and biophysical measurements. The native dimer is supported in inhibitor-free solution and by the interface-mutant activity test; ppGpp and GDP favor tetramers, with a protein-concentration-dependent equilibrium. The lower specific activity of ppGpp-bound tetramers was directly tested. No constitutive dimer, exclusive dimer, physiological octamer or independently catalytic monomer is asserted. The crystal octamer is discussed by the authors as an artifact; no corresponding graph node is invented.

PMID10879466 was inspected at primary-abstract scope and reports purified Gsk, dimeric mass, substrate/phosphate-donor breadth, GDP inhibition and the ATP/Mg2+ ratio. Gsk's additional free-Mg2+ requirement remains probable in the current UniProt annotation. Only magnesium is asserted as a cofactor; potassium stimulation/binding is retained as ungraphed context, without inventing occupancy or an obligatory cofactor requirement.

PMID235429 was inspected at primary-abstract scope and supports the DeoD six-subunit assembly. Current reviewed UniProt supplies the exact guanosine activity, reversibility and corresponding experimental evidence codes. PMID9100006 was inspected at primary-abstract scope and directly supports Gpt's tetramer, phosphoribosyltransferase activity and bound Mg2+; no crystal-derived ion count is extrapolated to physiological stoichiometry. Full-text-only details of these older papers are not claimed as directly inspected. The native pathway annotation, individual enzymes and Gsk mutant studies do not establish simultaneous wild-type flux through both routes under every condition.

## Conflicts and rejected assertions

- **Native copied summary:** PWY-6579 has the correct name, species, subpathway membership and layouts, but its comment describes adenosine/adenine/AMP metabolism. That comment is rejected as mechanistic evidence. Scope comes from the nested reaction lists and independent chemistry/protein sources.
- **GTP inhibition remains unresolved:** the 2000 Gsk primary abstract and current reviewed UniProt describe GTP inhibition; the 2020 full paper explicitly says GDP, but not GTP or GMP, provides feedback inhibition. The record cites both statements at the GDP regulation edge, retains the disagreement and asserts no GTP inhibitory edge. It does not claim later literature has definitively resolved the contradiction.
- **Gpt apparent trimer:** the 1983 primary abstract PMID6305942 reports apparent trimer behavior in gel filtration. The 1997 structure abstract PMID9100006 and current native/UniProt entries support a homotetramer, which is represented. The older measurement is retained as a historical discrepancy rather than silently rewritten.
- **Incomplete source acquisitions:** the two Europe PMC fullTextXML attempts for PMC8362273 returned HTTP 500 bodies; accessible PMC HTML and NCBI BioC are used instead. An initial unsupported EG11358-MONOMER request returned 404; the actual GSK-MONOMER identifier was then read from CPLX0-322 and independently fetched. PMID10026179 in a batch search was unrelated and is explicitly rejected. None supports a maintained claim.
- **Other regulation is not exhaustive:** native Gpt annotations describe GMP/ppGpp inhibition and other inhibitors; these remain source context without additional regulatory edges because this curation independently examined Gsk regulation in depth. Their omission is not evidence of absent Gpt regulation.
- **No compartment overclaim:** Gpt has both cytosolic GO evidence and an annotated peripheral membrane association; no exclusive whole-pathway compartment is asserted. Lack of a DeoD cofactor annotation is not cofactor independence.
