## Mechanistic summary

The core hydrolysis consumes one MG-P and one water and produces one anomer-unspecified D-mannopyranose6-phosphate and one(R)-glycerate. Glycerate2-kinase consumes one glycerate and oneATP, producing one2PG, oneADP and onehydron. Rhea27378 provides the exact forward kinase direction. Native RXN0-5216 is retained for hydrolysis because alpha-specific Rhea27867/CHEBI60332 would silently increase product-anomer specificity.

EntryRhea33308 exchanges extracellular MG plus phosphohistidyl HPr for intracellular MG-P plus histidyl HPr. ContextRhea23881 uses PEP and HPr to produce pyruvate and phospho-HPr. Each coefficient is one. HPr is represented by source-backed P0AA04 His15 state qualifiers, not free histidine; the phosphate is charged to PEP only once. MngA is a single658-aa EIIABC chain; His87/Cys192 remain putative relay sites. PtsI is an exact native homodimer; its His189 intermediate supplies HPr. The independently issued MetaCyc assembly and its native polypeptide original preserve the component bridge without weakening the narrower EcoCyc adapter.

MngB is represented as enabling the assigned hydrolysis because the inspected work supports physiology and surrogate mannosidase activity, not purified MG-P turnover. The annotated one divalent metal per subunit is similarity-based, with exact ion/assembly/occupancy unresolved.
