## Entry uptake and induction context — issue 441

The retained Sampaio full-text web observations locate the MC4100 intact-cell entry assay. Results reports weak uptake reduction with glucose, mannose and mannitol, a smaller effect with trehalose, and no observed reduction with fructose. Maltose, arabinose, gluconate, glycerate, 1-alpha-glucosylglycerol, 2-alpha-glucosylglycerate and 2-alpha-mannosylglycerol are negative comparisons. Discussion instead describes mannose and the three glycosyl analogues as not significantly inhibitory at 1 mM against 0.1 micromolar labeled MG. Both statements are retained; the weak-effect/no-significant-effect tension is unresolved. The record makes no direct binding claim, competitive mechanism or inhibition constant from these measurements.

Separately, 300 mM NaCl during MC4100 growth did not induce transport and reduced MG-dependent induction by about half. This is an induction endpoint, distinct from direct enzyme inhibition, the pACS31 inverted-membrane assay, and the KM522 500 mM salt-protection experiment.
