# Complete classical menaquinol-8 superpathway

The selected record is **MetaCyc:PWY-5838**, “superpathway of menaquinol-8 biosynthesis I”, restricted to the evidence-backed E. coli K-12 protein implementation. It contains all ten native reactions. PWY-5837 supplies the seven-reaction chorismate-to-DHNA core, PWY-5783 supplies the one-reaction IspB donor branch, PWY-5852 supplies MenA prenylation, and MENAQUINONESYN-PWY supplies only the terminal UbiE reaction. The latter is not used as the identifier of the entire route. The supplied starting materials include chorismate, 2-oxoglutarate, IPP, farnesyl diphosphate, ATP, CoA and SAM; their upstream biosynthesis is outside this boundary.

Thus this is not labeled an unchanged PathBank import.
