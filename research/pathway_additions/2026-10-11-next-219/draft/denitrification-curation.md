## Primary evidence and material limits

NarK2 has the dominant transport phenotype; NarK1 has a smaller contribution. Discussion paragraph 5 explicitly leaves antiport versus uniport unresolved. Consequently TRANS-RXN0-239 remains an explicit native convention and has no assigned exact PAO1 catalyst. NarK1/NarK2 enable the overall pathway rather than being asserted to catalyze experimentally established 1:1 exchange.
* **PMID17400734**, full original 2007 PMC HTML: PAO1 promoter reporters, mutations and complementation support Anr, Dnr and NarL expression-level regulation. NarL influence on nirS/norC is described as indirect via the regulatory network. No catalytic activation or direct inhibition is inferred. The IHF CHA-A2 experiment is explicitly non-isogenic to PAO1 and is not transferred as a PAO1 protein assay.
* **PMID19935885**, full original 2010 PMC HTML: periplasmic Nap can compensate under selected mutants/conditions. This prevents a claim that Nar is the only possible nitrate-reduction route. Nap is outside the selected native route. The introduction's apparent subunit-name typo is not propagated.
* **PMID25386171**, full original 2014 JATS: nitrate-dependent anoxic growth, reporter/transcript measurements and nosZ-mutant N2O accumulation. Exact PAO1 preparation distinctions are retained; the nirS-N deletion is not an isolated purified NirS experiment.
* **PMID30093706**, full original 2018 JATS: engineered PAO1 nosZ deletion and N2O accumulation support the terminal physiological function.
