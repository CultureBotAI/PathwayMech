### Enzymes, complexes and assays

- EutBC catalysis belongs to the complete native **MetaCyc:ETHAMLY-CPLX**. Native component slots specify six EutB and six EutC chains. Conflicting prose, non-K12 purification, recombinant constructs and the alpha6/beta6 abstract remain separately scoped. No EutB/C chain independently `catalyzes` the whole reaction. Conditional `enables` denotes a source-annotated subunit contribution in the heteromer; omit it if a consumer would equate it with independent catalysis.
- Native EutD complexes have two chains. The 2004 LT2 H6-EutD experiment directly tests acetyl phosphate plus CoA to acetyl-CoA; the 2016 P/Q study separately uses EutD to generate acetyl phosphate from acetyl-CoA and phosphate. These support different assay directions. Conditional rescue or deletion phenotypes do not establish universal essentiality.
- The source E. coli EutE activity was tested through heterologous yeast and is not a purified native-cell assay. Separate LT2 H6-EutE in BL21(DE3) RIL ZH103 was approximately90% pure and had ALDH/ACR activity with NAD(H) preference; tested alcohol-dehydrogenase activity was absent. Engineered MG1655 glucose fermentation is not native ethanolamine flux.
- EutG's K12-derived allele was overexpressed in AL626 **crude extracts**, with nonzero controls. Similar observed NADH/NADPH rates do not establish equal kinetic cofactor preference. The LT2 EutG assignment remains inferred. The ethanol branch remains even where a stronger purified-enzyme assertion is unavailable.
- EutP/Q recombinant LT2 acetate-kinase assays retain tag-cleaved BL21 preparations. The EutQ Gly-Thr remnant is specifically documented; it is not silently assigned to EutP. EDTA-treatment resistance was reported, but documented kinetic and HPLC assays contain6.6mM and1mM MgCl2. Neither magnesium-independent catalysis nor a bound-Mg requirement is inferred. The stronger raw UniProt assertion remains attributable source text under the accepted successor.
- EutP/Q GTP substitution is representable as `has_input` from the already-issued molecular-activity nodes to exact MetaCyc:GTP. This does not alter the canonical ATP/ADP equation or assert an unmeasured GDP output. EutG NADPH and alternate-aldehyde assay facts remain explicit; their precise alternate activity/material identities were not selected merely from name resemblance.
