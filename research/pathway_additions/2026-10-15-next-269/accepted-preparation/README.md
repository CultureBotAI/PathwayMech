# Glutaryl-CoA preparation evidence

These sixteen standard tar.gz archives preserve the selected frozen source, design, chemical diagnostic and independent-review packets listed in [inventory.json](inventory.json). The inventory includes hidden and ignored regular files. All archive members were decoded and byte-hash checked; the original owners were rechecked unchanged. Filesystem metadata is not part of the evidence identity.

The initial locator, direct-taxon and reaction-label findings remain in their original reviews. Their corrections and bounded independent acceptances are separate successors. The accepted 2018 primary ledger is in root-locator-correction.tar.gz. Research JSON node descriptions are prose inputs, not allowed NamedNode fields; the correction acceptance requires visible record prose and faithful incident-edge descriptions and evidence so qualifications also survive KGX export.

The 2010 full main article remains unacquired; its public supplement does not resolve the IP08 kinetic conflict. Generic ETF identity and whole-route balance/flux limits remain explicit. Source-only annotations are not promoted to independently verified experiments. Acceptance applies only to each packet's stated scope.

Inspect or extract an archive into a fresh scratch directory with a standard tar reader. Archived helper scripts are historical artifacts and need not be executed. This finite set does not claim complete discovery coverage, final maintained-record acceptance or authority implementation acceptance.
