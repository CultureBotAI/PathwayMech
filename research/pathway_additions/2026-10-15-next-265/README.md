## Mechanistic summary

The selected reaction sequence is RHEA:48313, RHEA:48309, RHEA:25392,
MetaCyc:RXN-2424, RHEA:76649, RHEA:34852, and RHEA:19483. All 32 material-side
incidences, 18 core chemical identities, and six material-flow joins are
retained. Rhea direction fields preserve source-family orientation; the two
right-to-left members have canonical labels describing the selected pathway
equations. These fields do not reverse the graph's material edges.

The 2010 work identifies the BoxAB product as an epoxide, superseding the
historical dihydrodiol assignment. BoxC opens the ring and releases formate;
BoxD oxidizes the semialdehyde. The fourth reaction, RXN-2424, is explicitly
putative and has no assigned catalyst. Its product uses the exact E chemical
identity CHEBI:195329. Hydration, NAD-dependent oxidation, and thiolysis complete
the source-defined route.

NADP/NADPH cancels in the formal sum:

`benzoyl-CoA + O2 + 3 H2O + NAD + CoA -> 3 H+ + formate + NADH + succinyl-CoA + acetyl-CoA`.

Both sides have C70H100N21O52P8S2 and charge -9 under the selected chemical
representations. This is stoichiometric accounting, not an added elementary
reaction, a thermodynamic calculation, or measured whole-pathway flux.

The graph has 35 participants, seven reactions, four taxon contexts, 67 edges,
68 reference declarations, eight endpoint qualifiers, and 127 evidence items.
Its 45 chemical edges and 22 biological edges distinguish gene products,
complex composition, catalysis, cofactors, location, and CIB regulation.
Functional BoxAB evidence uses mixed native BoxA and tagged BoxB; BoxC is doubly
tagged and BoxD is an MBP fusion preparation. Composition annotations do not
establish a stable isolated BoxAB or BoxABC assembly. Isolated BoxA peroxide
formation is not autonomous benzoyl-CoA epoxidation.

FAD is represented as CHEBI:57692, without claiming that its normalized charge
is an experimentally established bound microstate. MetaCyc:CPD-7 remains the
generic protein-associated iron-sulfur class. Motifs, spectra, and bulk iron
and sulfur measurements do not establish exact ligation, redox state, or
occupancy. The reported FAD content varies with preparation.

CIB BoxR and BzdR transcriptional effects remain separate from A. evansii
enzyme evidence. Benzoyl-CoA relief of BoxR binding retains the 2 mM condition,
tagged protein, active crude extract, and inactive purified aggregate limits.
It does not establish binding affinity, a physical regulator complex, or
anaerobic Box pathway flux.
