# Reproduction and evidence boundaries

The maintained owners are the pathway YAML, source authority manifest, independent
source and ontology snapshots, and the bounded source-builder/parser tests. Pages
and downloadable KGX files are generated. Raw papers, supplements, figures,
native XML/HTML/JSON, RDF and SQL remain external.

1. Recover the exact original artifacts using the acquisition URLs and SHA-256
   bindings in `authorities/`, `route/`, `constructs/`, `native-evidence/` and the
   immutable preceding next253 dossier. Official supplements retain their issuing
   Figshare file IDs and MD5 checks. A failed source request is not evidence that
   its full article was read.
2. Use the reviewed source builder and unchanged ontology builder at the hashes
   in `authorities/canonical-build-commands.json`. Recreate the ID-only selector
   from all retained and planned identifiers; it supplies no names or biological
   assertions. The 997-entry maintained manifest and exact original cache are
   required for the full source union. Preserve all prior objects, not only IDs
   currently appearing in pathway YAML.
3. Run the captured canonical commands with the explicit worktree PYTHONPATH and
   pinned SQL/RDF artifacts. `preservation-and-promotion.json` records exact
   before/after digests and all newly extracted IDs. The source builder's new
   native-alias branch is intentional; it is not described as unchanged.
4. Author helpers retained as `.py.txt` explain the historical construction and
   checks. They are reproducibility references, not production scripts or shell
   instructions to execute blindly. The route fragment and biological fragment
   retain their own stage hashes; final label alignment and LigK assay refinements
   are documented in `primary-assay-followup.json` and `record-index.json`.
5. Run `just render-pages`, `just validate`, `just test`, `just lint`,
   `just vendored-check`, and `git diff --check` using the project environment.
   The final gate receipts bind the actual tested core files and lossless output.
   Review-bundle validation runs again after saving the independent observations.
6. Independently inspect graph/source coverage and generated-page/KGX preservation.
   Working-tree hashes do not establish Git membership. The publication workflow
   separately verifies actual committed blobs, review input hashes, retained
   evidence declarations and the merge-queue/result tree.

The earlier LigK biological ledger's abstract-only wording describes that author's
reading stage. The construct ledger and new primary follow-up explicitly disclose
the later targeted full-text methods and enzyme-property inspection. No historical
research or review bundle is rewritten into a broader reading claim.

The modern LigU/LigJ main ACS methods remain inaccessible through the inspected
routes. Supplement and deposited sequence evidence supports explicit exclusions
and construct distinctions; it does not establish every kinetic preparation's
exact tag state. The source-defined historical pathway remains distinct from a
single-strain experimental reconstruction and from the modern KCH alternative.
