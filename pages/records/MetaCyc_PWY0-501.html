lipoate biosynthesis and incorporation I

PathwayMech / Browse pathways / MetaCyc:PWY0-501

MetaCyc:PWY0-501

The complete two-step endogenous LipB-LipA route in E. coli K-12 transfers an octanoyl group from ACP to a target-protein lysine, then inserts sulfur at C6 and C8. The endpoint is protein-bound dihydrolipoyl state, following the native MetaCyc boundary and current Rhea chemistry. Whole ACP, target-protein, ferredoxin and cluster-carrier states remain distinct from reactive-part ChEBI moieties. Rhea 142 formally releases Fe(III), consumes oxidized ferredoxin and uses four hydrons, whereas MetaCyc 22.5 releases Fe(II), consumes reduced ferredoxin and uses six; those net conventions are preserved as a source conflict, not resolved into a measured physiological electron balance. LipB has active monomer/trimer annotations; the native LipA dimer is retained alongside the UniProt monomer/dimer alternative. The selected proteins are K-12, while primary biochemical abstracts generally report E. coli without independently establishing every strain detail. The older PathBank donor labeled acetyl-ACP is rejected. Fatty-acid synthesis, LplA salvage, Fe-S cluster regeneration, and downstream lipoyl-cofactor turnover are outside the two-step boundary.
