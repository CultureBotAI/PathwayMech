methionine salvage pathway

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY3O-64

The methionine salvage pathway in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C recycles 5'-methylthioadenosine through methylthioribose phosphate, 4-methylthio-2-oxobutanoate, and L-methionine using the SGD GO-CAM MTA salvage reactions.
