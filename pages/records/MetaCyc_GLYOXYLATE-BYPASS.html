glyoxylate cycle

MetaCyc:GLYOXYLATE-BYPASS

The glyoxylate cycle diverts D-threo-isocitrate to succinate and glyoxylate, then condenses glyoxylate with acetyl-CoA and H2O to form (S)-malate.
