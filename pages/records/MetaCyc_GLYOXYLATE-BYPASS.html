glyoxylate cycle

MetaCyc:GLYOXYLATE-BYPASS

The glyoxylate cycle assimilates acetyl-CoA through citrate synthase, aconitase, isocitrate lyase and malate synthase. Malate dehydrogenase regenerates oxaloacetate, closing the cycle while succinate is released. Enzyme activities have been demonstrated in Mycobacterium tuberculosis.

central carbon metabolism
