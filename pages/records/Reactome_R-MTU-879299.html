Mycothiol biosynthesis

Reactome:R-MTU-879299

Mycobacterium tuberculosis mycothiol biosynthesis converts alpha-D-glucose 6-phosphate to mycothiol through inositol-phosphate, GlcNAc-Ins-P, GlcNAc-Ins, GlcNI, and desacetylmycothiol intermediates.
