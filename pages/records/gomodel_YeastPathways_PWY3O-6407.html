phosphatidate biosynthesis I (the dihydroxyacetone pathway)

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY3O-6407

Phosphatidate biosynthesis I in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C acylates glycerone phosphate to 1-acylglycerone 3-phosphate, reduces it to 1-acyl-sn-glycerol 3-phosphate, and acylates that lysophosphatidate to 1,2-diacyl-sn-glycerol 3-phosphate through the SGD GO-CAM dihydroxyacetone pathway.
