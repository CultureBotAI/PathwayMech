formaldehyde oxidation II (glutathione-dependent)

Formaldehyde oxidation II in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C condenses formaldehyde with glutathione, oxidizes S-(hydroxymethyl)glutathione to S-formylglutathione, hydrolyzes S-formylglutathione to formate and glutathione, and oxidizes formate to carbon dioxide.
