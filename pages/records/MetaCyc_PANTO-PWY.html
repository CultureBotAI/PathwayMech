phosphopantothenate biosynthesis I

Phosphopantothenate biosynthesis I condenses 3-methyl-2-oxobutanoate with 5,10-methylene-tetrahydrofolate to form 2-dehydropantoate, reduces it to (R)-pantoate, ligates (R)-pantoate with beta-alanine to form (R)-pantothenate, and phosphorylates (R)-pantothenate to (R)-4'-phosphopantothenate.
