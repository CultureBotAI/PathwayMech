carnitine shuttle

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY3O-4

The carnitine shuttle in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C uses CAT2, YAT1, and YAT2 carnitine O-acetyltransferase activities to interconvert acetyl-CoA and (R)-carnitine with O-acetyl-L-carnitine and coenzyme A.
