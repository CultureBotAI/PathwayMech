fructoselysine and psicoselysine degradation

PathwayMech / Browse pathways / MetaCyc:PWY0-521

MetaCyc:PWY0-521

The source-defined three-reaction pathway converts psicoselysine to fructoselysine through FrlC, then uses ATP-dependent FrlD phosphorylation and FrlB deglycation to produce glucose 6-phosphate and lysine. Fructoselysine enters at the kinase step. The two downstream reactions overlap the narrower GO:1901281 fructoselysine catabolic record; these are overlapping boundaries, not disjoint capabilities or exact identifier synonyms. The graph selects catabolic traversal while preserving FrlC reversibility and in vitro FrlB reversibility. Native MG1655 annotations, reviewed K-12 protein identities, experimental constructs and conditional growth evidence retain separate scopes.
