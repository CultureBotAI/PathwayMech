MetaCyc:GLCMANNANAUT-PWY

Complete META 27.0 two-child, six-core-reaction amino-sugar degradation pathway, scoped here to E. coli K-12 evidence. NanQ ring opening feeds NanA, NanK and NanE, then NagA and NagB produce fructose 6-phosphate; alpha ring opening is a supported alternative. Neu5Ac/NanT, ManNAc/ManXYZ PTS and GlcNAc/NagE or ManXYZ PTS supply the three entry contexts. Native assemblies, alternative metal cofactors and transcriptional control are retained. Ser1-NagB regulatory assays carry explicit construct qualifiers. Generic transport pools and anomer-specific core species remain distinct. BiGG ManNAc identities retain their native model scope: the substrate InChIKey conflicts with ChEBI:17122, so no exact cross-database alias or intervening stereochemical conversion is asserted. The coupled ManNAc PTS and shared PTS phosphorylation reaction are overlapping descriptions, not additive net stoichiometry. The glycolysis link is an opaque downstream interface. PathBank SMP0002044 is a related conflicting definition lacking NanQ, not an exact identifier synonym.
