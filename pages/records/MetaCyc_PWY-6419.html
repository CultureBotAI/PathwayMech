shikimate degradation II

PathwayMech / Browse pathways / MetaCyc:PWY-6419

MetaCyc:PWY-6419

The MetaCyc shikimate degradation II pathway oxidizes shikimate with NAD+ to 3-dehydroshikimate and dehydrates it to protocatechuate in two reactions. This route is distinct from PQQ-dependent shikimate degradation I and the three-step quinate degradation II route. Strain R growth, uptake and gene-expression evidence is distinguished from ATCC 13032 QsuD and ATCC 13869 QsuB enzyme assays. Native database taxon associations do not establish shikimate uptake or growth in every strain.
