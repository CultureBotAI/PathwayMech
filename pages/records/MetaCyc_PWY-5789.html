3-hydroxypropanoate/4-hydroxybutanate cycle

PathwayMech / Browse pathways / MetaCyc:PWY-5789

MetaCyc:PWY-5789

The complete 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate carbon-fixation cycle is represented for Metallosphaera sedula TH2 (DSM 5348). Acetyl-CoA and propionyl-CoA carboxylation feed malonyl-CoA and methylmalonyl-CoA, followed by succinyl-CoA reduction and conversion through 4-hydroxybutyryl-CoA, trans-crotonyl-CoA and (S)-3-hydroxybutyryl-CoA to two acetyl-CoA molecules. One acetyl-CoA regenerates the acceptor; succinyl-CoA is also an experimentally supported major biosynthetic exit. The 18 native reaction objects correspond to 16 enzymatic transformations plus auxiliary CO2 hydration: two biotin-carboxylase half-reactions are represented by their independently issued net reaction, giving 17 reaction nodes. Native charged intermediates and all current Rhea side coefficients are retained; the native crotonyl-CoA structure is less specific at one conserved CoA stereocenter. The measured archaeal carboxylase assembly and sequence-verified subunits are distinguished from generic bacterial carrier notation. Later primary work supports Msed_2001 and Msed_1423 as the principal crotonyl-CoA hydratase and hydroxybutyryl-CoA dehydrogenase; measured alternative activities remain qualified. Native and current sequence annotation conflicts, inferred cofactors and cross-species recombinant measurements are explicitly scoped. Recombinant Msed_2001 monomer/octamer and Msed_1424 dimer/pentamer estimates remain unresolved rather than universal assembly claims. The selected autotrophic archaeal strain, rather than its E. coli expression hosts or other carbon-fixation pathways, defines the organism scope.
