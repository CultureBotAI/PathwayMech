Acetogenesis

WikiPathways:WP5019

Acetogenesis is a carbon fixation and fermentation pathway in Acetobacterium woodii that reduces carbon dioxide to formate, carries the methyl branch through tetrahydrofolate intermediates, produces carbon monoxide on the carbonyl branch, and forms acetate through acetyl-CoA and acetyl phosphate. The corrinoid-mediated methyl transfer and carbonyl condensation are represented as one aggregate assembly step, with no free methyl-radical intermediate.
