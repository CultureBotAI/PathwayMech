L-tyrosine biosynthesis I

MetaCyc:TYRSYN

L-tyrosine biosynthesis I converts chorismate to prephenate, oxidatively decarboxylates prephenate with NAD+ to 3-(4-hydroxyphenyl)pyruvate, and transaminates 3-(4-hydroxyphenyl)pyruvate with L-glutamate to form L-tyrosine.

Pathway type
amino acid biosynthesis
