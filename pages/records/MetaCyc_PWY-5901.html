2,3-dihydroxybenzoate biosynthesis

PathwayMech / Browse pathways / MetaCyc:PWY-5901

MetaCyc:PWY-5901

The complete three-step pathway converts chorismate to 2,3-dihydroxybenzoate through isochorismate and (2S,3S)-2,3-dihydroxy-2,3-dihydrobenzoate. It follows the exact MetaCyc PWY-5901 boundary and the experimentally resolved EntC–EntB–EntA sequence in Escherichia coli K-12. EntC is an active monomer; its reversible chemistry is oriented toward biosynthesis. EntB contributes its N-terminal isochorismatase activity, represented by the native holo-EntB dimer with two explicitly modified protein components. That form does not establish a requirement for phosphopantetheinylation of its separate carrier domain in DHB production. EntA uses NAD and produces DHB by oxidation followed by rapid aromatization, represented once as the net reaction. Its selected native tetramer coexists with concentration-dependent dimer–tetramer behavior; an older octamer estimate remains a historical conflict. Magnesium annotations are retained for EntC and EntB with their stated evidence limits. K-12 mutant physiology and reviewed K-12 proteins ground the taxon; native MG1655 assembly attribution does not relabel every experiment as MG1655. Upstream chorismate biosynthesis, downstream EntB carrier maturation/loading and enterobactin assembly, and the extra CueO-associated oxidation on the broader PathBank map are outside this independently defined pathway. Pairwise EntC–EntB interaction does not establish obligatory substrate channeling or a fixed multienzyme assembly.
