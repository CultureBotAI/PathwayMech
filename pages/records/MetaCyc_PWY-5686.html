UMP biosynthesis I

MetaCyc:PWY-5686

UMP biosynthesis I uses glutamine-dependent carbamoyl-phosphate synthase to form carbamoyl phosphate, condenses it with L-aspartate, cyclizes and oxidizes the pyrimidine ring to orotate, transfers a phosphoribosyl group to form orotidine 5'-phosphate, and decarboxylates that intermediate to UMP.
