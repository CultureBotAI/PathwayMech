phosphatidylcholine biosynthesis I

Phosphatidylcholine biosynthesis I in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C phosphorylates choline to choline phosphate, converts choline phosphate and CTP to CDP-choline, and transfers phosphocholine from CDP-choline to 1,2-diacyl-sn-glycerol to form 1,2-diacyl-sn-glycero-3-phosphocholine.
