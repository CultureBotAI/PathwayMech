ppGpp metabolism

MetaCyc:PPGPPMET-PWY

ppGpp metabolism interconverts GDP, GTP, guanosine 3'-diphosphate 5'-triphosphate, and guanosine 3',5'-bis(diphosphate) through kinase, pyrophosphokinase, and hydrolase reactions.
