inosine-5'-phosphate biosynthesis II

MetaCyc:PWY-6124

Inosine-5'-phosphate biosynthesis II carboxylates 5-amino-1-(5-phospho-beta-D-ribosyl)imidazole directly to CAIR, ligates the carboxylate to L-aspartate, releases fumarate to form AICAR, formylates AICAR with 10-formyltetrahydrofolate, and cyclizes FAICAR to IMP.
