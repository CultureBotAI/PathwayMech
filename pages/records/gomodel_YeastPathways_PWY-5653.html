NAD biosynthesis from 2-amino-3-carboxymuconate semialdehyde

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY-5653

NAD biosynthesis from 2-amino-3-carboxymuconate semialdehyde in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C cyclizes 2-amino-3-carboxymuconate-6-semialdehyde to quinolinic acid, converts quinolinic acid to nicotinate D-ribonucleotide, adenylates nicotinate D-ribonucleotide to deamido-NAD through NMA1 or NMA2 nicotinate-nucleotide adenylyltransferase activity, and amidates deamido-NAD with glutamine to form NAD.
