glutathione degradation

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWYQT-4432

Glutathione degradation uses ECM38 to transfer glutathione's gamma-glutamyl moiety to a proteinogenic amino acid, forming L-cysteinylglycine and an L-gamma-glutamyl-L-amino acid, then uses DUG1 to hydrolyze L-cysteinylglycine to L-cysteine and glycine in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C.
