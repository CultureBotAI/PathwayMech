galactose degradation

gomodel:YeastPathways_YEAST-GALACT-METAB-PWY

Galactose degradation in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C epimerizes beta-D-galactose to alpha-D-galactose, phosphorylates it, transfers UDP-glucose to form alpha-D-glucose 1-phosphate and UDP-galactose, regenerates UDP-glucose, and converts alpha-D-glucose 1-phosphate to D-glucose 6-phosphate in the SGD GO-CAM galactose degradation pathway.
