Purine Deoxyribonucleosides Degradation

PathwayMech / Browse pathways / PathBank:SMP0002089

PathBank:SMP0002089

The complete six-reaction PathBank pathway annotated here for Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 channels deoxyadenosine, deoxyinosine and deoxyguanosine into free purine bases, acetaldehyde and D-glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate. Add deaminates deoxyadenosine to deoxyinosine; the DeoD homohexamer provides three parallel phosphorolysis branches, including direct deoxyadenosine cleavage. DeoB transfers the deoxyribose phosphate from C1 to C5 and DeoC cleaves the resulting sugar phosphate. Degradation-directed Rhea reactions retain every chemical side and its exact charge and stereochemistry. This native boundary ends before acetaldehyde oxidation to acetyl-CoA and does not assert purine ring degradation, nucleoside uptake, or nucleotide salvage. MG1655 follows native component annotations; the reviewed proteins are K-12 entries, and the inspected enzyme studies do not establish whole-pathway flux in MG1655. DeoC's monomer/dimer states and Add's inferred zinc assignment remain explicitly qualified.
