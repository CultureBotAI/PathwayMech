L-arginine biosynthesis II (acetyl cycle)

gomodel:YeastPathways_ARGSYNBSUB-PWY

L-arginine biosynthesis II in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C uses ARG2, ARG56, ARG8, and ARG7 acetyl-cycle reactions to convert L-glutamate to L-ornithine, then uses CPX-579, ARG3, ARG1, and ARG4 to convert L-ornithine through L-citrulline and argininosuccinate to L-arginine.
