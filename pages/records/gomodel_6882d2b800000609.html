lipid IV(A) biosynthesis pathway

gomodel:6882d2b800000609

Lipid IV(A) biosynthesis converts UDP-N-acetyl-alpha-D-glucosamine to lipid IV(A) through UDP-3-O-acylated glucosamine, lipid X, and disaccharide intermediates in Escherichia coli K-12.
