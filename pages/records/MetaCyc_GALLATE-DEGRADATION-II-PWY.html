MetaCyc:GALLATE-DEGRADATION-II-PWY

Four-step aerobic gallate cleavage to pyruvate and oxaloacetate through keto-4-oxalomesaconate, the E,E enol and stereochemically unspecified carboxyhydroxyoxoadipate. The exact MetaCyc accession has suffix II but displays gallate degradation I. Formal reaction structures control the E,E assignment despite a conflicting Z,Z narrative link. KTGAL repaired GalT, KT2440 reporter constructs, historical native preparations and recombinant assays remain distinct. Source associations do not establish this complete route in every component organism. SYK-6 DesZ and LigAB oxygen-consumption assays show alternative gallate turnover without resolving the immediate product; PCA-specific LigAB regulator edges do not imply gallate-flux regulation.

Tested negative substrates are conditional assay negatives.
