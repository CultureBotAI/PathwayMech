siroheme biosynthesis

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY-5194-1

Siroheme biosynthesis in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C methylates uroporphyrinogen III to precorrin-2, oxidizes precorrin-2 to sirohydrochlorin, and inserts iron into sirohydrochlorin to form siroheme.
