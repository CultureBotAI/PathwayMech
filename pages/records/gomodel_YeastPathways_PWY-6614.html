tetrahydrofolate biosynthesis

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY-6614

tetrahydrofolate biosynthesis in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C converts 4-aminobenzoate and (7,8-dihydropterin-6-yl)methyl diphosphate to tetrahydrofolate through FOL1-catalyzed dihydropteroate synthase activity, FOL3-catalyzed dihydrofolate synthase activity, and DFR1-catalyzed dihydrofolate reductase activity.
