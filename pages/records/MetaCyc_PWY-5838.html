superpathway of menaquinol-8 biosynthesis I

MetaCyc:PWY-5838

The complete E. coli K-12 classical menaquinol-8 superpathway contains ten reactions: seven steps convert chorismate to 1,4-dihydroxy-2-naphthoate, IspB extends farnesyl diphosphate with five isopentenyl diphosphates, and MenA and UbiE prenylate and methylate the naphthalenediol to menaquinol-8. This is the full native PWY-5838 boundary, including its octaprenyl donor branch; upstream chorismate, IPP and farnesyl diphosphate synthesis and downstream respiratory redox cycling are separate. MenF is the dedicated isochorismate synthase. MenD forms SEPHCHC, MenH releases pyruvate, and MenI hydrolyzes DHNA-CoA; older assignments that collapsed these activities are superseded. K-12 protein annotation and MG1655 native frames are distinguished from primary experiments in specific K-12 derivatives, including XL1 Blue and BW25113. The quinol endpoint follows exact native reactions and current Rhea chemistry; older quinone extraction studies do not directly establish intracellular redox occupancy. Native MenE tetramer, reviewed homodimer by similarity and monomeric inhibitor-bound crystal assemblies disagree, so its edge expresses supported function without resolving assembly. MenC, MenA and UbiE functional edges likewise do not assert isolated-monomer sufficiency. UbiE membership in the ubiquinone metabolon is not transferred to this pathway. Magnesium and manganese alternatives for MenD and inferred IspB magnesium binding retain their evidence limits; bicarbonate is a supported MenB cofactor, not a stoichiometric substrate.
