asparagine degradation

gomodel:YeastPathways_ASPARAGINE-DEG2-PWY

Asparagine degradation hydrolyzes L-asparagine to L-aspartate and ammonium through ASP1/ASP3 activities and then transaminates L-aspartate to oxaloacetate through AAT1/AAT2 in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C.
