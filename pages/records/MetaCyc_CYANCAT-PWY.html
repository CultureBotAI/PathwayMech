MetaCyc:CYANCAT-PWY

The CynS/CynT bicarbonate-recycling route annotated for Escherichia coli K-12 MG1655 converts cyanate to ammonium and carbon dioxide. The cyanase homodecamer first forms carbamate from cyanate and bicarbonate; carbamate then decomposes spontaneously. Zinc-containing CynT hydrates carbon dioxide to replenish bicarbonate. The graph selects this physiological hydration direction of a reversible reaction. K-12 mutant experiments support the recycling role at 0.03% CO2, with rescue of the cynT deletion at 3% CO2; they do not establish an unconditional requirement for CynT. This record represents the explicit intermediate route, not an additional parallel net cyanase reaction.
