glutathione biosynthesis

MetaCyc:GLUTATHIONESYN-PWY

Glutathione biosynthesis ligates L-cysteine to L-glutamate with ATP to form gamma-L-glutamyl-L-cysteine, then ligates glycine to gamma-L-glutamyl-L-cysteine with a second ATP to form glutathione.
