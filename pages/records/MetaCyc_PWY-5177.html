glutaryl-CoA degradation

MetaCyc:PWY-5177

This source-native glutaryl-CoA degradation pathway converts glutaryl-CoA through glutaconyl-CoA, crotonyl-CoA, (S)-3-hydroxybutanoyl-CoA and acetoacetyl-CoA to two acetyl-CoA molecules. MetaCyc PWY-5177 defines five members and their order. Free-glutarate activation, alternative glutarate hydroxylation and downstream TCA/glyoxylate metabolism are outside these native pathway boundaries. All graph edges below preserve explicit structured source assertions; native evidence codes and cited papers are retained without treating unread citations as independently verified experiments.

The pathway-selected hydration is crotonyl-CoA + water -> (S)-3-hydroxybutanoyl-CoA. Its native reaction RXN-11667 is named '(S)-3-hydroxybutanoyl-CoA dehydrogenase'; that source name conflicts with the actual hydration/dehydration equation. The record preserves the authoritative node label and explains the chemistry here and in the incident edge descriptions. No redox reaction is inferred from that name.

The combined GLUTARYL-COA-DEHYDROGENASE-RXN equation formally equals the first two members, but the K172 and KT2440 GcdH activity annotations attach only to that composite. It is not a sixth pathway member, and neither catalyst is assigned to an isolated half-reaction.
