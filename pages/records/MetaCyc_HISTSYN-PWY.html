L-histidine biosynthesis

MetaCyc:HISTSYN-PWY

5-phospho-alpha-D-ribose 1-diphosphate and ATP are converted to L-histidine through phosphoribosyl-ATP, phosphoribosyl-AMP, phosphoribulosylformimino-AICAR-phosphate, imidazole-glycerol phosphate, imidazole acetol-phosphate, L-histidinol phosphate, L-histidinol, and L-histidinal.
