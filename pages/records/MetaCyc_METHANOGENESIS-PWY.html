MetaCyc:METHANOGENESIS-PWY

MetaCyc METHANOGENESIS-PWY defines six ordered reactions from CO2 to methyl-CoM, using methanofuran, tetrahydromethanopterin and coenzyme M carriers. The source label is retained, but methyl-CoM reduction to methane belongs to the linked METHFORM-PWY boundary and is not a seventh member. Carrier regeneration, methane formation and a complete energy-conserving system are outside this six-member record. Native pathway evidence tags are source metadata, not independent proof of the complete route.

The native Mtr equation specifies two Na+ on each side, from CCO-IN to CCO-OUT. CoM and methyl-THMPT inputs and Me-CoM and THMPT outputs are all explicitly inside; the other 24 material occurrences in the pathway have unspecified locations. Exact coefficients, source side, selected orientation and occurrence-specific locations are retained in incident descriptions/evidence and the research ledger. They do not establish newly measured sodium stoichiometry, a globally located sodium species, ATP yield or physiological flux. The Kurth2021 energy scheme is a model, not a purified Mtr transport assay.

The fourth native member is Hmd using H2, distinct from the F420-dependent Mtd alternative discussed in Kurth2021. Five generic reaction definitions are reversible; the root selects R2L Fmd, L2R Ftr, R2L Mch, L2R Hmd, R2L Mer and L2R Mtr. Exact native reaction frame labels are retained where their own records lack common names. The Hmd abstract (PMID:1521540) supplies bounded corroboration, not evidence for replacing Hmd with Mtd.

The root selects Mch dehydration, whereas native ENZRXN-662 and ENZRXN-4121 explicitly specify physiological L2R hydrolysis. Both activities remain exact molecular-activity nodes with their enzyme enables and regulation assertions; neither is assigned as a catalyst of the selected dehydration. The generic reaction remains reversible. PMID:3782039 and PMID:10215859 were read at abstract scope, without a new full-methods reconciliation of the opposing native directions.

Exact native pointers and the pinned NCBI Taxonomy 2026-07-12 label/rank statements support these identities. A species-slot placement does not turn Methanothermus into a species.
