MetaCyc:P321-PWY

Complete nine-reaction MetaCyc route from benzoyl-CoA through saturated cyclic intermediates and pimeloyl-CoA to glutaryl-CoA and acetyl-CoA. R. palustris genetics and BadK/BadH/BadI assays are distinguished from Thauera K172 BCR and Syntrophus SB reverse enzyme assays. Lower PimCD/F/B assignments remain predictions; step 8 is YES-PUTATIVE with no assigned enzyme. Native carrier equations and generic cluster units do not establish physiological ETF coupling or whole-protein stoichiometry. Listed taxa describe source and preparation contexts, not measured complete routes in every strain. BadR/BadM regulation refers to transcription. All three assayed acetamidobenzoic acid reagent effects on BadM DNA binding are retained with dose and solubility limits; the physiological ligand remains unresolved. The two BadL homologs have a separately scoped ortho acetylation activity, outside the nine selected pathway steps. Measured meta/para homolog acetylation remains explicit context because exact activity identifiers were not closed by bounded lookup.
