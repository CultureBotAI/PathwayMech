phosphatidate biosynthesis II (the glycerol-3-phosphate pathway)

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY3O-6499

Phosphatidate biosynthesis II in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C forms sn-glycerol 3-phosphate, acylates it to 1-acyl-sn-glycerol 3-phosphate, and acylates that lysophosphatidate to 1,2-diacyl-sn-glycerol 3-phosphate through the SGD GO-CAM glycerol-3-phosphate pathway.
