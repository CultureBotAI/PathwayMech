D-glucarate degradation I

MetaCyc:GLUCARDEG-PWY

D-glucarate degradation I dehydrates D-glucarate to 5-dehydro-4-deoxy-D-glucarate, cleaves that intermediate to 2-hydroxy-3-oxopropanoate and pyruvate, reduces 2-hydroxy-3-oxopropanoate to (R)-glycerate with NADPH or NADH, and phosphorylates (R)-glycerate to (2R)-2-phosphoglycerate.
