Entner-Doudoroff pathway, glucose-6P => glyceraldehyde-3P + pyruvate

PathwayMech / Browse pathways / KEGG:M00008

KEGG:M00008

Complete phosphorylative Entner–Doudoroff module from beta-D-glucose 6-phosphate to D-glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate and pyruvate, scoped to current E. coli K-12 proteins with experiment-specific strain and construct limits. Five reaction objects cover four sequential positions: alternative NADP- and NAD-dependent oxidations converge on lactone hydrolysis, dehydration and aldol cleavage. The beta identity is the native KEGG convention, not experimentally proven exclusive anomer use. First-step charged forms are explicit acid-base normalizations, and the two donor branches are never summed or assumed to carry equal in vivo flux. R10907 is listed by M00008 even though its inverse MODULE field omits M00008. Edd historical sequence and inferred iron-sulfur details remain qualified; Eda has source-supported homotrimer assembly. The CsrA Results/Fig.7 and Discussion report indirect positive expression effects, despite a negative section heading/current UniProt statement; no direct inhibition edge is inferred. Cofactor assays do not make Mg/Mn obligatory bound cofactors or LDH reporter NADH an Eda reactant. Uptake, precursor phosphorylation, downstream lower glycolysis, ATP production and other Eda substrate activities are outside this exact boundary.
