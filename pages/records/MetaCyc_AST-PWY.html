L-arginine degradation II (AST pathway)

MetaCyc:AST-PWY

The arginine succinyltransferase pathway converts L-arginine through N(2)-succinyl-L-arginine, N(2)-succinyl-L-ornithine, N-succinyl-L-glutamate 5-semialdehyde, and N-succinyl-L-glutamate to L-glutamate and succinate.

Pathway type
amino acid degradation
