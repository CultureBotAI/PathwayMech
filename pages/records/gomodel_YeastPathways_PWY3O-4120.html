tyrosine biosynthesis

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY3O-4120

Tyrosine biosynthesis in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C uses chorismate mutase, prephenate dehydrogenase, and aromatic-amino-acid transaminase activities to convert chorismate through prephenate and 3-(4-hydroxyphenyl)pyruvate to L-tyrosine.
