dolichyl phosphate D-mannose biosynthesis

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY3O-123

Dolichyl phosphate D-mannose biosynthesis in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C converts beta-D-fructofuranose 6-phosphate to alpha-D-mannose 1-phosphate, forms GDP-alpha-D-mannose, and transfers D-mannose to dolichyl phosphate to produce dolichyl D-mannosyl phosphate.

Pathway type
