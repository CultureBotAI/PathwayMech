4-aminobutyrate degradation

gomodel:YeastPathways_YEAST-4AMINOBUTMETAB-PWY

4-aminobutyrate degradation in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C transaminates gamma-aminobutyric acid to 4-oxobutanoate and oxidizes 4-oxobutanoate to succinate in the SGD GO-CAM 4-aminobutyrate degradation pathway.
