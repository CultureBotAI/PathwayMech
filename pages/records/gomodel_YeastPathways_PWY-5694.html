allantoin degradation to glyoxylate I

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY-5694

Allantoin degradation to glyoxylate I in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C hydrolyzes (S)-(+)-allantoin to allantoate, hydrolyzes allantoate to (-)-ureidoglycolate and urea, and cleaves (-)-ureidoglycolate to glyoxylate and urea.
