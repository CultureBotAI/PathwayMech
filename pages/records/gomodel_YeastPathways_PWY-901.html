methylglyoxal catabolism

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY-901

Methylglyoxal catabolism in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C converts methylglyoxal and glutathionate to (R)-S-lactoylglutathionate through GLO1 lactoylglutathione lyase activity, then hydrolyzes (R)-S-lactoylglutathionate to (R)-lactate and regenerates glutathionate through GLO2 and GLO4 hydroxyacylglutathione hydrolase activities.

Pathway type
detoxification
Organism scope
  • Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C NCBITaxon:559292
