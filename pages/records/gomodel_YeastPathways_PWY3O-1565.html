dolichyl glucosyl phosphate biosynthesis

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY3O-1565

Dolichyl glucosyl phosphate biosynthesis in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C converts D-glucopyranose 6-phosphate to alpha-D-glucose 1-phosphate through PGM1 or PGM2 phosphoglucomutase activity, couples alpha-D-glucose 1-phosphate with UTP through UGP1 or YHL012W to form UDP-alpha-D-glucose, and uses ALG5 to transfer glucose from UDP-alpha-D-glucose to dolichyl phosphate, producing dolichyl beta-D-glucosyl phosphate and UDP.

glycan biosynthesis
Organism scope
