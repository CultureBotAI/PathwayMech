L-tryptophan biosynthesis

MetaCyc:TRPSYN-PWY

L-tryptophan biosynthesis converts chorismate and L-glutamine to anthranilate, transfers a phosphoribosyl group from 5-phospho-alpha-D-ribose 1-diphosphate, isomerizes N-(5-phospho-beta-D-ribosyl)anthranilate to 1-(2-carboxyphenylamino)-1-deoxy-D-ribulose 5-phosphate, cyclizes that intermediate to (1S,2R)-1-C-(indol-3-yl)glycerol 3-phosphate, and condenses it with L-serine to make L-tryptophan.
