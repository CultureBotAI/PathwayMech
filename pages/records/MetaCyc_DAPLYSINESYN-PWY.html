L-lysine biosynthesis I

MetaCyc:DAPLYSINESYN-PWY

L-lysine biosynthesis I converts L-aspartate to L-lysine through L-aspartate 4-semialdehyde, cyclic tetrahydrodipicolinate intermediates, a succinylated diaminopimelate branch, and meso-2,6-diaminopimelate.
