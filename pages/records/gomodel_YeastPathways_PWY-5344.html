L-homocysteine biosynthesis

PathwayMech / Browse pathways / gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY-5344

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY-5344

L-homocysteine biosynthesis in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C uses MET2 to acetylate L-homoserine with acetyl-CoA, forming O-acetyl-L-homoserine and coenzyme A, then uses MET17 to condense O-acetyl-L-homoserine with hydrogen sulfide to produce L-homocysteine, acetate, and hydron.
