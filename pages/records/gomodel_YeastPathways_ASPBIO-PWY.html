aspartate biosynthesis

gomodel:YeastPathways_ASPBIO-PWY

Aspartate biosynthesis in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C uses PYC1 and PYC2 pyruvate carboxylase activities to carboxylate pyruvate to oxaloacetate and AAT1 and AAT2 L-aspartate:2-oxoglutarate transaminase activities to transaminate oxaloacetate and L-glutamate to L-aspartate and 2-oxoglutarate.
