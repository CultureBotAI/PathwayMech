fatty acid elongation

gomodel:YeastPathways_FASYN-ELONG2-PWY

Fatty acid elongation in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C uses CEM1, OAR1, FAS1, and FAS2 to condense and reduce acyl-carrier protein intermediates through 3-oxoacyl-[acp], (3R)-3-hydroxyacyl-[acyl-carrier-protein], enoyl-[acp], palmitoyl-[acp], and palmitoyl-CoA, then uses TES1 to hydrolyze palmitoyl-CoA to palmitate.
