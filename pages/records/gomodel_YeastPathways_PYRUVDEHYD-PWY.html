pyruvate decarboxylation to acetyl CoA

gomodel:YeastPathways_PYRUVDEHYD-PWY

Pyruvate decarboxylation to acetyl CoA in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C uses pyruvate dehydrogenase activities to transfer acetyl groups through lipoyl-lysine intermediates, form acetyl-CoA, and regenerate oxidized lipoyl cofactor while reducing NAD(+) to NADH.
