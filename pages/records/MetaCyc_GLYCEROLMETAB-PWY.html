glycerol degradation V

MetaCyc:GLYCEROLMETAB-PWY

glycerol degradation V oxidizes glycerol with NAD(+) to dihydroxyacetone, then phosphorylates dihydroxyacetone with phosphoenolpyruvate to form dihydroxyacetone phosphate and pyruvate.
