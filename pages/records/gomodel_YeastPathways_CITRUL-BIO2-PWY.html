citrulline biosynthesis

gomodel:YeastPathways_CITRUL-BIO2-PWY

Citrulline biosynthesis in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C uses CPX-579 carbamoyl-phosphate synthase to form carbamoyl phosphate from L-glutamine, hydrogencarbonate, water, and ATP, then ARG3 ornithine carbamoyltransferase transfers the carbamoyl group to L-ornithine to form L-citrulline in the SGD GO-CAM citrulline biosynthesis pathway.
