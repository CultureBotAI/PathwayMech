Dicarboxylate-hydroxybutyrate cycle

PathwayMech / Browse pathways / KEGG:M00374

KEGG:M00374

The complete 14-reaction dicarboxylate/4-hydroxybutyrate carbon-fixation cycle is represented for Thermoproteus neutrophilus DSM 2338, currently Pyrobaculum neutrophilum V24Sta. It regenerates acetyl-CoA while forming one net acetyl-CoA from inorganic carbon. All activities were measured in autotrophic-cell extracts; Tneu_0421, Tneu_0419, Tneu_0420 and Tneu_0249 were subsequently characterized as recombinant proteins. Other exact protein roles remain explicitly inferred. Four alternative pyruvate-synthase locus groups remain unresolved and are not assigned a catalyst. Native KEGG R01196 generic ferredoxin states avoid an unsupported cluster or protein identity: the selected pyruvate and CoA anions give a derived one-H+ input, rather than the native two-H+ equation using acid reference forms. This acid-base bookkeeping is not a measured proton stoichiometry or a whole-carrier formula balance. Fumarate reduction retains generic A/AH2 because reduced methyl viologen worked in vitro whereas NAD(P)H did not; native KEGG quinol chemistry is not transferred to this organism. T. neutrophilus succinyl-CoA reduction is NADPH-dependent, unlike the unresolved physiological donor of the I. hospitalis route. Current metal and assembly annotations retain their automated inference tiers. No purified Tneu_0541 fusion-protein experiment, native ligase acetylation regulation, particular PFOR complex, transporter or compartment is inferred from a different organism.
