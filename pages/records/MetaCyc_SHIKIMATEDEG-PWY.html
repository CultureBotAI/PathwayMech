shikimate degradation I

PathwayMech / Browse pathways / MetaCyc:SHIKIMATEDEG-PWY

MetaCyc:SHIKIMATEDEG-PWY

The two-step MetaCyc 27.0 shikimate degradation I definition converts shikimate through 3-dehydroshikimate to protocatechuate. The initial oxidation retains the source-defined quinone and quinol classes, with PQQ as a separate enzyme cofactor. This is distinct from the NAD-dependent shikimate II route and the three-step quinate I route. Enzyme and regulatory evidence retains organism, preparation and inference scope; the record does not assert a purified two-enzyme reconstruction for every source-listed organism.
