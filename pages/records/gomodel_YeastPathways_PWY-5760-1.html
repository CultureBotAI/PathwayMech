beta-alanine biosynthesis IV

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY-5760-1

Beta-alanine biosynthesis IV in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C oxidizes spermine to spermidine, 3-ammoniopropanal, and hydrogen peroxide, then oxidizes 3-ammoniopropanal to beta-alanine through ALD2 or ALD3 aldehyde dehydrogenase activity.
