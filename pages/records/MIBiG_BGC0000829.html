Chromobacterium violaceum violacein biosynthetic gene cluster

MIBiG:BGC0000829

Literature-refined cluster scope from C. violaceum JCM 1249 (ATCC 12472), using the experimentally studied AB032799.1 locus. The active MIBiG entry points to AF172851.1; retired BGC0000828 points to AB032799.1 and explicitly merges into BGC0000829. The four AB proteins differ from the AF proteins and are not equated. VioE is independently sequence-resolved in the unannotated AB locus region. This graph records five gene-product contributions and observed violacein output, including subsequent spontaneous chemistry; it does not assign all final chemistry to enzymes or assert elementary reactions. Native MIBiG quality remains questionable.
