L-lysine biosynthesis IV

gomodel:YeastPathways_LYSINE-AMINOAD-PWY-2

L-lysine biosynthesis IV in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C routes acetyl-CoA and 2-oxoglutarate through homocitrate, homoaconitate, 2-oxoadipate, 2-aminoadipate, saccharopine, and L-2-aminoadipate 6-semialdehyde chemistry to L-lysine. Homocitrate dehydration is assigned to ACO2 with a minor ACO1 contribution; LYS4 catalyzes the following homoaconitate hydration.
