tryptophan degradation

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY3O-214

Tryptophan degradation in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C uses ARO8 or ARO9 aminotransferase activity to form 3-(indol-3-yl)pyruvate from L-tryptophan, decarboxylates 3-(indol-3-yl)pyruvate through ARO10, PDC1, PDC5, or PDC6 indolepyruvate decarboxylase activity to indol-3-ylacetaldehyde and carbon dioxide, and reduces indol-3-ylacetaldehyde with NADH through ADH1, ADH2, ADH3, ADH4, ADH5, or SFA1 indole-3-acetaldehyde reductase activity to tryptophol and NAD.
