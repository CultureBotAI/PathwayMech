D-galactonate degradation

PathwayMech / Browse pathways / MetaCyc:GALACTCAT-PWY

MetaCyc:GALACTCAT-PWY

The complete three-step MetaCyc D-galactonate pathway dehydrates D-galactonate, phosphorylates the resulting 2-dehydro-3-deoxy-D-galactonate with ATP, and cleaves the phosphorylated intermediate to D-glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate and pyruvate. The scope is E. coli K-12: native protein annotations refer to MG1655, while modern growth and transcription experiments used BW25113 derivatives. Current reviewed DgoD and DgoA sequences correct an older predicted gene fusion. DgoD and DgoK catalytic assignments are retained as reviewed annotations supported by native pathway curation and genetic physiology; DgoA also has purified-enzyme and crystal evidence. Functional enzyme edges do not imply isolated-monomer sufficiency: DgoA is homotrimeric. DgoD magnesium binding is annotated by similarity. DgoR represses the dgo operon, and D-galactonate relieves its DNA binding under the tested conditions. Later recombinant-protein work supports a divalent-metal requirement for effector binding without identifying a unique physiological metal. The selected aldolase direction is catabolic despite its reversible native reaction. Uptake, upstream D-galactose oxidation, galactitol utilization and downstream glyceraldehyde-phosphate metabolism lie outside this exact pathway boundary.
