L-asparagine biosynthesis I

gomodel:YeastPathways_ASPARAGINE-BIOSYNTHESIS-1

L-asparagine biosynthesis I aminates oxaloacetate to L-aspartate and then amidates L-aspartate to L-asparagine through paired AAT1/AAT2 and ASN1/ASN2 activities in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C.
