MetaCyc:CALVIN-PWY

Complete 13-reaction native Calvin–Benson–Bassham cycle, with reduction and full pentose regeneration, scoped to Synechocystis PCC 6803 and separately identified current Kazusa protein annotations. The exact native identity is retained rather than equating the plant-oriented GO:0019253 definition. Rhea charged forms refine native chemical classes, without class-wide exactMatch. Weighted closure fixes three CO2 into one glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate using nine ATP and six NADPH; these are formal stoichiometric weights, not measured culture fluxes. Both aldolase activities, both transketolase activities and both functions of the bifunctional phosphatase are retained. Aldolase isoforms and the additional FBPase are alternatives, not serial requirements. Native parent-strain assemblies are connected to Kazusa accessions only through explicit database component mappings, not assumed culture identity. The erroneous native TPI link to mouse Q95554 is rejected; Q59994 has independent inferred activity evidence. Native TKTASYN and current P73282 remain separately scoped annotations without an asserted equivalence. The 2023 L-erythrulose surrogate-donor assay is not evidence for either canonical transketolase substrate pair. Sequence-matched synthetic CP12 rescue supports conditional system-level regulation in GT PCC6803, not a purified inhibition assay or counted ternary complex. Metal annotations and assay metals remain distinct; AMP-dependent inactive phosphatase assemblies are not treated as invariant active complexes. Carbon uptake, light reactions, photorespiration and biomass synthesis lie outside the native 13-member boundary.
