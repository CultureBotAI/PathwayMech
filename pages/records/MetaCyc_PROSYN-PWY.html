L-proline biosynthesis I (from L-glutamate)

L-proline biosynthesis I phosphorylates L-glutamate to L-glutamyl 5-phosphate, reduces it to L-glutamate 5-semialdehyde, cyclizes that intermediate to (S)-1-pyrroline-5-carboxylate, and reduces it to L-proline.
