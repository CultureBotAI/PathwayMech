ergothioneine biosynthesis I (bacteria)

MetaCyc:PWY-7255

The complete seven-member native pathway combines gamma-glutamylcysteine synthesis, histidine trimethylation, oxygen-dependent C-S coupling, deglutamylation, PLP-dependent cleavage, generic reduction and thiol-thione equilibration. The five named gene products are current Mycolicibacterium smegmatis mc(2)155 proteins; EgtA activity is inferred by similarity, and the early B/C/D reconstitution used DSM43465/ATCC607. Later EgtC and EgtE studies directly identify mc(2)155 constructs. Functional enables assertions retain EgtC tetramer and EgtE homodimer contexts without inventing complex identifiers or monomer sufficiency. Current Rhea defines the first five equations; the terminal native equations retain neutral ergothioneine thiol and thione as separate states. Current Rhea reduction instead uses protonated ergothioneine and an added proton and is not substituted into the neutral tautomer sequence. The AH2/A pair remains generic: DTT-supported experiments do not identify the physiological reductant. Sulfoxide sulfur stereochemistry remains unspecified in the selected authoritative chemical classes, not a claim that every stereoisomer is accepted. Fungal bypasses, disproportionation side products, transport, antioxidant outcomes and precursor biosynthesis are outside this complete native boundary. The 2018 purified EgtD product-inhibition observation (DOI:10.1021/acschembio.8b00127.s001) is retained as DSM43465/ATCC607-derived engineered-preparation context only; it is not transferred to the exact mc(2)155 protein.
