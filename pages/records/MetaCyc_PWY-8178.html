MetaCyc:PWY-8178

The native MetaCyc PWY-8178 boundary contains six reversible reactions: ribose-phosphate isomerase, ribulose-phosphate epimerase, two transketolase events, PPi-dependent S7P phosphorylation and sedoheptulose-bisphosphate cleavage. The displayed pentose-to-hexose layout reverses the native left/right direction of RIB5PISOM-RXN and 1TRANSKETO-RXN. RXN-21730 consumes S7P and PPi and releases SBP, phosphate and a proton; PPi is a consumed donor, not merely an activating cofactor. The source supplies twenty side occurrences but no explicit coefficient fields. Generic S7P and R5P classes, beta-D-fructofuranose6-phosphate and the source's distinct ionic identities are retained. The native PPI cross-reference to neutral diphosphoric acid conflicts with its charged native structure; no neutral-acid/anion exactMatch is asserted. Shared Rhea chemistry for the other five events does not establish a Rhea identity for the PPi-S7P event or narrow the native classes.

Entamoeba histolytica 200:NIH provides historical activity-level support. The 1982 study used axenic TP-S-1 culture and partially purified native transketolase, PPi-PFK and aldolase; it did not identify a modern protein sequence, tag or oligomer. Transketolase apoenzyme requires both Mg2+ and thiamine diphosphate, with a lag when Mg is added first. Its two substrate-pair assays had apparent Km values dependent on the fixed cosubstrate: R5P0.027mM with Xu5P0.3mM, Xu5P0.23mM with R5P2mM, GAP1.60mM with F6P12mM, and E4P0.005mM with Xu5P0.1mM. The partial PFK preparation has apparent S7P Km64micromolar and PPi Km about10micromolar; the cited F6P comparator is38micromolar. Partial aldolase cleaves SBP with Km13micromolar versus FBP3micromolar and about half the FBP Vmax. Slight PFK contamination is disclosed; added triose-phosphate isomerase did not increase SBP cleavage. Reconstitution requires transketolase, PFK, aldolase and PPi with omission controls; yeast aldolase can substitute after PFK is supplied. Extract R5P consumption was8.1±1.7nmol/min/mg in four preparations, with triose0.46±0.04 and S7P0.40±0.04 per R5P, accounting for86% of substrate. These results support the route without assigning a present-day Entamoeba sequence. Reverse biosynthetic flux and retention of PPi energy were proposed, not all measured directly.

Modern clostridial work separates species, strains and recombinant preparations. Thermosuccinogenes CDQ83_11320=PNT94038.1 and CDQ83_07225=PNT93300.1 are issued DSM5809 sequences; the metabolomics cultures are not silently equated to every issuing genome preparation. DSM1313 Clo1313_1876=ADU74928.1 is a separate PPi-PFK. In 2019 the pET28b constructs carried N-terminal His6 flanking a TEV site, without documented tag cleavage. Heat-treated Rosetta extracts assayed at55C gave CDQ83_11320 PPi-dependent F6P activity220±26U/mg CFE, with ATP/GTP activity not detected; CDQ83_07225 gave ATP13±1 and GTP13±5 with PPi not detected. CDQ83_10650 remained inactive in the tested heated and nonheated preparations. These are CFE measurements, not equal expression or purified kcat. CDQ83_07225 donor Km values differed: ATP0.155mM and GTP0.016mM. The 2019 Discussion mistakenly calls CDQ83_11320 PPdK; Tables2/3 identify PPdK as CDQ83_07455.

The 2020 study used pET28b+/Rosetta for CDQ83 proteins and DSM1313 PCR into pD861-NT/pSH157 in E. coli T7 Express lysY/Iq for Clo1313_1876. Direct55C MS showed S7P-to-SBP conversion with PPi, alongside minus-PPi and minus-enzyme controls. The MS assay contained5mM MgCl2,5mM sodium PPi and1mM acceptor; kinetic assays used1mM PPi. Mg presence is an assay condition, not an independently measured universal metal requirement. Rabbit aldolase coupling supplies E4P/DHAP; it does not demonstrate activity of a clostridial aldolase. Unequal triose signals led to a TPI-contamination hypothesis, not a new core reaction. NADH/GPDH and other auxiliary enzymes belong to the assay, not core stoichiometry. Purified CDQ83_11320 has S7P Km0.11mM, kcat49/s and F6P0.070mM,182/s; Clo1313_1876 has S7P0.10mM,80/s and F6P0.046mM,127/s. CDQ83_07225 has no detected S7P activity through4mM and a F6P Khalf0.688mM with Hill coefficient24, not a Michaelis Km. Its ammonium requirement belongs to the ATP/F6P comparator using lyophilized rabbit aldolase/GPDH in place of ammonium-sulfate suspensions, because S7P Ba-salt precipitated BaSO4. Ultrafiltration applied to purified PFK buffer exchange, not the auxiliary enzymes; the source's NH4Cl2 formula is inconsistent. E. coli PfkA, its0.25mM ADP assay addition and delta-talAB physiology are ATP-route comparisons, not an E. coli PPi-branch assignment.

The thesis-version supplemental figures reproduce the minus-enzyme control and separate Michaelis/Hill fits. Hill fits for PPi enzymes are not uniformly one; the very steep CDQ83_07225 fit and poor alternative Michaelis fit must not be confused. This thesis supplement is not asserted byte-identical to a journal supplementary PDF. Thermosuccinogenes SBP and S7P pools were about fourfold and2.5-fold higher on xylose than glucose in biological triplicates normalized to OD600. Pool accumulation does not establish six-event flux, direct xylose binding or transcriptional regulation.

The 2023 study tests ATCC27405=DSM1237 Cthe_0347 Pfp=ABN51585.1 and Cthe_0349 Aldoa=ABN51587.1, distinct from DSM1313. Aldoa uses a synthetic codon-optimized pRSET-A construct with N-terminal His6/linker; Pfp uses pAHCT0347/pRSET-A with an N-terminal His6 tag. Both were expressed in E. coli T7 Shuffle. The inspected Pfp-specific passages do not establish a linker or codon optimization. Ni purification does not identify physiological nickel use. Pfp forward MS at50C used2mM PPi,2.115mM S7P and2.5mM MgCl2. Reported S7P Km3.236mM exceeds the highest measured2.12mM; Vmax is6.294±3.723micromol/min/mg. The F6P Km0.075mM and Vmax100.222±11.6 were measured separately. This preparation/strain/assay difference from the2020 values remains unresolved. Mixed S7P/F6P lowered FBP formation, consistent with competition in that assay, without a direct binding constant. Only forward S7P phosphorylation was tested.

The original Taillefer thesis adds Cthe_0347 F6P-assay context.
