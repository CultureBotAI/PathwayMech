curcumin catabolic process

PathwayMech / Browse pathways / GO:0036040

GO:0036040

The complete two-step reductive curcumin pathway converts curcumin through dihydrocurcumin to tetrahydrocurcumin using the NADPH-dependent CurA homodimer. The graph follows MetaCyc PWY0-1527 and the two native reactions in PathBank SMP0001910. The primary study first found activity in a fecal Escherichia coli isolate, then used K-12 DH10B for purification and gene identification and a recombinant BL21 host for detailed enzyme characterization. Reviewed K-12 CurA and the native MG1655 annotation support the selected K-12 scope; this is not a direct MG1655 flux measurement. Both C=C reductions and their shared intermediate are retained. The redundant net Rhea reaction is excluded. Tetrahydrocurcumin is the demonstrated CurA endpoint, without an assertion of complete mineralization, downstream fate, or a curcumin transporter. NADPH is a diffusible cosubstrate; the reported enzyme is metal-independent. Cytosol localization is retained only as an explicitly computational native annotation.
