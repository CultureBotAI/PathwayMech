methylthiopropanoate degradation I (cleavage)

PathwayMech / Browse pathways / MetaCyc:PWY-6048

MetaCyc:PWY-6048

The complete three-reaction MetaCyc route converts 3-(methylthio)propionate (MMPA) to methanethiol, acetaldehyde and carbon dioxide in Ruegeria pomeroyi DSS-3. DmdB2 activates MMPA with ATP and CoA; DmdC1 oxidizes the thioester to trans-methylthioacryloyl-CoA; DmdD-dependent hydration and hydrolysis complete cleavage. The terminal net reaction includes spontaneous methanethiol elimination and expected spontaneous decarboxylation. Generic electron-transfer flavoprotein redox states retain the MetaCyc/Rhea database convention: the physiological partner remains unidentified, and direct DmdC1 assays used artificial acceptors. DmdB2 and DmdC1 are experimentally supported homodimers and DmdD a homohexamer; protein enables edges represent their functional roles without asserting monomer sufficiency. DmdB magnesium dependence and DmdC inferred FAD cofactor are distinguished from the whole carrier. DMSP-dependent DmdB2 inhibition and its conditional relief by MMPA are supported in vitro. The route starts after DMSP demethylation and stops before acetaldehyde oxidation or methanethiol assimilation; it does not assert these adjacent pathways or flux in every environmental condition.
