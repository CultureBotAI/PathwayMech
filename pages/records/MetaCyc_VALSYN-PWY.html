L-valine biosynthesis

MetaCyc:VALSYN-PWY

L-valine biosynthesis condenses pyruvate to (2S)-2-acetolactate, reduces and rearranges it to (2R)-2,3-dihydroxy-3-methylbutanoate, dehydrates that intermediate to 3-methyl-2-oxobutanoate, and transaminates it to L-valine.
