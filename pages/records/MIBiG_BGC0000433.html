MIBiG:BGC0000433

Surfactin biosynthetic gene cluster of Bacillus velezensis FZB42, called Bacillus amyloliquefaciens FZB42 in the original study. This bounded graph preserves three annotated NRPS components and cluster-level genetic evidence; it does not assert that each component independently synthesizes surfactin. MIBiG reports active status, questionable quality and complete locus coverage. The graph is partial. Module substrate assignments are structure-based inferences and are not promoted to measured reaction steps. The native locus coordinates 0..0 are unspecified and are omitted rather than treated as bounds.
