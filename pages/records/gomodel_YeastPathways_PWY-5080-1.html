very long chain fatty acid biosynthesis I

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY-5080-1

The very long chain fatty acid biosynthesis I GO-CAM for Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C links ELO2/ELO3 fatty acid elongase, IFA38 acetoacetyl-CoA reductase, PHS1 enoyl-CoA hydratase, and TSC13 very-long-chain enoyl-CoA reductase activities that elongate long-chain fatty acyl-CoA through 3-oxo, 3-hydroxy, and trans-2-enoyl-CoA intermediates.
