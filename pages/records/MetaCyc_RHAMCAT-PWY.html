L-rhamnose degradation I

PathwayMech / Browse pathways / MetaCyc:RHAMCAT-PWY

MetaCyc:RHAMCAT-PWY

L-rhamnose degradation I converts alpha-L-rhamnose to beta-L-rhamnose, isomerizes L-rhamnopyranose to L-rhamnulose, cyclizes keto-L-rhamnulose to L-rhamnulofuranose, phosphorylates L-rhamnulose with ATP, and cleaves L-rhamnulose 1-phosphate to (S)-lactaldehyde and dihydroxyacetone phosphate.

Pathway type
carbohydrate degradation
Organism scope
