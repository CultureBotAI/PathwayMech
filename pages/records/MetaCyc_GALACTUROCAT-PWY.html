D-galacturonate degradation I

MetaCyc:GALACTUROCAT-PWY

D-galacturonate degradation I isomerizes aldehydo-D-galacturonate to keto-D-tagaturonate, reduces it to D-altronate, dehydrates and phosphorylates it to 2-dehydro-3-deoxy-6-phospho-D-gluconate, and cleaves that phosphorylated intermediate to D-glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate and pyruvate.

Pathway type
carbohydrate degradation
