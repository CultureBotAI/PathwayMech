5-aminoimidazole ribonucleotide biosynthesis II

PathwayMech / Browse pathways / MetaCyc:PWY-6122

MetaCyc:PWY-6122

5-Aminoimidazole ribonucleotide biosynthesis II converts 5-phospho-alpha-D-ribose 1-diphosphate to 5-amino-1-(5-phospho-beta-D-ribosyl)imidazole through 5-phospho-beta-D-ribosylamine, glycinamide ribonucleotide, ATP- and formate-dependent formylation to FGAR, and ATP-dependent amidine formation and cyclization.
