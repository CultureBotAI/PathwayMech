spermidine biosynthesis I

gomodel:YeastPathways_BSUBPOLYAMSYN-PWY

Spermidine biosynthesis I decarboxylates S-adenosyl-L-methionine through SPE2 and transfers its aminopropyl group to 1,4-butanediammonium through SPE3 to form spermidine in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C.

Pathway type
polyamine biosynthesis
