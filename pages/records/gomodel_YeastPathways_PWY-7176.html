UTP and CTP de novo biosynthesis

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY-7176

UTP and CTP de novo biosynthesis in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C converts UMP to UDP via URA6-catalyzed UMP kinase activity, phosphorylates UDP to UTP through YNK1-catalyzed nucleoside diphosphate kinase activity, and aminates UTP to CTP through URA8- or URA7-catalyzed CTP synthase activity.
