inositol phosphate biosynthesis

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY3O-402

Inositol phosphate biosynthesis in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C converts phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate and soluble inositol phosphates through inositol tetrakisphosphate, inositol pentakisphosphate, inositol hexakisphosphate, and inositol pyrophosphate intermediates. The source includes the additional diphosphoinositol pentakisphosphate phosphorylation step without assigning its enabling protein.
