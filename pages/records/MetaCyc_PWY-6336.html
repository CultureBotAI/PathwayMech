protocatechuate degradation III (para-cleavage pathway)

PathwayMech / Browse pathways / MetaCyc:PWY-6336

MetaCyc:PWY-6336

The complete five-step protocatechuate 2,3-cleavage route in Paenibacillus sp. JJ-1b proceeds through the Z,Z carboxysemialdehyde, hydroxymuconate semialdehyde, hydroxymuconate and oxalocrotonate to the Z-enol hydroxypentadienoate. It connects to the separate downstream degradation pathway. The five individual recombinant enzymes are assayed as crude extracts; their endpoint qualifications retain construct uncertainty. The historical native dioxygenase assembly and iron annotation remain distinct from those recombinant preparations. Spontaneous intermediate conversion is compatible with enzyme acceleration. NAD is the selected reaction coenzyme; lower NADP-supported PraB activity is retained separately. Upstream 4-hydroxybenzoate conversion and the downstream three-enzyme module are outside the internal five-step boundary.
