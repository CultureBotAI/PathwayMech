quinate degradation II

PathwayMech / Browse pathways / MetaCyc:PWY-6416

MetaCyc:PWY-6416

The MetaCyc 27.0 quinate degradation II definition converts L-quinate through 3-dehydroquinate and 3-dehydroshikimate to protocatechuate. Its first reaction retains the native NAD(P) and NAD(P)H classes. Individual enzyme evidence distinguishes NAD preference, assay constructs, native assemblies and species or strain scope. This three-step route is separate from the PQQ-dependent quinate degradation I and the two-step shikimate degradation routes. The separately displayed NAD-specific reaction is a molecular realization of the first step, not an additional sequential fourth step.
