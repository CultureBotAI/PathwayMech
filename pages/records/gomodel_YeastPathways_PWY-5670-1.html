epoxysqualene biosynthesis

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY-5670-1

Epoxysqualene biosynthesis in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C converts farnesyl diphosphate to presqualene diphosphate, reduces presqualene diphosphate to squalene, and monooxygenates squalene to form (S)-2,3-epoxysqualene.
