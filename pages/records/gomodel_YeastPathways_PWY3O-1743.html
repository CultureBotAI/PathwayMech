mannose degradation

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY3O-1743

Mannose degradation in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C phosphorylates D-mannopyranose with ATP through GLK1 glucokinase or HXK1/HXK2 hexokinase activity to produce D-mannopyranose 6-phosphate, ADP, and hydron, and converts D-mannopyranose 6-phosphate through PMI40 mannose-6-phosphate isomerase activity to beta-D-fructofuranose 6-phosphate.
