phospholipid biosynthesis II (Kennedy pathway)

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY3O-259

Phospholipid biosynthesis II (Kennedy pathway) in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C phosphorylates ethanolaminium to O-phosphonatoethanaminium, converts that intermediate and CTP to CDP-ethanolamine, and transfers phosphoethanolamine from CDP-ethanolamine to 1,2-diacyl-sn-glycerol to form phosphatidylethanolamine.

Pathway type
lipid biosynthesis

  • Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C NCBITaxon:559292
