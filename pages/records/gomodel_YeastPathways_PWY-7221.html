guanosine ribonucleotides de novo biosynthesis

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY-7221

Guanosine ribonucleotides de novo biosynthesis in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C oxidizes IMP to xanthosine 5-monophosphate through IMD2, IMD3, and IMD4 IMP dehydrogenase activity, aminates xanthosine 5-monophosphate to GMP, and phosphorylates GMP and GDP to form GTP.
