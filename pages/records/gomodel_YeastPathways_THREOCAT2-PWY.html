threonine degradation

gomodel:YeastPathways_THREOCAT2-PWY

Threonine degradation in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C includes CHA1-enabled threonine deamination to 2-oxobutanoate and the GLY1 threonine aldolase route to glycine and acetaldehyde. The broader native outline contains unassigned or neighbouring reactions; KBL is absent and threonine dehydrogenase is uncertain in yeast. An unsupported reverse cystathionine-condensation branch is excluded.
