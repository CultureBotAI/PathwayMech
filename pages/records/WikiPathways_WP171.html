NAD salvage pathway V

WikiPathways:WP171

NAD salvage pathway V in Saccharomyces cerevisiae converts nicotinamide through nicotinate, beta-nicotinate D-ribonucleotide, and nicotinate adenine dinucleotide to regenerate NAD in the WP171 GPML diagram.
