coenzyme A biosynthesis I (prokaryotic)

MetaCyc:COA-PWY

The prokaryotic coenzyme A biosynthesis I pathway converts (R)-4'-phosphopantothenate to CoA through phosphopantothenoyl-L-cysteine, 4'-phosphopantetheine, and 3'-dephospho-CoA intermediates.
