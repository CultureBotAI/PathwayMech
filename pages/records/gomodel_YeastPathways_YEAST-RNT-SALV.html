salvage pathways of pyrimidine ribonucleotides

gomodel:YeastPathways_YEAST-RNT-SALV

Salvage pathways of pyrimidine ribonucleotides in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C recover uridine, cytidine, cytosine, and uracil through nucleosidase, deaminase, kinase, and uracil phosphoribosyltransferase reactions to regenerate UMP, CMP, CDP, UDP, CTP, and UTP in the SGD GO-CAM pyrimidine ribonucleotide salvage pathway.
