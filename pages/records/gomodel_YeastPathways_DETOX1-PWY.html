superoxide radicals degradation

gomodel:YeastPathways_DETOX1-PWY

Superoxide radicals degradation in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C uses SOD1 and SOD2 superoxide dismutase activities to convert superoxide and hydrons into dioxygen and hydrogen peroxide, then CTA1 and CTT1 catalase activities decompose hydrogen peroxide into water and dioxygen.
