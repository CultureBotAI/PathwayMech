pentose phosphate pathway (non-oxidative branch) I

MetaCyc:NONOXIPENT-PWY

The non-oxidative branch of the pentose phosphate pathway reversibly interconverts D-ribulose 5-phosphate, aldehydo-D-ribose 5-phosphate, and D-xylulose 5-phosphate with D-sedoheptulose 7-phosphate, D-erythrose 4-phosphate, beta-D-fructose 6-phosphate, and D-glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate through isomerase, epimerase, transketolase, and transaldolase reactions.
