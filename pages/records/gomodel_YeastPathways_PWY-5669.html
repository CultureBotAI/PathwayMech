phosphatidylethanolamine biosynthesis I

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY-5669

Phosphatidylethanolamine biosynthesis I in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C transfers phosphatidyl groups from CDP-diacylglycerol to L-serine to form 3-sn-phosphatidyl-L-serine, then decarboxylates 3-sn-phosphatidyl-L-serine through PSD1 or PSD2 to form phosphatidylethanolamine.
