benzoyl-CoA degradation I (aerobic)

PathwayMech / Browse pathways / MetaCyc:PWY-1361

MetaCyc:PWY-1361

The complete source-defined seven-step aerobic Box route converts benzoyl-CoA to acetyl-CoA and succinyl-CoA. The upper BoxAB epoxidase, BoxC dihydrolase and BoxD semialdehyde dehydrogenase are supported with their actual experimental preparations. The 2010 epoxide result supersedes historical dihydrodiol assignments; proposed oxepin intermediates and predicted absolute epoxide stereochemistry are not established products. The fourth isomerization is explicitly putative and has no assigned catalyst. Lower hydration, NAD-dependent oxidation and thiolysis preserve native reaction chemistry and ordering without transferring catalysts from phenylacetate, engineered reverse-adipate, or expression-only studies. Native taxon annotations include Aromatoleum evansii, Geobacillus stearothermophilus and Paraburkholderia xenovorans LB400; they do not establish every reaction experimentally in each taxon. Azoarcus sp. CIB is an additional, separately issued regulatory context: BoxR/BzdR repress box-gene transcription, and benzoyl-CoA relieves tagged BoxR DNA binding in crude-extract assays. Those findings are not transferred to A. evansii. Complex membership is stored composition, not proof of stable assembly. FAD is normalized to CHEBI:57692; CPD-7 remains a generic iron-sulfur cofactor class, without fixed occupancy or ligation. Benzoate-CoA ligase lies upstream of this seven-step boundary. CIB bclA insertion-disruption growth loss was reported as data not shown; it is contextual support, not an eighth reaction or a complete seven-step enzyme assay. The originals print DSMZ 6869 for KB740 in some studies, whereas issuing taxonomy/UniProt use DSM 6898; this discrepancy is retained, not silently harmonized. PMID11306574, PMID15916608, PMID15458418 and PMID18462753 remain full-original access gaps; complete retained abstracts and independently accepted later originals are distinguished in the evidence ledger.
