ergosterol biosynthesis I

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY-6075-1

Ergosterol biosynthesis I in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C converts zymosterol to ergosterol through ERG6, ERG2, ERG3, ERG5, and ERG4 sterol methyltransferase, isomerase, desaturase, and reductase activities.
