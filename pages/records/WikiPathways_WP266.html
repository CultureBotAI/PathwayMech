Triglyceride biosynthesis

WikiPathways:WP266

Triglyceride biosynthesis converts sn-glycerol 3-phosphate to 1-acyl-sn-glycerol 3-phosphate, 1,2-diacyl-sn-glycerol 3-phosphate, 1,2-diacyl-sn-glycerol, and triacyl-sn-glycerol in Saccharomyces cerevisiae.
