pyochelin biosynthetic process

GO:0042864

The complete eleven-object MetaCyc PWY-6408 thiotemplate pathway is represented from supplied salicylate and two L-cysteines through pyochelin release. The PAO1 implementation uses PchD, PchE, PchF and PchG; it does not include upstream PchA/PchB salicylate synthesis. The shared cysteine-adenylation reaction turns twice per formal product. Carrier-class coefficients count reactive sites, not whole polypeptides: PchE has distinct aryl and peptidyl carrier domains on one chain. The three-ATP total is a stoichiometric derivation, not a measured yield. Native CPD-9992 specifies the pyochelin I monoanion, one member of the experimentally observed interconverting I/II products. Its use preserves a source-defined I projection; it does not assert exclusive I production, identity with the neutral I/II class, or a separate experimentally resolved release stereoselectivity. Native carrier names do not specify every stereocenter. PchE epimerization occurs after amide formation; its coupled cyclization step retains that scope without adding an unsupported separate reaction. Direct 2022 PAO1 structural work observes a PchE homodimer and bound magnesium; functional enables edges do not assert monomer sufficiency or a stable PchDEFG heterotetramer. The 1999 deposited PchD/E/F sequences are from PA14 and differ from current PAO1; the older PAO1 PchF submission also differs at six residues. Later PAO1 source and construct evidence is distinguished from exact engineered-sequence identity. Current PchD and some PchE/F mechanistic annotations retain similarity or curator inference. PchG is an unreviewed current entry, supported separately by PAO1 genetics and biochemical work; free-analogue measurements are not full carrier-substrate kinetics. PchC editing, transport, iron loading and regulation are outside this native assembly boundary.
