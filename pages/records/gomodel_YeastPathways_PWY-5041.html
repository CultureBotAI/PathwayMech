S-adenosyl-L-methionine cycle II

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY-5041

The SGD S-adenosyl-L-methionine cycle II GO-CAM for Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C models MET6-enabled 5-methyltetrahydropteroyltriglutamate-homocysteine S-methyltransferase, SAM1- and SAM2-enabled methionine adenosyltransferase, and SAH1-enabled adenosylhomocysteinase activities. The source also models the generic SAM-dependent methyl transfer step without assigning an enabling protein.
