Cysteine synthesis from O-phosphoserine

Reactome:R-MTU-936654

The CysM-CysO route in Mycobacterium tuberculosis transfers O-phosphoserine onto thiocarboxylated CysO, then Mec releases L-cysteine and regenerates the carrier. The graph begins at CysO-COSH: the upstream MoeZ sulfur-charging step is excluded because its physiological sulfur donor is unresolved. Carrier modification states, PLP-bearing CysM and the source zinc-containing, cleaved Mec assembly remain distinct.
