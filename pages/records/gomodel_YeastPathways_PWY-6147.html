6-hydroxymethyl-dihydropterin diphosphate biosynthesis I

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY-6147

6-hydroxymethyl-dihydropterin diphosphate biosynthesis I in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C converts GTP to (7,8-dihydropterin-6-yl)methyl diphosphate through FOL2-catalyzed GTP cyclohydrolase I activity, 7,8-dihydroneopterin triphosphate pyrophosphohydrolase, 7,8-dihydroneopterin 3'-phosphate dephosphorylation, and FOL1-catalyzed aldolase and diphosphokinase activities.
