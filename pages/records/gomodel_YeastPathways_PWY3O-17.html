thiamine biosynthesis

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY3O-17

Thiamine biosynthesis in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C builds the pyrimidine and thiazole branches, condenses 4-amino-2-methyl-5-diphosphonatooxymethylpyrimidine and 4-methyl-5-(2-phosphonatooxyethyl)thiazole to thiamine monophosphate, and dephosphorylates and diphosphorylates it to thiamine diphosphate.
