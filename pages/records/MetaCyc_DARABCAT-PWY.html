D-arabinose degradation I

MetaCyc:DARABCAT-PWY

D-arabinose degradation I isomerizes D-arabinose to D-ribulose, then phosphorylates D-ribulose with ATP to form D-ribulose 5-phosphate for entry into pentose phosphate metabolism.
