L-arginine degradation V (arginine deiminase pathway)

MetaCyc:ARGDEGRAD-PWY

L-arginine degradation through the arginine deiminase pathway converts L-arginine to L-citrulline, L-ornithine, ammonium, hydrogencarbonate, and ATP through arginine deiminase, catabolic ornithine carbamoyltransferase, and carbamate kinase.
