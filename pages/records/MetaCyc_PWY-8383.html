MetaCyc:PWY-8383

Complete nine-member native route from supplied 2,3-dihydroxybenzoate and L-lysine to both myxochelin A and B, scoped to native Sg a15 sequences and qualified recombinant experiments. DHB precursor pathway PWY-5901 is linked supply, not a nested member. The N6-monoacyl intermediate and N6-first order are the selected MetaCyc database convention: primary Figure 5C explicitly leaves the first amidated lysine nitrogen unresolved. This convention is retained on every affected edge and is not direct experimental or exclusive positional specificity. RXN-23314 carries native right-to-left physiological direction, reducing the free aldehyde to A. Native stereospecific A (CPD-25904) is not exactly CHEBI:31877, which omits its stereocenter; B is structurally identical to CHEBI:7061, whose inconsistent hydroxy/reduction definition is excluded. Whole carrier states are source-issued reactive protein classes, not free residues, invented whole-protein formulas or exact tagged constructs. E/F/G derive from Sg a15 DNA; their intein purification leaves extensions and processing. The later His6-MxcL preparation derives from the cited pCBP-MxcL plasmid, whose complete engineered sequence was not independently reconstructed. Direct 2001 A reconstitution and official 2008 supplementary A/B and aldehyde experiments have distinct scopes; the 2001 MxcL/B role was proposed. NADPH is selected by the native equations; experimentally slower NADH activity is retained as context. Heterologous MtaA coexpression supports carrier maturation, but does not identify a physiological Sg a15 PPTase. No fixed E:F:G:L assembly, isolated-enzyme magnesium requirement, compartment or regulatory edge is established. The three-ATP-per-product totals are derived net balances, not measured cellular yields. Current transcriptome inventory offers no exact organism dataset; it is not evidence of biological absence.
