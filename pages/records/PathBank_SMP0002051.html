L-Carnitine Degradation I

PathwayMech / Browse pathways / PathBank:SMP0002051

PathBank:SMP0002051

The four-reaction PathBank route represents anaerobic L-carnitine reduction to gamma-butyrobetaine through CoA thioesters, using characterized Escherichia coli K-12 Cai proteins. CaiC supplies the initial carnitinyl-CoA pool; CaiD dehydrates it, CaiA reduces crotonobetainyl-CoA, and the CaiB homodimer transfers CoA to another carnitine molecule, releasing gamma-butyrobetaine and regenerating carnitinyl-CoA. Thus ATP-dependent initiation is distinct from the recycling loop. Rhea charge forms and both generic electron-transfer flavoprotein redox states are retained; the exact physiological donor complex is unresolved. FAD bound to CaiA is a separate cofactor. Selected catabolic directions do not imply irreversibility of CaiD or CaiB. This enzyme mechanism does not establish growth or expression in every K-12 strain, and is distinct from aerobic carnitine assimilation. The older native map defines the route boundary; its erroneous or underspecified chemical xrefs are not adopted as exact identities.
