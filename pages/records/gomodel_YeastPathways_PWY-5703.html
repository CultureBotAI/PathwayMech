urea degradation I

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY-5703

Urea degradation I in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C uses Dur12p to carboxylate urea to urea-1-carboxylate and then hydrolyze urea-1-carboxylate to ammonium and carbon dioxide.
