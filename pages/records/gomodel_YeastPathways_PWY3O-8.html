xylose metabolism

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY3O-8

Xylose metabolism in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C uses GRE3 aldose reductase to reduce D-xylopyranose to xylitol with NADPH, and XYL2 xylitol dehydrogenase to oxidize xylitol to D-xylulose with NAD+ in the SGD GO-CAM xylose metabolism pathway.
