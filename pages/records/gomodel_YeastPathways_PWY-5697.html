allantoin degradation to ureidoglycolate I (urea producing)

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY-5697

Allantoin degradation to ureidoglycolate I in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C hydrolyzes (S)-(+)-allantoin to allantoate and then hydrolyzes allantoate to (-)-ureidoglycolate and urea.
