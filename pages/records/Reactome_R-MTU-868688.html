Mycobacterium tuberculosis trehalose biosynthesis

Reactome:R-MTU-868688

Mycobacterium tuberculosis trehalose biosynthesis forms alpha,alpha-trehalose through OtsAB trehalose-6-phosphate synthesis, TreS conversion of maltose, and TreYZ conversion of 1,4-alpha-glucan.
