heme b biosynthesis I (aerobic)

gomodel:YeastPathways_HEME-BIOSYNTHESIS-II

Aerobic heme b biosynthesis in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C decarboxylates uroporphyrinogen III to coproporphyrinogen III, oxidizes it through protoporphyrinogen to protoporphyrin, and inserts iron into protoporphyrin to produce ferroheme b.

Pathway type
cofactor biosynthesis
Organism scope
  • Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C NCBITaxon:559292
