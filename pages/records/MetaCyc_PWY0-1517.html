sedoheptulose bisphosphate bypass

PathwayMech / Browse pathways / MetaCyc:PWY0-1517

MetaCyc:PWY0-1517

This ATP-dependent two-event bypass converts sedoheptulose7-phosphate (S7P) through sedoheptulose1, 7-bisphosphate (SBP) to erythrose4-phosphate (E4P) and glycerone phosphate (DHAP). Exact Rhea chemistry is S7P + ATP -> SBP + ADP + H(+), followed by SBP -> E4P + DHAP. Native RXN0-6541 and SEDOBISALDOL-RXN map to directional RHEA31444 and cleavage30169; native 30170 is bidirectional and 30168 is condensation. The generic S7P class is distinct from alpha-pyranose CPD-19339/CHEBI133984. PfkA is a native homotetramer and FbaA a homodimer, not the mixed PfkA/PfkB assembly suggested by the frozen PathBank component list. Reference P0A796/b3916 and P0AB71/b2925 and MG1655 GeneIDs948412/947415 anchor identity; off-taxon GeneIDs93777982/93779073 belong to Shigella sonnei and are rejected. Database taxonomic range and reference identity do not establish unmodified wild-type flux in every strain. The Calvin-cycle and riboneogenesis condensation E4P+DHAP->SBP and subsequent SBP hydrolysis have different direction and energy chemistry. Classical PPP transaldolase uses S7P+GAP, while ADP-heptose formation isomerizes S7P. The ATP-dependent phosphorylation/cleavage pair is distinct from those routes and from the six-event PPi branch; shared metabolites and earlier research mentions do not merge the pathway definitions.

Native PfkA sequence-based NAD-kinase prediction, antisense/production engineering, altered carbon-catabolite repression and enzyme allostery studies are neighboring enzyme contexts, not additional bypass reactions. FbaA ts8/h8, RNA/engineered substrate variants and broader physiological annotations likewise do not demonstrate more steps in this pathway. Physical substrate channeling discussed speculatively in older work is not a demonstrated multienzyme complex. Older unavailable primary bodies remain explicitly abstract/native-annotation evidence.
