superpathway of thiamine diphosphate biosynthesis I

MetaCyc:THISYN-PWY

The complete native MetaCyc de novo route is represented for the Escherichia coli K-12 protein core: AIR-to-HMP-PP synthesis, cysteine-derived sulfur transfer and DXP/tyrosine-dependent thiazole synthesis, then ThiE coupling and ThiL phosphorylation to thiamine diphosphate. Its three children contain eleven native reactions; current Rhea net sulfur transfer replaces two native ThiI relay steps, yielding ten nonduplicated reactions. All five sulfur-carrier states are whole proteins. The net reductant AH2/A remains generic, and the graph does not assert a specific ferredoxin or an elementary ThiI/ThiF disulfide sequence. ThiI stimulation in reconstitution is preserved without universal indispensability. ThiGH is a heterodimeric unit isolated also as larger multimers; its native six-plus-six assembly is qualified by experiment. ThiC cluster/oligomer and ThiE magnesium assignments retain their inference grades; ThiD oligomer annotations differ between sources. Exact thiazole tautomer chemistry follows current curated annotation, with Bacillus product-structure studies kept distinct from E. coli assays. Dxs requires the pathway product as a cofactor. PurF/D/N-or-T/L/M steps in the initial PathBank lead are upstream of this native AIR boundary; they are not omitted branches of THISYN-PWY. Thiamine salvage, TenI/ThiO variants, tRNA thiolation, Fe-S assembly, downstream cofactor utilization and engineered bypasses are outside this record. This reaction-level graph does not claim every transient intermediate is resolved, uniform wild-type flux, or complete elementary enzymology.
