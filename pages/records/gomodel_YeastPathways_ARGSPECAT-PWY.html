spermine biosynthesis

gomodel:YeastPathways_ARGSPECAT-PWY

Spermine biosynthesis decarboxylates S-adenosyl-L-methionine through SPE2 and transfers its aminopropyl group to spermidine through SPE4 to form spermine in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C.
