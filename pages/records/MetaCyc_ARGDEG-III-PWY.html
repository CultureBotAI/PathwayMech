L-arginine degradation IV (arginine decarboxylase/agmatine deiminase pathway)

MetaCyc:ARGDEG-III-PWY

L-arginine degradation through arginine decarboxylase and the agmatine deiminase pathway converts L-arginine to agmatine, N-carbamoylputrescine, putrescine, ammonium, and carbon dioxide.

Pathway type
amino acid degradation
