L-cysteine biosynthesis III (from L-homocysteine)

L-cysteine biosynthesis III (from L-homocysteine) condenses L-homocysteine with L-serine to form L-cystathionine and then cleaves L-cystathionine to L-cysteine, 2-oxobutanoate, and ammonia in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C.
