pyridoxal 5'-phosphate salvage I

MetaCyc:PLPSAL-PWY

The pyridoxal 5'-phosphate salvage I pathway phosphorylates pyridoxal, pyridoxamine, and pyridoxine, then oxidizes pyridoxamine 5'-phosphate and pyridoxine 5'-phosphate to regenerate pyridoxal 5'-phosphate.
