2-O-alpha-mannosyl-D-glycerate degradation

PathwayMech / Browse pathways / MetaCyc:PWY0-1300

MetaCyc:PWY0-1300

The native two-reaction pathway hydrolyzes imported MG phosphate to anomer-unspecified D-mannopyranose 6-phosphate and (R)-glycerate, then phosphorylates glycerate to 2-phosphoglycerate. MngA uptake and the PEP–PtsI–HPr relay are linked entry/energy context, not extra core members. E. coli K-12 culture and genetic evidence supports the route; the MngB purified-substrate assay, product anomer and physiological metal remain unresolved. Regulatory expression effects, constructs, source conflicts and opaque downstream interfaces retain their evidence limits. MG biosynthesis and unphosphorylated thermophile MG hydrolysis are excluded.
