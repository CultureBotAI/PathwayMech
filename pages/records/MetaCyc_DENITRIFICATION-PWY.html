nitrate reduction I (denitrification)

PathwayMech / Browse pathways / MetaCyc:DENITRIFICATION-PWY

MetaCyc:DENITRIFICATION-PWY

Complete native nitrate-to-dinitrogen route with five listed objects: vectorial Nar nitrate reduction, nitrate/nitrite exchange, c551-dependent nitrite reduction, cytochrome-c-dependent NO reduction and N2O reduction. PAO1 experiments support the respiratory system; strict NarK2 antiport versus uniport remains unresolved, so native antiport is explicitly a database convention and has no assigned PAO1 catalyst. Whole carrier classes and side-specific proton coefficients are retained without choosing a quinone species or a unique NosZ donor. Functional gene-product edges do not imply isolated subunit catalysis. Other-species native complex IDs are not transferred; NirS/NosZ dimer and Nar/Nor assembly annotations retain source-specific evidence tiers. Current NorB proton-pump annotation conflicts with cNOR structural evidence and is excluded; RCSB 3O0R has source-PAO1 protein sequences differing from current entries. Inferred cofactors retain their evidence tiers; ligand-state IDs do not fix catalytic redox state. Native background nitrite-to-ammonium prose, Nap compensation, qNOR variants, assimilatory reduction, electron-carrier regeneration and ATP synthesis are outside this selected route.
