L-phenylalanine biosynthesis I

MetaCyc:PHESYN

L-phenylalanine biosynthesis I converts chorismate to prephenate, dehydrates and decarboxylates prephenate to 3-phenylpyruvate, and transaminates 3-phenylpyruvate with L-glutamate to form L-phenylalanine.

Pathway type
amino acid biosynthesis
