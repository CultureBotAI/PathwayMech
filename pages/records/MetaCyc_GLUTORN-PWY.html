L-ornithine biosynthesis I

MetaCyc:GLUTORN-PWY

L-ornithine biosynthesis I converts L-glutamate to L-ornithine through N-acetyl-L-glutamate, N-acetyl-L-glutamyl 5-phosphate, N-acetyl-L-glutamate 5-semialdehyde, and N(2)-acetyl-L-ornithine.
