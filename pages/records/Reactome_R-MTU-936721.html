Cysteine synthesis from O-acetylserine

Reactome:R-MTU-936721

The Mycobacterium tuberculosis O-acetylserine route joins CysH/Sir sulfate reduction with CysE serine acetylation and CysK1-dependent cysteine synthesis. The outdated CysK2 O-acetylserine reaction is excluded: CysK2 uses a distinct O-phosphoserine route. Native thioredoxin and ferredoxin states and PLP-bearing CysK1 are retained. Reactome chemical forms include sulfurous acid, sulfide(2-) and acetic acid; this graph does not claim physiological protonation or complete stoichiometric balance.
