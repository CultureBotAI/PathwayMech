adenosylcobalamin biosynthesis from adenosylcobinamide-GDP I

PathwayMech / Browse pathways / MetaCyc:PWY-5509

MetaCyc:PWY-5509

This three-reaction nucleotide-loop assembly pathway starts with supplied adenosylcobinamide-GDP and 5,6-dimethylbenzimidazole. CobT transfers phosphoribose from nicotinate ribonucleotide, CobS joins the resulting alpha-ribazole phosphate to the activated corrinoid, and CobC removes the terminal phosphate. The graph represents reviewed Escherichia coli K-12 assignments with explicit evidence limits: CobT homodimer and CobS function/magnesium annotations include similarity evidence, and the current E. coli CobC annotation is inferred from Salmonella CobC. K-12 growth genetics supports salvage; the 2021 EcCobS assays used a BL21-derived construct and heterologous accessory enzymes. A 2023 engineered cell-free B12 production system separately used MG1655 CobT, CobS and CobC, including a soluble SIMPLEx-CobS fusion and engineered complementation. This strengthens MG1655 functional support without establishing isolated CobC kinetics, native quaternary structures or terminal-step physiology. Chemical naming inconsistencies in that supplement are preserved as source conflicts; the graph uses independently balanced Rhea chemistry. Terminal-step physiological timing was reassessed in Salmonella, not directly in K-12. No universal enzyme essentiality, exclusive route or physiological flux is implied. The activated corrinoid input separates this exact pathway from upstream cobinamide uptake, reduction, adenosylation and GDP activation; this record does not cover the full salvage superpathway or de novo corrin-ring synthesis. The alternative unphosphorylated-ribazole route and cobamide remodeling are excluded.
