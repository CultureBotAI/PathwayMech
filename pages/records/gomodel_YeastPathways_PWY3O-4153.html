phenylalanine biosynthesis

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY3O-4153

Phenylalanine biosynthesis in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C uses chorismate mutase, prephenate dehydratase, and aromatic-amino-acid transaminase activities to convert chorismate through prephenate and 3-phenylpyruvate to L-phenylalanine.
