L-arginine biosynthesis I (via L-ornithine)

MetaCyc:ARGSYN-PWY

L-arginine biosynthesis I acetylates L-glutamate, reduces the phosphorylated acetylglutamate through N-acetyl-L-glutamate 5-semialdehyde, transaminates it to N(2)-acetyl-L-ornithine, deacetylates L-ornithine, and converts L-ornithine through L-citrulline and argininosuccinate to L-arginine.

amino acid biosynthesis
