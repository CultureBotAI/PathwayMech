glycine cleavage

gomodel:YeastPathways_GLYCLEAV-PWY

Glycine cleavage in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C uses GCV2 to decarboxylate glycine with lipoylprotein, GCV1 to transfer the aminomethyl group to tetrahydrofolate, and LPD1 to reoxidize dihydrolipoylprotein with NAD.
