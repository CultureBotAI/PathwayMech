NAD salvage pathway IV (from nicotinamide riboside)

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY3O-4106

NAD salvage pathway IV from nicotinamide riboside in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C uses nicotinamide riboside kinase and nicotinamide-nucleotide adenylyltransferase activities to convert N-ribosylnicotinamide through NMN to NAD.
