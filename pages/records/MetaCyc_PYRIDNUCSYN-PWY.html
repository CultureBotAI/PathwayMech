NAD de novo biosynthesis I (from aspartate)

PathwayMech / Browse pathways / MetaCyc:PYRIDNUCSYN-PWY

MetaCyc:PYRIDNUCSYN-PWY

NAD de novo biosynthesis I oxidizes L-aspartate to iminosuccinate, condenses iminosuccinate with dihydroxyacetone phosphate to form quinolinate, converts quinolinate through nicotinate beta-D-ribonucleotide to deamido-NAD(+), and amidates deamido-NAD(+) through either an ammonia-dependent or glutamine-dependent NAD(+) synthase.

cofactor biosynthesis
