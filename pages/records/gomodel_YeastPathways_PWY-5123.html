trans, trans-farnesyl diphosphate biosynthesis

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY-5123

trans, trans-farnesyl diphosphate biosynthesis in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C isomerizes isopentenyl diphosphate to dimethylallyl diphosphate, condenses dimethylallyl diphosphate with isopentenyl diphosphate to geranyl diphosphate, and condenses geranyl diphosphate with isopentenyl diphosphate to (2E,6E)-farnesyl diphosphate.

Pathway type
lipid biosynthesis
