periplasmic NAD degradation

gomodel:YeastPathways_PERIPLASMA-NAD-DEGRADATION

Periplasmic NAD degradation in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C hydrolyzes NAD to AMP and NMN, then dephosphorylates AMP to adenosine and hydrogenphosphate.
