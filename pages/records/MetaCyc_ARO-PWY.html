chorismate biosynthesis I

MetaCyc:ARO-PWY

Chorismate biosynthesis I converts D-erythrose 4-phosphate and phosphoenolpyruvate to chorismate through 7-phospho-2-dehydro-3-deoxy-D-arabino-heptonate, 3-dehydroquinate, 3-dehydroshikimate, shikimate, 3-phosphoshikimate, and 5-O-(1-carboxyvinyl)-3-phosphoshikimate.
