glycerol biosynthesis

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY3O-48

Glycerol biosynthesis in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C uses GPD1 and GPD2 glycerol-3-phosphate dehydrogenase activities with GPP1 and GPP2 glycerol-1-phosphatase activities to convert glycerone phosphate to glycerol via sn-glycerol 3-phosphate.
