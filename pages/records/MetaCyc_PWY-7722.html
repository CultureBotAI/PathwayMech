sulfoquinovose degradation II

PathwayMech / Browse pathways / MetaCyc:PWY-7722

MetaCyc:PWY-7722

The complete five-transformation sulfo-Entner-Doudoroff route from free sulfoquinovose to pyruvate and sulfolactate in Pseudomonas putida strain SQ1 (DSM 100120). Six exact Rhea activities represent the five native members because the final SLA oxidation has alternative NAD- and NADP-dependent forms. All five SQ1 enzymes were reconstituted in vitro and induced during aerobic SQ growth. Current native reaction structures and reviewed Rhea/UniProt assignments retain S-SLA/S-SL; older native protein prose linking R-SLA is superseded, without recasting the SQ1 HPLC-MS assay as an enantiomer-resolved experiment. SQDH has direct tetramer evidence; the other functional protein/activity relations do not assert monomer sufficiency. Metal annotations preserve inference levels, and the aldolase genomic-start difference is explicit. The endpoint retains organic sulfur. SQ-glycoside hydrolysis, mutarotation, unidentified transport/regulatory mechanisms, downstream pyruvate metabolism and partner-organism sulfolactate mineralization are outside this exact native boundary. KT2440 expression datasets do not establish SQ1 applicability.
