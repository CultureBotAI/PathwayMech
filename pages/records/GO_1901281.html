fructoselysine catabolic process

PathwayMech / Browse pathways / GO:1901281

GO:1901281

The two-step fructoselysine catabolic route represented by the reviewed Escherichia coli K-12 proteins FrlD and FrlB converts fructoselysine to D-glucose 6-phosphate and L-lysine. FrlD uses ATP to form fructoselysine 6-phosphate; FrlB cleaves this intermediate with water. The graph selects the catabolic direction of the experimentally reversible FrlB reaction. Its scope is the core chemical conversion, with transport, psicoselysine epimerization, transcriptional regulation and alternative bacterial routes documented separately in the curation notes.
