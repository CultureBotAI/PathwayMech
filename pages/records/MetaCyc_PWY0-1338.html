polymyxin resistance

MetaCyc:PWY0-1338

The complete six-reaction native L-Ara4N-modified lipid A biosynthetic route, represented for Escherichia coli K-12. Starting at UDP-glucuronate, ArnA and ArnB form UDP-aminoarabinose; ArnA formylation, ArnC carrier transfer and ArnD deformylation supply the undecaprenyl-phosphate-linked donor used by ArnT to modify Kdo2-lipid A. The terminal source equation summarizes addition of two aminoarabinose residues, not a single elementary transfer or every cellular lipid species. ArnE and ArnF have jointly required transport-associated roles; no fixed heterodimer or energy-coupling mechanism is asserted. The older native hypothetical ArnD step is supported by later E. coli genetics and separately scoped purified Salmonella enzyme experiments. ArnA hexamer evidence is preparation-specific, and ArnB dimerization remains a similarity annotation. Resistance is conditional on pathway expression and the studied mutant or induction context; the record does not claim constitutive resistance of all K-12 cells. Upstream UDP-glucuronate and Kdo2-lipid A biosynthesis are outside this native boundary.
