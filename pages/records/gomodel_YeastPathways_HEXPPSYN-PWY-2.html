hexaprenyl diphosphate biosynthesis

gomodel:YeastPathways_HEXPPSYN-PWY-2

Hexaprenyl diphosphate biosynthesis in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C condenses dimethylallyl diphosphate with isopentenyl diphosphate through geranyl, farnesyl, geranylgeranyl, and pentaprenyl diphosphate intermediates to form all-trans-hexaprenyl diphosphate for ubiquinone biosynthesis.
