nicotine degradation II (pyrrolidine pathway)

PathwayMech / Browse pathways / MetaCyc:PWY-6993

MetaCyc:PWY-6993

The complete eleven-step native pyrrolidine pathway converts (S)-nicotine to fumarate and succinate, with methylamine, ammonium and formate coproducts. The experimentally characterized enzymes are scoped to Pseudomonas putida S16. Current NicA2 and Pnao reactions use two oxidized CycN carrier molecules per turnover; their older oxygen-based representations are superseded for this physiological role. N-methylmyosmine hydration is spontaneous. The SpmABC step retains a generic electron acceptor: its physiological acceptor is not assigned. The native NADP-dependent aldehyde and succinate-semialdehyde oxidation steps remain in the complete route, but an exact experimentally supported S16 catalyst is not asserted here for either step. NicA2 assembly reports differ, and functional protein edges do not assert an isolated monomer as the catalytic unit. HspB and maleate isomerase retain verified native dimers. CycN reoxidation, transport and metabolism beyond fumarate, succinate and the released coproducts are outside this record.
