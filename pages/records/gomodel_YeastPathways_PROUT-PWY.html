L-proline degradation

gomodel:YeastPathways_PROUT-PWY

L-proline degradation in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C oxidizes L-proline to (S)-1-pyrroline-5-carboxylate, hydrates that intermediate to L-glutamic 5-semialdehyde, and oxidizes it to L-glutamate.
