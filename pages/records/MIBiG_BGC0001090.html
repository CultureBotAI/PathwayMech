MIBiG:BGC0001090

Bacillomycin D biosynthetic gene cluster of Bacillus velezensis FZB42, called Bacillus amyloliquefaciens FZB42 in the original study. This partial graph preserves four source-annotated biosynthetic components, not a complete reaction sequence or independent enzyme sufficiency. MIBiG reports active status, questionable quality and complete locus coverage. Its bmyD name for ABS74182.1 differs from the current GenBank fabD annotation. MIBiG bounds refer to CP000560.1; current protein coded_by annotations refer to CP000560.2 and are not used to remap those bounds. Module substrates are inferred from structure and sequence and are not promoted to measured reactions.
