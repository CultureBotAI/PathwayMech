N-acetylglucosamine degradation I

MetaCyc:GLUAMCAT-PWY

N-acetylglucosamine degradation I deacetylates N-acetyl-D-glucosamine 6-phosphate to D-glucosamine 6-phosphate and acetate, then deaminates alpha-D-glucosamine 6-phosphate to beta-D-fructose 6-phosphate and ammonium.
