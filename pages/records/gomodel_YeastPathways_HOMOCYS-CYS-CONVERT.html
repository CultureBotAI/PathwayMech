homocysteine and cysteine interconversion

gomodel:YeastPathways_HOMOCYS-CYS-CONVERT

Homocysteine and cysteine interconversion in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C combines cystathionine beta-synthase and gamma-lyase activities that convert L-homocysteine to L-cysteine via L-cystathionine with cystathionine gamma-synthase and beta-lyase activities that convert L-cysteine toward L-homocysteine through the same intermediate.
