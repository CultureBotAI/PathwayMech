MetaCyc:P101-PWY

Ectoine biosynthesis converts L-aspartate to L-ectoine through 4-phospho-L-aspartate, L-aspartate 4-semialdehyde, L-2,4-diaminobutanoate, and (2S)-4-acetamido-2-aminobutanoate.
