gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY3O-1801

Palmitoleate biosynthesis in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C uses Ole1p to desaturate palmitoyl-CoA to palmitoleoyl-CoA and Tes1p to hydrolyze palmitoleoyl-CoA to palmitoleate and coenzyme A.
