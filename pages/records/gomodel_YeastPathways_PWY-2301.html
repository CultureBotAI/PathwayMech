myo-inositol biosynthesis

gomodel:YeastPathways_PWY-2301

myo-Inositol biosynthesis in Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C converts D-glucopyranose 6-phosphate to 1D-myo-inositol 3-phosphate and hydrolyzes 1D-myo-inositol 3-phosphate to myo-inositol through INM1 or INM2 inositol monophosphate 1-phosphatase activities.
