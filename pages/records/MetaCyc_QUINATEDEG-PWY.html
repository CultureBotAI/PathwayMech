quinate degradation I

MetaCyc:QUINATEDEG-PWY

The three-step MetaCyc 27.0 quinate degradation I definition converts L-quinate through 3-dehydroquinate and 3-dehydroshikimate to protocatechuate. The initial oxidation retains the source-defined quinone/quinol classes; PQQ is an enzyme cofactor, not a net reaction substrate. The shared dehydroquinate dehydration is reversible although the pathway selects catabolic forward flux. Enzyme evidence is qualified by organism, preparation and construct; the record does not assert a purified three-enzyme reconstitution in one strain.
