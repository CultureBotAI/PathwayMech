2-phenylethanol biosynthesis

WikiPathways:WP5587

2-phenylethanol biosynthesis in Saccharomyces cerevisiae places glucose, phosphoenolpyruvate, erythrose 4-phosphate, 3-deoxy-D-arabino-heptulosonic acid 7-phosphate, shikimate, chorismate, phenylalanine, and 2-phenylethanol in shikimate- and Ehrlich-route reactions in the WP5587 GPML diagram. The graph distinguishes reversible phenylalanine transamination, DAHP condensation and the connected phenylpyruvate-to-phenylacetaldehyde Ehrlich route; collapsed upstream branches represent multiple enzymatic steps.
